# Privacy Policy

> **Draft for review by counsel before launch.** This document is a draft. It is not in effect and no one may rely on it. It takes effect only when GHXSTSHIP Industries LLC publishes it at /legal, and its effective date is that date of publication.

This Privacy Policy explains how GHXSTSHIP Industries LLC ("GHXSTSHIP", "we", "us" or "our") handles personal data in connection with XOS 4.0: Atlas, Gateway, Compass, the public API, our websites and our support channels (together, the "Service").

## 1. Two Roles: Controller and Processor

We handle personal data in two different roles, and which role applies decides whom you should contact.

**When an organization uses XOS (processor role).** Organizations ("Customers") use XOS to run productions. They put information into XOS about their members, crew, staff, vendors, contractors, clients, artists, sponsors, guests and incidents. For that information the Customer is the controller and GHXSTSHIP is its processor or service provider. We process it only on the Customer's instructions under our Data Processing Agreement. If you are a crew member, vendor, worker or guest of an organization that uses XOS, that organization's privacy notice governs how your information is used, and requests about it should go to that organization. We will pass on any request we receive about Customer data to the right Customer and help them answer it.

**When we run our own business (controller role).** GHXSTSHIP is the controller for:

- account and sign-in data for every XOS user, to the extent needed to secure the account across organizations
- the public marketplace profile a person chooses to publish in Gateway
- billing contacts and subscription records of Customers
- visitors to our websites and documentation
- support requests, community forum posts and feedback
- security logs and fraud prevention
- marketing communications to business contacts

This Policy describes the processing we do as controller. Section 12 describes what we do as processor.

## 5. AI Features

AI features are off until an organization Admin turns them on. When they are on, the Service sends the minimum data needed for the request to Anthropic's Claude models through the Anthropic API. Medical records and fields classified Restricted are never sent. Customer data is never used to train models. Prompts and outputs are logged with personal data redacted. The AI Features Notice describes each feature.

## 6. How We Share Personal Data

We share personal data only:

- with the subprocessors and service providers listed on our Subprocessor List, under contracts that limit their use of it
- with an organization you join or apply to, limited to the profile fields you choose to share with it
- with Stripe for payments, and with other payment or tax authorities where the law requires
- with professional advisers under duties of confidentiality
- with authorities where the law compels it, after reviewing the request and giving notice where we can
- with a successor in a merger or acquisition, under the protections of this Policy

**We do not sell personal data and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.** We have not done so in the past 12 months.

## 7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

- access your personal data and receive a copy
- correct inaccurate data
- delete your data
- restrict or object to processing, including processing based on legitimate interests and direct marketing
- receive your data in a portable format
- withdraw consent at any time, without affecting earlier processing
- opt out of sale, sharing, targeted advertising and profiling with legal or similarly significant effects (we do none of these)
- limit the use of sensitive personal information (we use it only for purposes the law permits without that right)
- appeal our decision on your request
- not be discriminated against for using your rights

**How to make a request.** Use Settings, Data and Privacy in the Service, or write to the privacy mailbox on the Contact Page. We verify your identity before acting, using your signed-in session or by confirming control of your email address. An authorized agent may make a request for you with proof of authority. We answer within 30 days, which meets or beats the deadline of every regime in Section 9. Where a law allows an extension for complex or numerous requests, we tell you within the first 30 days and explain why. If we refuse a request, we tell you why and how to appeal. If your appeal is refused, you may contact your regulator.

**Universal opt-out signals.** We honor Global Privacy Control and other recognized universal opt-out signals as a valid opt-out request for the browser or device that sends them.

**Complaints.** You may complain to your data protection authority. We would welcome the chance to address your concern first.

- **Canada.** Our privacy officer under PIPEDA, and our person in charge of the protection of personal information under Quebec Law 25, is named on the Contact Page. Before transferring personal information outside Quebec we complete a privacy impact assessment.
- **Brazil, Australia and Japan.** You have the rights the LGPD, the Australian Privacy Principles and the APPI provide, through the workflow in Section 7. We document transfers under each law's requirements.
- **India.** We provide notices in plain language, keep consent records, and notify the Data Protection Board and affected people of breaches as the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 requires. Our grievance officer is named on the Contact Page.
- **Singapore, South Africa and South Korea.** You have the rights the PDPA, POPIA and PIPA provide, through the workflow in Section 7. We keep consent records, document transfers and notify breaches as each law requires.

## 10. Retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes in Section 4, then delete or anonymize it. Our Data Retention and Deletion Policy sets the periods. Key examples: the audit ledger is kept for 7 years with personal data in its text redacted after 90 days; billing records are kept as long as tax law requires; marketplace profiles are kept until you delete them or your account.

## 11. Security

We protect personal data with encryption in transit (TLS 1.2 or higher) and at rest (AES-256), field-level encryption for the most sensitive identifiers, role-based access enforced in the database, multi-factor authentication, logging and monitoring. Our Security Overview describes these measures. No system is perfectly secure; we will notify you and regulators of a breach as the law requires.

## 12. Processing for Customers

When we act as processor we:

- process data only on the Customer's documented instructions
- bind our personnel to confidentiality
- use only the subprocessors on our Subprocessor List, with notice of changes
- help the Customer answer data subject requests through the Service's DSAR workflow
- notify the Customer of a personal data breach without undue delay
- delete or return data at the end of the subscription as our Terms of Service describe

## 13. Children

The Service is not directed to children. Accounts require age 16 or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 and, consistent with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act, we delete any such information we learn we have collected. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact the privacy mailbox on the Contact Page.

## 14. Cookies

Our Cookie Policy explains the cookies we use and how to choose. Non-essential cookies are blocked until you consent, and your choice is stored on our servers so it follows your account.

## 15. Changes

We publish every version of this Policy at /legal with a summary of changes. For a material change we notify you in the Service or by email before it takes effect.
