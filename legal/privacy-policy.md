# Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how GHXSTSHIP Industries LLC ("GHXSTSHIP", "we", "us" or "our"), a Florida company based in Miami, handles personal data in connection with XOS: Atlas, Gateway, Compass, the public API, our websites and our support channels (together, the "Service").

Last updated: November 2026.

## 1. Two Roles: Controller and Processor

We handle personal data in two different roles, and which role applies decides whom you should contact.

**When an organization uses XOS (processor role).** Organizations ("Customers") use XOS to run productions. They, and the people they invite, put information into XOS about these people:

- their owners, administrators, members and staff
- crew, workers and contractors, including people engaged through an agency
- vendors, sole traders and their contacts
- artists, their representatives and their travel parties
- clients, sponsors and other business contacts
- applicants for an opportunity
- guests and attendees, and people who pass through credential or access scans
- people named in incidents, injury records or medical encounters
- emergency contacts, referees and other people someone else names
- anyone who reaches a page through a link or code the Customer issues, with no XOS account, such as a signing, advance or shared page
- people who write to a project inbox or a reply-by-email address, where the Customer uses one

For that information the Customer is the controller, or a "business" under California law, and GHXSTSHIP is its processor or service provider. We process it only on the Customer's instructions under our Data Processing Agreement. If you are one of these people, that organization's privacy notice governs how your information is used, the organization is responsible for giving you that notice, and requests about the information should go to that organization. Some pages reached by a link, such as a signing, advance or guest page, do not yet link to this Policy or name the organization, so ask the organization that sent you the link. We run those pages, and we are controller of the consent records and security logs they create. If a request about Customer data reaches us, we pass it to the right Customer and help them answer it. Section 12 describes this role.

**When we run our own business (controller role).** GHXSTSHIP is the controller for:

- your account and sign-in data, because one account works across every organization in Atlas, Gateway and Compass
- the public marketplace profile a person chooses to publish in Gateway
- the Work Details and supplier diversity certifications a person keeps in their own account, until that person shares them (Section 3.2)
- billing contacts and subscription records of Customers, and the billing evidence Section 10 describes
- the country an organization's creator states, which decides whether XOS is offered to it
- the display of your ratings on your profile when you opt in, and disputes about ratings of you
- legal and security requests we handle, and the emails we send about moderation results
- visitors to our websites and documentation
- support requests and feedback
- security logs and fraud prevention
- our communications to business contacts

Everything about an engagement with an organization is processor data, including what a person shares with that organization from their own account, apart from the rules for ratings below. Sections 3.1 and 4 to 11 describe the processing we do as controller. Sections 3.2 and 3.3 say which role applies to each kind of data.

**Ratings.** An organization gives a rating as part of an engagement, and that record is the organization's. An organization's rating of you is shown beyond that organization only if you opt in to showing your ratings on your profile, and GHXSTSHIP is the controller of that display. Even then, nobody with hiring, booking or staffing access in XOS sees it: anyone who holds such a capability in an organization or on a project, anyone who runs an external account, a live Advancing Lead, an artist's representative and anyone engaged as an Artist. A rating of a company or vendor, and a party's rating of an organization, is shown to signed-in users under a rule we set (Section 6). You can dispute a rating of you in Gateway, Profile, Dispute this rating, on the grounds the Worker Terms name in Section 8, and you can also write to support@ghxstship.pro with "Privacy" in the subject line. When the platform team upholds a dispute, the rating is removed from every product read. It stays as evidence in the rating organization's audit ledger, for those with audit access there, and with the platform operators who decide disputes, and it does not cross to another organization.

## 5. AI Features

XOS's AI features, called Aurora, are off until an organization Admin turns them on, and they need the person's confirmation that they are 18 or older. Compass asks each person before an AI feature first sends anything of theirs, and a decline turns AI features off for that person across XOS. That choice covers the features the person uses. It does not stop others in the organization from using AI features on records they can see, including records about the person. When AI features are on and a person uses one, the Service sends what that feature needs to Anthropic's Claude models through the Anthropic API. Anthropic is a third party. These are the limits:

- Stored fields classified Restricted, medical encounter records, Work Details other than loyalty program numbers, emergency contacts, badge photos, supplier diversity certifications, guest needs, sensitive data consent records and project travel, uniform and vehicle records are removed from the record data sent to the model.
- Those limits apply to stored fields. They do not reach what a person types, free text inside other records, or a document a person chooses to have read. Document extraction sends the whole file, with whatever it prints. If an invoice shows bank details or a form shows a tax identifier, they reach the model.
- Loyalty program numbers are not yet on that list. The assistant can read your own numbers through your own access and send them to the model when you use it.
- We do not use Customer data to train models. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, as published in November 2026, Anthropic may not train on it unless the account holder opts in, and this commitment rules that out for the account behind XOS.
