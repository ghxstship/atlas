# Subprocessor List

This list names the third parties that GHXSTSHIP Industries LLC ("GHXSTSHIP") engages to process Customer Personal Data in providing XOS. It is Annex III of our Data Processing Agreement. It is drawn from the technology stack recorded in our architecture decisions and from the code, and it changes only through the notice process below.

## 1. Change Notifications

- We give at least 30 days' notice before a new subprocessor processes Customer Personal Data.
- Anyone can subscribe to change notifications with the subscription form on this page. Each Customer's Owner and Admins are subscribed by default and can unsubscribe in Settings, Notifications.
- Each notice names the subprocessor, what it will process, where, and why, and links to the version history of this page.
- A Customer may object under Section 7.3 of the Data Processing Agreement.
- This notice covers the subprocessors we engage directly. Each of them may change its own vendors under its own published terms, as Section 7.5 of the Data Processing Agreement explains.

## 4. Services That Are Not Subprocessors

- **Connected file storage.** The Google Drive and Sheets connection is not yet open to customers, because Google's review of GHXSTSHIP's OAuth app is not complete. Once it is, an Admin can connect Google Drive and Sheets through GHXSTSHIP's own OAuth app, which asks for read access to that person's files (`drive.readonly`). OneDrive and SharePoint are not offered. XOS keeps the connection's tokens in Vault, lists and reads files for the person using the connection, links files in place from a folder the Admin chooses, and can copy spreadsheet values into private import storage. Data comes from the provider into XOS, where it becomes Customer Data. The account itself stays under the person's or the Customer's agreement with Google or Microsoft. Our Privacy Policy describes our handling of Google user data.
- **Customer-directed integrations.** When a Customer connects an integration, data flows to that provider on the Customer's instruction and under the Customer's own agreement with it. Those providers are the Customer's vendors, not GHXSTSHIP's subprocessors. The integrations are Xero, QuickBooks Online, NetSuite and Ramp; Google Calendar and Microsoft 365; Dropbox; Slack and Microsoft Teams; Gusto, ADP and Rippling; UPS, FedEx and DHL; ticketing systems by file or API; Zapier and Make; delivery to the Customer's own S3-compatible storage; and webhooks the Customer sets up.
- **AI tools a Customer connects.** Where hosted connections are offered, a person can connect an outside AI tool, such as Claude or ChatGPT, through OAuth after an Admin registers it. That tool can then read what that person's token allows, on their own and the Customer's direction and under the Customer's agreement with the tool's provider, including a provider other than Anthropic. Restricted groups are refused, and each write is a proposal the person confirms in XOS.
- **Identity providers.** Google and Apple sign-in, and a Customer's own SAML or OIDC identity provider, authenticate users under their own terms. They receive only what the sign-in protocol requires. When a person who signed in with Apple deletes their account, XOS asks Apple to revoke its token.
- **Apple Wallet passes.** An Apple Wallet pass is a file delivered to the person's device, who adds it to Apple Wallet by their own choice. Google Wallet passes are in Section 3.
- **Browser push services.** When a person allows web push, their browser vendor's push service receives an endpoint and an encrypted payload it cannot read.
- **PowerSync.** Not in use at launch. Compass downloads its offline copy from Supabase and sends queued changes back the same way, inside the infrastructure above. If GHXSTSHIP later turns on PowerSync, a self-hosted instance runs inside that infrastructure, and a hosted PowerSync service would be added to this list with 30 days' notice.
- **Services not in use.** Stripe Connect is not offered: XOS records payments and never holds funds, and payout bank details a vendor gives XOS are stored encrypted in XOS, not sent to Stripe. Stripe Identity and OpenCorporates are not in use. Any of these would be added to this list with 30 days' notice before it processes Customer Personal Data.
- **Maps.** No third-party map service receives location data. Where base map tiles are shown, they are Protomaps PMTiles files served from our own Supabase Storage.
- **Exchange rates and virus signatures.** We download European Central Bank reference rates and ClamAV signature updates. No personal data is sent.
