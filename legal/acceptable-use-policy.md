# Acceptable Use Policy

This Acceptable Use Policy applies to everyone who uses XOS (the "Service"): Customers, their Members, External Parties using Gateway, anyone using Compass, the public API, the SDKs, the CLI or the MCP servers, and anyone who reaches a page through a link or access code an organization issues. It forms part of the Terms of Service, the Worker Terms and the Mobile End User License Agreement. An organization's internal Members and people who use a link accept nothing from GHXSTSHIP when they are added or follow the link, so the organization that added them or issued the link passes these rules on to them and answers for their use of the Service. GHXSTSHIP Industries LLC ("GHXSTSHIP") may update this policy. No one is asked to accept an update. It applies to everyone from the date it states, after at least 30 days' notice in the Service if it is material, or sooner where the law or a security or abuse risk requires it. Anyone who does not agree to an update should stop using the Service, and a Customer can use the exit in Section 15 of the Terms of Service.

## 1. Prohibited Content

You must not use the Service to store, publish or send content that:

- is unlawful, defamatory, harassing, threatening or hateful toward people based on a protected characteristic
- infringes intellectual property, privacy or publicity rights, including photos, video or likenesses of people you have no right to use
- sexually exploits or endangers minors, which we report to the authorities
- shows a person in an intimate image without their consent, including an image made or altered by AI
- contains malware, or links to malware or phishing
- impersonates a person or organization, or misrepresents your affiliation
- promotes violence or terrorism
- is a scam, or a fake opportunity, bid, profile, certification or document

## 2. Prohibited Conduct

You must not:

- access, probe or test another tenant's data, or try to bypass row-level security, field masking, sealed bids, rate limits or any other control, except under our Responsible Disclosure Policy
- share accounts, credentials, API keys, token links or access codes with people not authorized to use them
- scrape the marketplace, harvest profiles or contact details, or use automated means to create accounts or applications
- overload the Service, interfere with other tenants, or exceed rate limits deliberately
- reverse engineer the hosted Service beyond what the open source licenses allow for the published code
- use the Service to send unsolicited email or SMS, or to message people without the consent the law requires, including under CAN-SPAM, CASL and the TCPA, or put promotions in text notifications
- use the Service in violation of export control or sanctions laws
- run facial recognition or any other biometric matching on photos in or from the Service, or add it through the API or an integration. XOS does not do this, and you must not make that untrue
- scan or swipe government-issued identity documents, such as driver licenses, with the Compass scanner, or upload copies of them as general files
- use location, scan or time records to track a person outside what their work requires and the law allows

## 3. Marketplace and Workforce Rules

When publishing opportunities, applying, bidding, rating or engaging through Gateway and Compass, you must:

- publish only real opportunities with honest descriptions, and include a pay range wherever the law requires one, whether or not the Service's jurisdiction rules ask for it
- if you hide the client or event on a listing until booking, still describe the work honestly
- not ask applicants about protected characteristics such as race, color, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin, age, disability or genetic information, and not collect voluntary equal employment answers through application questions, which selectors can read. Run that reporting outside the Service. A minimum age set for an opportunity is the one exception, and only where it is lawful and tied to the work
- run background checks only through a consumer reporting agency and only with the candidate's disclosure and authorization, following pre-adverse and adverse action steps and applicable fair-chance rules
- make your own worker classification decisions and keep a record of their basis. XOS never makes them for you
- not offer, request or accept payment or other incentives for ratings, not post ratings for engagements that did not happen, and not ask anyone to remove or suppress a truthful rating
- not use ratings as a background check or screening report. A person with hiring, booking or staffing access in XOS, whether in an organization or on an external account, does not see another organization's rating of a person, and a rated person can dispute a rating only on the grounds Section 8 of the Worker Terms names
- not use Do Not Engage flags, ratings, schedule orderings or internal notes to discriminate unlawfully
- respect child labor laws for any age requirement you set. Every account holder is 18 or older, so these duties apply to records you keep about minors, such as a young performer in an artist's travel party: people under 18 may not do work the law treats as hazardous for them, which at events can include operating forklifts, lifts and hoists, and XOS does not check this for you
- not treat a certification shown in XOS as proof that a person is competent for a task. You remain responsible for your own training records and checks
- not try to see another bidder's bid or anything an organization has not shared with you

These rules do not stop anyone from giving an honest review or rating.

## 4. Data Handling Rules

- Enter Restricted data, such as tax identifiers, bank details, government identifiers, pay rates, allergies and medical notes, only in the fields designed for it, never in free text, comments or file names.
- Record medical encounter data only if you hold the Medical capability and have a lawful reason. Keep identifying medical detail out of the patient description.
- Do not upload special category data or criminal records data unless the law permits it and the Customer has a lawful basis. Do not put payment card numbers in the Service.
- Read dietary, allergy and accessibility information, and accommodation requests, only for the purpose they were shared for, such as catering or safety, and do not use them to decide whom to engage.
- Take care with records about people under 18, such as guests or a young performer in a travel party. They hold no account of their own. Get the consent or give the notice the law requires, and get a parent's or guardian's consent for a minor's likeness where the law asks for it.
- Collect dietary, allergy and accessibility needs only in the fields built for them, never in free text, custom fields, form questions, banquet event order notes or imports. If you enter a guest's needs or a rostered person's meal preference, you must have that person's authority, and the person must have had notice.
- Have the rights and give the notices the law requires for photos, video and recordings you upload.

## 5. API and Automation

- Respect rate limits and use the Idempotency-Key header on every POST.
- Store API keys and tokens securely, give them the narrowest capability scopes and an expiry, and rotate them if exposed.
- Applications and AI agents connected through a local MCP server act with the permissions of their API key. The hosted MCP server, personal access tokens and connected applications act with the permissions of the person who authorized them. The key holder or authorizing person is responsible for what they do, and some can write without the confirmation step Aurora uses.

## 6. Reporting

- **Marketplace listings:** use the Report action on the listing in Gateway, which needs you to be signed in. We decide these reports.
- **Posts, comments and chat messages:** use the Report action in Compass. These go to the organization's own moderators, who are notified at once and aim to decide within 24 hours. We do not see them. You can also block a person in Compass.
- **Anything else, or if you have no account:** write to support@ghxstship.pro with "Abuse Report" in the subject line. That includes a profile, any content you think is illegal, and a post, comment or chat message when the organization's moderators do not respond.
- **A good report** says what the content is and exactly where it is, why you think it is illegal or breaks this policy, your name and email (unless the report concerns child sexual abuse material, where you may leave them out), and that you are reporting in good faith.
- **Ratings of you:** dispute them in Gateway as Section 8 of the Worker Terms describes.
- **Other routes:** report security issues under the Responsible Disclosure Policy, and copyright infringement under the Copyright and DMCA Policy.
- **Emergencies:** XOS is not an emergency service. Call the emergency services, and tell the organization you are working with.

## 7. Enforcement

We may investigate suspected violations. Depending on severity, we may remove content, restrict a listing or feature, suspend an account, or terminate access. Sections 11.5, 11.9 and 11.10 of the Terms of Service and Section 11 of the Worker Terms give us those rights and say what happens next. Our decisions are made by people. We do not use automated means to detect or decide reported listings. We apply this policy with care and in proportion to the violation, with regard to the rights of everyone involved.

Where we restrict content or an account, we send a statement of reasons. It explains what we did and for how long, the facts, the law or rule relied on, whether automated means were used, and your options for redress. Those options are an appeal to us, any out-of-court dispute settlement the law gives you, and the courts. You may appeal within six months. A person looks at your appeal afresh, records the reasons and tells you the result. GHXSTSHIP is a small team, so that person may be the one who made the original decision, and if so we tell you. If we appoint a named outside reviewer, your appeal goes to them and we tell you who they are. Someone who reported a listing is told the outcome and can appeal a decision to take no action or to restore the listing, by writing to support@ghxstship.pro with "Appeal" in the subject line. We send these by email if our notifications would not reach you. If you have deleted your account we may have no way to reach you, and we record that.

A rating dispute is decided under Section 8 of the Worker Terms. Its statement of reasons gives the ground and the reasons for the decision. The six-month appeal above covers our decisions on content and accounts, not rating disputes. The out-of-court and court options remain.

A decision by an organization's moderators about its own posts, comments or chat is that organization's decision. The people involved receive the organization's statement of it, and GHXSTSHIP does not hear an appeal from it. Anyone can still write to support@ghxstship.pro with "Abuse Report" in the subject line about that content, as Section 6 says. A person reads what is sent, and anything we then do falls under this section.

We act immediately and without prior notice where needed to protect people, data or the Service, or where the law requires it. If we learn of something suggesting a crime that threatens someone's life or safety, we tell the authorities.

## 8. AI Features

AI features run on Anthropic's models. Anthropic's Usage Policy and Supported Regions Policy apply to you through us, and these rules are part of how we keep to them.

- **Keep a person accountable.** A recommendation from an AI feature about a specific person's hiring, screening, ranking, advancement, rejection or promotion, their shifts, hours or pay, or their performance or discipline, and any such recommendation in a legal, medical or financial matter, must be reviewed before it is used by a qualified person who can change it. Tell the person affected that AI was used. Using AI to draft, summarize or analyze inside your team is fine, as long as a person makes the decision and the output is not treated as the final recommendation.
- **Check the output.** AI output can be wrong. Do not rely on a factual statement in it without checking it independently.
- **Label it.** Do not present AI-generated content as human-made where the law requires a label.
- **Do not use AI features to generate content that breaks this policy.**
- **Do not use AI to track a person's location without their consent.**
- **Age.** AI features are only for people who have confirmed they are 18 or older. The Service refuses them to any person who has not confirmed it. That confirmation is the person's own statement, and XOS does not verify it. A service account is not a person and is not asked: the organization that created it answers for what its API keys do with AI features. Do not give AI features to anyone younger or use them to talk with minors.
- **Regions.** Do not use AI features from a place, or for an organization based or controlled in a place, that Anthropic's Supported Regions Policy does not list.
- **Competing models.** Do not use AI features or their output to build a competing AI product or to train another model, and do not reverse engineer the models or help anyone else do so.
- **No physical control.** Do not connect AI features or output to anything that moves or powers equipment, such as rigging, hoists, lifts, pyrotechnics or safety systems.
