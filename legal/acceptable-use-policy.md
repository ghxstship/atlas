# Acceptable Use Policy

> **Draft for review by counsel before launch.** This document is a draft. It is not in effect and no one may rely on it. It takes effect only when GHXSTSHIP Industries LLC publishes it at /legal, and its effective date is that date of publication.

This Acceptable Use Policy applies to everyone who uses XOS 4.0: Customers, their Members, External Parties using Gateway, and anyone using Compass, the public API, the SDKs, the CLI or the MCP server. It forms part of the Terms of Service, the Worker Terms and the Mobile End User License Agreement. GHXSTSHIP Industries LLC ("GHXSTSHIP") may update it under the notice rules in those documents.

## 1. Prohibited Content

You must not use the Service to store, publish or send content that:

- is unlawful, defamatory, harassing, threatening or hateful toward people based on a protected characteristic
- infringes intellectual property, privacy or publicity rights
- sexually exploits or endangers minors, which we report to the authorities
- contains malware, or links to malware or phishing
- impersonates a person or organization, or misrepresents your affiliation
- promotes violence or terrorism

## 2. Prohibited Conduct

You must not:

- access, probe or test another tenant's data, or try to bypass row-level security, field masking, sealed bids, rate limits or any other control, except under our Responsible Disclosure Policy
- share accounts, credentials, API keys, token links or access codes with people not authorized to use them
- scrape the marketplace, harvest profiles or contact details, or use automated means to create accounts or applications
- overload the Service, interfere with other tenants, or exceed rate limits deliberately
- reverse engineer the hosted Service beyond what the open source licenses allow for the published code
- use the Service to send unsolicited email or SMS, or to message people without the consent the law requires, including under CAN-SPAM, CASL and the TCPA
- use the Service in violation of export control or sanctions laws
- use AI features to generate content that breaks this policy, or present AI-generated content as human-made where the law requires a label

## 3. Marketplace and Workforce Rules

When publishing opportunities, applying, bidding, rating or engaging through Gateway and Compass, you must:

- publish only real opportunities with honest descriptions, and include a pay range wherever the Service's jurisdiction rules require one
- not ask applicants about protected characteristics such as race, color, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin, age, disability or genetic information. Voluntary equal employment questions, where an organization runs that reporting, are stored apart from applications and never shown to selectors
- run background checks only through the provider integration and only with the candidate's disclosure and authorization, following pre-adverse and adverse action steps and applicable fair-chance rules
- make your own worker classification decisions and record their basis. XOS never makes them for you
- not offer, request or accept payment or other incentives for ratings, not post ratings for engagements that did not happen, and not ask anyone to remove or suppress a truthful rating
- not use Do Not Engage flags, ratings or internal notes to discriminate unlawfully
- not set a minimum age below 16 for any opportunity, and respect child labor laws for any age requirement you set
- not try to see another bidder's bid or anything an organization has not shared with you

## 4. Data Handling Rules

- Enter Restricted data, such as tax identifiers, bank details, government identifiers and medical notes, only in the fields designed for it, never in free text, comments or file names.
- Record medical encounter data only if you hold the Medical capability and have a lawful reason.
- Do not upload special category data or criminal records data unless the law permits it and the Customer has a lawful basis.

## 5. API and Automation

- Respect rate limits and use the Idempotency-Key header on every POST.
- Store API keys securely, give them the narrowest capability scopes and an expiry, and rotate them if exposed.
- AI agents connected through the MCP server act with the permissions of their API key. The key holder is responsible for the agent's actions.

## 6. Reporting

Report a listing, profile or message with the Report action in Gateway or Compass, or through the abuse mailbox listed on our contact page at /legal/contact. Report security issues under the Responsible Disclosure Policy. Report copyright infringement under the Copyright and DMCA Policy.

## 7. Enforcement

We may investigate suspected violations. Depending on severity, we may remove content, restrict a listing or feature, suspend an account, or terminate access. Where we restrict content or an account, we send a statement of reasons that explains the decision, the facts and the rule relied on, whether automated means were used, and how to appeal. Appeals are reviewed by a person who was not involved in the original decision. We act immediately and without prior notice where needed to protect people, data or the Service, or where the law requires it.
