## 4. Device Data and Permissions

The App asks for permissions only when a feature needs them:

- **Location** to check clock-in and clock-out against a work site geofence, and to fill in the location of an incident report. Location is sampled only while a shift or report needs it, within a battery budget.
- **Camera** to scan barcodes, QR codes and credentials, and to take photos for incidents, inspections and deliveries. Photos are resized, and location data in photo metadata is removed unless your organization turns it on.
- **Microphone** for voice notes.
- **Notifications** for shift reminders, approvals and alerts.
- **Biometrics** to unlock the App. Biometric matching happens on your device through the operating system. We never receive your biometric data.
- **NFC** to read credential tags.

The App stores data on your device in an encrypted database so it works offline. If your organization or you sign out remotely, the App wipes that data. The App does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
