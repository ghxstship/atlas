# AI Features Notice

> **Draft for review by counsel before launch.** This document is a draft. It is not in effect and no one may rely on it. It takes effect only when GHXSTSHIP Industries LLC publishes it at /legal, and its effective date is that date of publication.

This notice explains the artificial intelligence features in XOS 4.0: what they do, what data they use, how to turn them off, and how GHXSTSHIP Industries LLC ("GHXSTSHIP") meets its transparency duties, including under the EU Artificial Intelligence Act.

## 1. Off by Default

Every AI feature is off until an organization Admin turns it on in Settings, AI Features. Admins can turn features on and off one by one, and turning a feature off stops all new data from being sent for it. Usage is metered per organization and limited per plan.

## 2. The Model Provider

AI features use Anthropic's Claude models through the Anthropic API. Anthropic is listed on our Subprocessor List. The model in use is set in configuration and shown in Settings, AI Features. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, data sent through the API is not used to train Anthropic's models.

## 3. The Features and the Data They Use

| Feature               | What it does                                                                                                                 | Data sent to the model                                                                                  |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Assistant             | Answers questions about your organization's data, with links to the records it used                                          | Your question and the records the assistant retrieves through the public API using your own permissions |
| Ask in plain language | Turns a sentence into a saved view or filter, and shows the filter it built                                                  | Your sentence and the names of the fields and values available to you                                   |
| Document extraction   | Reads riders, insurance certificates, invoices, receipts and quotes into structured fields with a confidence score per field | The document you choose to extract                                                                      |
| Drafting              | Drafts SOPs, advance packets, emails, day sheets and change order descriptions in your organization's verbiage library       | Your instructions, the relevant records and the verbiage library                                        |
| Summaries             | Summarizes a record's activity, a project's week and gate readiness                                                          | The activity and records being summarized                                                               |
| Mapping help          | Suggests column mappings in the import wizard and catalog bindings for unknown scanned codes                                 | Column headers and sample values from your import, or the unknown code                                  |
| Compass assistant     | Answers policy, procedure and role questions from your organization's own SOPs, handbook and records only                    | Your question and the matching content from your organization's SOPs, handbook and records              |

## 4. What Is Never Sent

- Medical encounter records.
- Any field classified Restricted, including pay rates, bank details, tax identifiers and government identifiers.
- Data you are not permitted to see. The assistant acts through the public API with your own token, so it can never see or do more than you.

## 5. No Training on Customer Data

Customer data, including prompts and outputs, is never used to train any model, by GHXSTSHIP or by Anthropic.

## 6. Human Control

- **Nothing happens without you.** AI features never write, send or approve anything without an explicit confirmation step that shows exactly what will change.
- **Extraction is reviewed.** Extracted fields show a confidence score and are recorded with provenance `agent`. A person confirms before anything is written.
- **No guessing.** When your data does not support an answer, the assistant returns NO ANSWER and says what is missing. It never guesses a price, date or quantity.
- **No automated decisions about people.** AI features do not decide whom to engage, rank applicants for selection, set pay or classify workers. Those decisions stay with people in your organization.

## 7. Transparency Labels

Content generated by an AI feature carries a visible label in the interface, and, where it is exported into a document, a label in that document, so readers know it was AI-generated. This meets the transparency obligations of Article 50 of the EU Artificial Intelligence Act for AI-generated content. When you interact with the assistant, the interface makes clear that you are interacting with an AI system.

## 8. Logging and Retention

Prompts and outputs are logged so the organization can audit AI use. Personal data in those logs is redacted, and the logs are kept under the audit retention rules in our Data Retention and Deletion Policy.

## 9. Quality

Each feature has a fixed evaluation test set. Our build fails if a feature drops below its accuracy threshold, so a change that makes a feature worse cannot ship.

## 10. Your Choices

- **Organizations** can turn any feature off in Settings, AI Features at any time.
- **Individuals** can choose not to use AI features, and can ask their organization's Admin to turn them off. Records are always available without AI.
- **Questions** go to your organization's Admin, or to the privacy mailbox listed on our contact page at /legal/contact.
