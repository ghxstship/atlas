# Accessibility Statement

> **Draft for review by counsel before launch.** This document is a draft. It is not in effect and no one may rely on it. It takes effect only when GHXSTSHIP Industries LLC publishes it at /legal, and its effective date is that date of publication.

GHXSTSHIP Industries LLC ("GHXSTSHIP") wants everyone who plans, staffs, supplies and works at live productions to be able to use XOS 4.0, including people with disabilities. This statement covers Atlas, Gateway, Compass for iOS and Android, the documentation site, our emails and the PDFs XOS generates.

## 1. Our Standard

We design and test XOS to conform to the **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA**. Meeting that standard is how we address:

- Section 508 of the US Rehabilitation Act
- Titles II and III of the Americans with Disabilities Act, for public-sector and public-accommodation customers
- EN 301 549 and the European Accessibility Act
- the Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act and the Accessible Canada Act

Generated PDFs are built to PDF/UA. Emails use semantic HTML with alternative text, a plain-text version, and remain readable at 200 percent zoom.

## 2. Conformance Status

XOS 4.0 is in development. Our target is full conformance with WCAG 2.2 Level AA. Formal evaluation begins in our independent verification phase, and our Accessibility Conformance Reports for Atlas and for Compass, in the VPAT 2.5 International edition format, will report the results. Until those reports are published, we do not claim conformance. We will update this statement with the conformance status and any known exceptions when evaluation is complete.

## 3. What We Build In

- Full keyboard operation of every screen, including the command menu, tables and dialogs, with visible focus.
- Screen reader support with VoiceOver on macOS and iOS, NVDA on Windows, and TalkBack on Android.
- Color contrast that meets WCAG 2.2 AA in light and dark themes. The white-label theme editor blocks any brand color combination that fails contrast.
- No information conveyed by color alone.
- Reduced motion respected everywhere, with haptics optional in Compass.
- In Compass: Dynamic Type and font scaling to 200 percent, labels for every control, and touch targets of at least 48 points.
- Layouts that support right-to-left languages.
- Help text on every field with legal or financial meaning, and tooltips that never hold information found nowhere else.

## 4. How We Test

- Automated axe-core checks in unit tests, component stories and end-to-end tests, with zero serious or critical violations allowed in any build.
- Keyboard-only journey tests.
- Manual keyboard and screen reader passes on every core screen, recorded in our accessibility audit log.
- Reduced-motion tests on web and mobile.
- An independent accessibility reviewer audits Atlas and Compass before each major release.

## 5. Feedback and Response Commitment

If you have trouble using any part of XOS, or need information in another format, tell us:

- in the Service: Help, then Contact Support, and choose Accessibility
- by email: the accessibility mailbox listed on our contact page at /legal/contact
- by mail: the postal address listed on that contact page

**We respond within 5 business days.** Our response will acknowledge the issue, tell you how we will address it, and offer a way to get the information or complete the task you need in the meantime. We log every report and track it to resolution.

If you are a crew member, vendor or other external party using XOS for an organization, you can also contact that organization, which can request help from us for you.

## 6. Formal Complaints

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may ask for review by writing to the accessibility mailbox with "Review" in the subject. A person not involved in the first response will review it within 10 business days. You may also contact the enforcement body in your country.

## 7. Third-Party Content

Content that Customers upload, such as riders, documents and images, is outside our control. We give Customers tools to add alternative text and accessible names, and we encourage them to use them.

## 8. Review of This Statement

We review this statement at least once a year and after each major release.
