# Trademark Policy

> **Draft before launch.** This document is a draft. It is not in effect and no one may rely on it. Showing it at /legal does not put it in effect. It takes effect only on the effective date that GHXSTSHIP Industries LLC states at the top of a final version.

This policy expands on `TRADEMARKS.md` in the XOS repository. It also covers Aurora, the GHXSTSHIP name and some uses that file does not mention. If they ever differ, the version published at /legal controls.

## 1. Our Marks

XOS, Atlas, Gateway, Compass and Aurora, as names of this platform and its features, the GHXSTSHIP name, and their logos (the "Marks") are marks of GHXSTSHIP Industries LLC ("GHXSTSHIP"). The open source licenses that cover XOS code (AGPL-3.0 and MIT) and canon data (CC BY 4.0) grant rights to the code and data. They do not grant rights to the Marks. Calling a name a mark in this policy does not say whether it is registered in any country.

We want the Marks to mean one thing: the official XOS platform and releases from GHXSTSHIP. This policy lets the community talk about XOS freely while protecting people from confusion about what is official.

## 2. Uses That Need No Permission

- Stating truthfully that your product or service works with, integrates with, or is built on XOS, for example "Sync your shifts with XOS" or "a Compass integration for ticketing data", as long as the Mark describes the connection and is not part of your product's name.
- Referring to the XOS Bible or XOS canon by name when you use or cite the published canon data under CC BY 4.0, with the attribution that license requires.
- Redistributing unmodified official releases under their original names.
- Writing articles, reviews, tutorials, courses or books about XOS, and using the word marks in their text.
- Naming a community group "for XOS users", provided it says it is not run by GHXSTSHIP.
- Saying that a modified version is "based on XOS", as Section 3 explains.

This policy does not limit any use the law allows without permission. That includes accurately naming XOS to compare it with something else, and honest reviews, criticism, commentary and news reporting, whether favorable or not.

## 3. Uses That Need Written Permission

- Using a Mark in the name of a product, service, company, app or domain name.
- Distributing a modified build under a Mark. A modified build must use a different name and state clearly that it is not the official release and is not endorsed by GHXSTSHIP. You may say it is "based on XOS".
- Hosting XOS for others under a Mark. A self-hosted deployment offered to third parties must use its own name.
- Using the logos, except in unmodified official materials.
- Merchandise bearing a Mark.

Permission counts only if it is in writing from GHXSTSHIP. It covers only the use it describes and may be withdrawn if that use stops following this policy.

## 4. Uses That Are Never Allowed

- Any use that suggests GHXSTSHIP sponsors, endorses or certifies you when it does not.
- Altering the Marks, combining them with other marks, or using confusingly similar names, logos or domains.
- Registering a Mark, or something confusingly similar, as a trademark, company name, domain name, social media handle or app store listing.
- Using the Marks in a way that is false or misleading.

## 5. White-Label Tenants

Customers on plans that include white label may apply their own brand and remove "Powered by XOS". That right comes from the subscription under Sections 5.8 and 10.3 of the Terms of Service, not from this policy, and it ends as those sections describe after a downgrade below the entitled plan. White label is a right to show the Customer's brand. It is not a license to use the Marks, and the Customer remains responsible for having the rights to the brand it applies.

## 6. How to Display the Marks

- Use the Marks as adjectives with a generic term where practical, for example "the XOS platform" or "the Compass app".
- Do not pluralize or abbreviate the Marks.
- Do not add a registration symbol to a Mark.
- In materials that use the Marks prominently, include this notice: "XOS, Atlas, Gateway, Compass, Aurora and GHXSTSHIP are marks of GHXSTSHIP Industries LLC."

## 7. Requests and Reports

Requests for permission, and reports of misuse, go to the Owner named in `GOVERNANCE.md` through support@ghxstship.pro with "Legal Notice" in the subject line. A request should say which Mark you want to use, where and how, and include a sample if you have one. A report should say what was used, where we can see it, and why it may confuse people. Changes to this policy require the Owner's written approval.

## 8. Other Companies' Marks

XOS and its documents refer to products of other companies, such as Apple, App Store, Apple Wallet, Face ID, Google, Google Play, Google Wallet, Sign in with Google, Stripe, Anthropic and Claude. Those names and logos belong to their owners. Naming them says only that XOS works with or uses their products. It does not mean they sponsor or endorse XOS, and this policy gives no right to use them.
