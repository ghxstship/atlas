# Trademark Policy

> **Draft for review by counsel before launch.** This document is a draft. It is not in effect and no one may rely on it. It takes effect only when GHXSTSHIP Industries LLC publishes it at /legal, and its effective date is that date of publication.

This policy expands on `TRADEMARKS.md` in the XOS repository and is consistent with it. If they ever differ, the version published at /legal controls.

## 1. Our Marks

XOS, Atlas, Gateway and Compass, as names of this platform, and their logos (the "Marks") are marks of GHXSTSHIP Industries LLC ("GHXSTSHIP"). The open source licenses that cover XOS code (AGPL-3.0 and MIT) and canon data (CC BY 4.0) grant rights to the code and data. They do not grant rights to the Marks.

We want the Marks to mean one thing: the official XOS platform and releases from GHXSTSHIP. This policy lets the community talk about XOS freely while protecting people from confusion about what is official.

## 2. Uses That Need No Permission

- Stating truthfully that your product or service works with, integrates with, or is built on XOS, for example "Sync your shifts with XOS" or "a Compass integration for ticketing data", as long as the Mark is not the most prominent element of your product name.
- Referring to the XOS Bible or XOS canon by name when you use or cite the published canon data under CC BY 4.0, with the attribution that license requires.
- Redistributing unmodified official releases under their original names.
- Writing articles, reviews, tutorials, courses or books about XOS, and using the word marks in their text.
- Naming a community group "for XOS users", provided it says it is not run by GHXSTSHIP.

## 3. Uses That Need Written Permission

- Using a Mark in the name of a product, service, company, app or domain name.
- Distributing a modified build under a Mark. A modified build must use a different name and state clearly that it is not the official release and is not endorsed by GHXSTSHIP. You may say it is "based on XOS".
- Hosting XOS for others under a Mark. A self-hosted deployment offered to third parties must use its own name.
- Using the logos, except in unmodified official materials.
- Merchandise bearing a Mark.

## 4. Uses That Are Never Allowed

- Any use that suggests GHXSTSHIP sponsors, endorses or certifies you when it does not.
- Altering the Marks, combining them with other marks, or using confusingly similar names, logos or domains.
- Using the Marks in a way that is false, misleading or disparaging.

## 5. White-Label Tenants

Customers on plans that include white label may apply their own brand and remove "Powered by XOS". That right comes from the subscription under the Terms of Service, not from this policy, and ends at the next billing cycle after a downgrade below the entitled plan.

## 6. How to Display the Marks

- Use the Marks as adjectives with a generic term where practical, for example "the XOS platform" or "the Compass app".
- Do not pluralize or abbreviate the Marks.
- In materials that use the Marks prominently, include this notice: "XOS, Atlas, Gateway and Compass are marks of GHXSTSHIP Industries LLC."

## 7. Requests and Reports

Requests for permission, and reports of misuse, go to the Owner named in `GOVERNANCE.md` through the legal notices mailbox listed on our contact page at /legal/contact. Changes to this policy require the Owner's written approval.
