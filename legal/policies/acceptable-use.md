# Acceptable Use Policy for Personnel

> **Draft for review by counsel before launch.** This internal policy is a draft. It takes effect when the Owner approves it, and its effective date is that date of approval.

This policy covers how GHXSTSHIP Industries LLC ("GHXSTSHIP") personnel use company systems, devices and information. The customer-facing rules for XOS users are in the separate Acceptable Use Policy published at /legal.

## 1. Scope

Employees, contractors and anyone else with access to GHXSTSHIP systems, including people who direct AI coding agents on GHXSTSHIP's behalf.

## 2. General Rules

- Use company systems for GHXSTSHIP business. Limited personal use is allowed if it does not affect security, performance or other people.
- Follow the Information Security Policy and its supporting policies.
- Protect information according to the Data Classification Policy.
- Report suspected incidents, lost devices and phishing immediately through the on-call channel.

## 3. Accounts and Credentials

- Use your own named accounts. Never share passwords, MFA factors, API keys or session tokens.
- Use the company password manager for every work credential.
- Use phishing-resistant MFA (passkeys or security keys) for production and administrative access.
- Never put credentials in code, tickets, chat, email or AI prompts.

## 4. Devices

- Work devices must have full-disk encryption, a supported and updated operating system, automatic screen lock after 5 minutes, and the firewall on.
- Install software only from trusted sources. Do not disable security tools.
- Lock your screen when you step away. Do not leave devices unattended in public.
- Report a lost or stolen device within one hour so access can be revoked.
- Personal devices used for work email or chat must meet the same rules and allow remote sign-out of work accounts.

## 5. Customer Data

- Do not view Customer Data in the Service except within a support session the Customer's Owner has granted, and only for the purpose of that session.
- Never copy Customer Data to personal devices, personal accounts, unapproved tools or AI services not on the Subprocessor List.
- Never use production data for development or testing.

## 6. AI Tools

- Use only AI tools approved by the Security Lead.
- Never paste Customer Data, Restricted information, secrets or unreleased security findings into an AI tool that is not approved for that class of data.
- Review AI-generated code and text as you would any contribution. You are responsible for what you commit.

## 7. Communication

- Use company channels for work communication.
- Do not forward company email to personal accounts.
- Verify unusual requests for payments, credentials or data through a second channel.

## 8. Prohibited Activities

- Accessing systems or data without authorization, or testing security outside an approved plan.
- Installing unapproved remote-access, file-sharing or browser extensions with broad permissions on work devices.
- Harassment, discrimination or illegal activity using company systems.
- Circumventing monitoring, logging or security controls.

## 9. Monitoring

GHXSTSHIP may monitor company systems and accounts for security and compliance, within the limits of applicable law. Personal use of company systems carries no expectation of privacy beyond what the law provides.

## 10. Return of Assets

On leaving, return all company devices and information, and confirm deletion of any company information on personal devices.

## 11. Acknowledgment and Review

Personnel acknowledge this policy at onboarding and every year with security training. It is reviewed at least once a year.
