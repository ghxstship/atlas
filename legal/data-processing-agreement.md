# Data Processing Agreement

> **Draft before launch.** This document is a draft. It is not in effect and no one may rely on it. Showing it at /legal does not put it in effect. It takes effect only on the effective date that GHXSTSHIP Industries LLC states at the top of a final version.

This Data Processing Agreement (the "DPA") forms part of the Terms of Service (the "Agreement") between GHXSTSHIP Industries LLC ("GHXSTSHIP" or "Processor") and the Customer named in the Agreement ("Customer" or "Controller"). The Customer's Owner accepts this DPA at signup by ticking the acceptance box. A partner can create client organizations only once GHXSTSHIP has turned that on for it, which is off by default. A partner that creates a client organization accepts this DPA for that client, as the client's agent, when it creates the organization. Under its own acceptance of this DPA, the partner warrants that it has the client's authority to do so, and it must have the client's Owner record the client's own acceptance in Settings, Legal, before any of the client's personal data enters the Service. This DPA applies whenever GHXSTSHIP processes Customer Personal Data in providing the Service, including for a client organization.

## 1. Definitions

- **Data Protection Laws** means all laws on privacy and personal data that apply to a party's processing under the Agreement, including, where they apply, the GDPR (Regulation (EU) 2016/679), the UK GDPR and the UK Data Protection Act 2018 as amended by the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025, the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (nFADP), the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA ("CCPA"), the other US state comprehensive privacy laws, US state data security and breach notification laws, PIPEDA and Quebec Law 25, the LGPD, the Australian Privacy Act, the APPI, the India Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, the Singapore PDPA, POPIA and the Korean PIPA.
- **Customer Personal Data** means personal data within Customer Data, as defined in the Agreement, and any other personal data GHXSTSHIP processes on the Customer's behalf in providing the Service, including data about people who reach the Service only through a link or code the Customer issues, or who appear only on a list or record the Customer keeps.
- **Personal Data Breach** means a breach of security leading to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure of, or access to, Customer Personal Data in GHXSTSHIP's systems or its subprocessors' systems.
- **Subprocessor** means a third party GHXSTSHIP engages that processes Customer Personal Data.
- **EU SCCs** means the standard contractual clauses for the transfer of personal data to third countries adopted by European Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914 of 4 June 2021.
- **UK Addendum** means the International Data Transfer Addendum to the EU Commission Standard Contractual Clauses, version B1.0, issued by the UK Information Commissioner under section 119A of the Data Protection Act 2018 and in force from 21 March 2022, as revised from time to time.
- **Contact Page** means the page at /legal/contact listing GHXSTSHIP's postal address, privacy and security mailboxes, privacy officer and appointed representatives.
- Terms such as "controller", "processor", "data subject", "personal data", "processing" and "supervisory authority" have the meanings Data Protection Laws give them. Under the CCPA, "controller" includes "business" and "processor" includes "service provider".

## 2. Roles and Scope

2.1 The Customer is the controller of Customer Personal Data, and GHXSTSHIP is its processor. Where the Customer is itself a processor for another controller (for example, an agency operating XOS for a client, or a partner organization operating a client organization), GHXSTSHIP is a subprocessor and the Customer warrants that its instructions are authorized by that controller.

2.2 Annex I describes the processing. The subject matter is the provision of the Service. The duration is the term of the Agreement plus the deletion period in Section 10.

2.3 This DPA does not apply to personal data for which GHXSTSHIP is a controller, as described in the Privacy Policy. That data is a person's own account and what they keep in it for themselves (including their Work Details, such as dietary needs, allergies, accessibility needs, passport details and emergency contacts, and any supplier diversity status they record), public marketplace profiles a person chooses to publish, the display of ratings and the rating disputes Section 2.5 describes, account security data, and Customer billing records, including the billing evidence in Section 10.3. When a person shares any of those details with a Customer for an engagement, what the Customer receives and everything about that engagement is Customer Personal Data: the Customer is its controller, and this DPA applies to it.

2.4 This DPA is not a business associate agreement under the US Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA). GHXSTSHIP does not offer the Service as a covered entity or business associate service and does not sign business associate agreements.

2.5 A rating the Customer gives of an individual is Customer Personal Data in the Customer's own records. It is shown beyond the Customer only if that individual opts in to showing it on their profile, as Section 7.5 of the Agreement describes, and GHXSTSHIP is the controller of that display. A rating of a company or vendor, and a party's rating of the Customer, can be read by signed-in users of the Service under the rule GHXSTSHIP sets, and GHXSTSHIP is the controller of that display too. The Customer's rating setting decides only whether its own Members are prompted to rate. GHXSTSHIP decides disputes about ratings and may remove a rating from every product read on the grounds the Worker Terms name, regardless of the Customer's instructions. The rating's row and its audit entry stay as evidence in the Customer's records, and a platform operator's change to a dispute is recorded in the Customer's audit ledger with the acting person's identifier.

## 3. Customer Instructions

3.1 GHXSTSHIP processes Customer Personal Data, including any transfer of it to another country, only on the Customer's documented instructions. The Agreement, this DPA, the Customer's configuration of the Service, and the actions of its Members and authorized API clients are the Customer's complete instructions. Additional instructions require written agreement.

3.2 GHXSTSHIP will immediately tell the Customer if, in its opinion, an instruction infringes Data Protection Laws, and may suspend the affected processing until the Customer confirms or changes the instruction.

3.3 GHXSTSHIP may process Customer Personal Data otherwise only where the law requires it. In that case GHXSTSHIP will inform the Customer before processing unless the law forbids it.

3.4 The Customer decides what personal data enters the Service and is responsible for its lawful basis and for the notices and consents the law requires, including for people who hold no account and accept no XOS terms, such as guests, form respondents, emergency contacts and people named in incidents. Special category, medical, financial and government identifier data belongs in the fields built for it, listed in Annex I, and not in free-text notes, custom fields, form answers or general files, where those protections do not apply. Dietary, allergy and accessibility needs belong only in the fields built for them: the Customer will not collect them in free text, custom fields, form questions, banquet event order notes or imports. As a backstop only, the Service refuses common words for them in door notes, travel notes, custom field labels, import column mappings and question labels, and it does not read answers. Where someone acting for the Customer enters a guest's needs or a rostered person's meal preference, the Customer warrants that they have that person's authority and that the person has had notice. The Customer is responsible for handling workers' medical and accommodation information as the law requires of an employer or engager, including keeping it confidential and limiting who holds the capabilities that reveal it. The Service is not an electronic Form I-9 system and has no field for right-to-work documents. Employment eligibility checks stay with the Customer or its payroll provider. The Customer will not upload copies of identity documents or Forms I-9 as general files, and will not ask people to do so through an onboarding upload.

## 4. CCPA and US State Law Service Provider Terms

GHXSTSHIP processes Customer Personal Data as a service provider for the limited and specified business purposes listed under "Purpose" in Annex I. GHXSTSHIP will not:

- sell or share Customer Personal Data, as those terms are defined in the CCPA
- retain, use or disclose Customer Personal Data for any purpose other than those business purposes, including for any commercial purpose other than providing the Service
- retain, use or disclose Customer Personal Data outside the direct business relationship between the parties
- combine Customer Personal Data with personal data it receives from other sources, except as the CCPA permits for service providers

GHXSTSHIP will comply with the obligations of the CCPA and the other US state privacy laws that apply to processors and service providers and provide the same level of privacy protection those laws require of the Customer. That includes helping the Customer with its cybersecurity audits, risk assessments, automated decision-making duties and consumer requests as Sections 8, 11 and 12 describe. GHXSTSHIP will notify the Customer promptly after it determines that it can no longer meet those obligations.

The Customer may take reasonable and appropriate steps to make sure GHXSTSHIP uses Customer Personal Data consistently with the Customer's obligations under those laws. Those steps may include reviewing the information in Section 11.1 and, at least once every 12 months, an assessment, audit or other technical and operational testing on the terms in Section 11.2, without first having to show that the information was insufficient. On notice, the Customer may also take reasonable and appropriate steps to stop and remediate unauthorized use. The Customer will tell GHXSTSHIP of any consumer request GHXSTSHIP must act on and that the Customer cannot complete through the Service. GHXSTSHIP will have a written contract with each Subprocessor, as Section 7.4 describes, before that Subprocessor processes Customer Personal Data. GHXSTSHIP does not act as a service provider for the display of ratings or the rating disputes under Section 2.5, so this section does not cover them. GHXSTSHIP certifies that it understands these restrictions.

## 5. Confidentiality and Personnel

GHXSTSHIP ensures that everyone it authorizes to process Customer Personal Data is bound by confidentiality and has access only as their role requires. Its written policies also call for security and privacy training at hire and every year.

GHXSTSHIP personnel open Customer Data in the Service only during a support session granted by the Customer's Owner. A session lasts from 1 to 24 hours, states a reason and is read-only, and each read is logged to the Customer's audit ledger. Without a session, operators see what they need to run the Service, such as an organization's name, plan and status, its billing state, and public listings and the reports about them. GHXSTSHIP's operator console also shows, for each open request in a Customer's data subject register, the organization, the kind of request and its dates, so that we can see when a clock is running. It does not show who asked or what they said.

A support session cannot find, return or erase data, and it shows only limited areas of an organization. So outside a session, GHXSTSHIP personnel may access Customer Personal Data only:

- on the Customer's written instruction under Section 8.3 or 10.2, or a person's request to delete their own account through the mailbox that the account deletion page names
- to run and verify backups and restores
- to respond to a security or availability incident
- where the law compels it or to prevent imminent harm to people or to the Service

GHXSTSHIP keeps a record of who did each such piece of work, when and why, and gives it to the Customer on request. A change made this way to a record with an audit trail also appears in the Customer's audit ledger. A read made this way is not written to that ledger automatically. GHXSTSHIP will tell the Customer of access for an incident, or where the law compels it or to prevent harm, unless the law forbids telling it. Automated processes that run the Service, such as virus scanning, notifications and backups, handle Customer Personal Data without a person viewing it.

GHXSTSHIP's written policy limits access to production infrastructure to named people for a stated task, with logging.

## 7. Subprocessors

7.1 **General authorization.** The Customer gives GHXSTSHIP general written authorization to engage the Subprocessors on the Subprocessor List published at /legal/subprocessors.

7.2 **Notice of changes.** GHXSTSHIP will give at least 30 days' notice before a new Subprocessor processes Customer Personal Data, by updating the Subprocessor List and notifying everyone who subscribes to change notifications through the subscription form on that page. The Customer's Owner and Admins are subscribed by default.

7.3 **Objection.** The Customer may object to a new Subprocessor on reasonable data protection grounds within the notice period. The parties will discuss the objection in good faith. If GHXSTSHIP cannot offer a reasonable alternative, the Customer may terminate the affected part of the Service and receive a refund of prepaid fees for the remaining term.

7.4 **Flow-down.** Before a Subprocessor processes Customer Personal Data, GHXSTSHIP will have a written contract with it that imposes in substance the same data protection obligations as this DPA. For most Subprocessors, that contract is the vendor's own published data protection terms, so it can differ from this DPA in detail. Section 7.5 and Section 10.4 state the differences GHXSTSHIP knows of, and the Subprocessor List gives the detail. GHXSTSHIP remains liable to the Customer for each Subprocessor's performance. On request, GHXSTSHIP will give the Customer a copy of a Subprocessor's data protection terms, with commercial or confidential details removed.

7.5 **A Subprocessor's own vendors.** Sections 7.2 and 7.3 cover the Subprocessors GHXSTSHIP engages directly. A Subprocessor may change its own subprocessors under its published terms, and GHXSTSHIP does not control those changes and cannot promise 30 days' notice of them. Section 7.4 still applies.

## 8. Data Subject Requests

8.1 Settings, Data and Privacy includes a register for requests for access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. It tracks each request's state with a 30-day clock (which can be extended by 60 days with a reason), shows legal holds and keeps an evidence record. The register records the Customer's handling of a request. It does not itself find, export, change or delete a person's data: the Customer does that with the Service's records, settings and exports. When a person deletes their own account, each related organization receives a completed erasure entry in its register, as Section 8.4 describes. GHXSTSHIP's operator console also shows, for each open request in the register, the organization, the kind of request and its dates, so that we can see when a clock is running. It does not show who asked or what they said. That clock remains the Customer's. GHXSTSHIP does not answer the request.

8.2 If GHXSTSHIP receives a request about Customer Personal Data, it will route it to the Customer without undue delay and will not answer it except to confirm the routing, unless the Customer authorizes it. A person's deletion of their own account follows Section 8.4 instead.

8.3 GHXSTSHIP will give reasonable help with requests the Customer cannot answer through the Service. That includes finding, returning or erasing data about a person across tables, and about a person who has no account, on the Customer's written instruction through the privacy mailbox on the Contact Page. GHXSTSHIP responds within 10 business days of that instruction, saying what it has done and anything it cannot do and why. Business days are Monday to Friday, except US federal holidays, in New York time.

8.4 **A person's own account deletion.** A person can delete their own account in Gateway or Compass, and it takes effect at once. GHXSTSHIP does not route this to the Customer first, and the Customer gets no notice before it happens. The Customer instructs the effects below by accepting this DPA:

- the person's memberships end, and engagements still Onboarding, Blocked or Active are canceled
- the full name on the person's account is cleared, so the Customer's time, pay, engagement and other records that point to that person then show a person with no name. Separate given name and family name fields on the account are not cleared today
- the records themselves stay with the Customer, and a tax form keeps its own legal name field
- a completed erasure entry appears in the Customer's register after the deletion

The Customer's only control before the deletion is an active legal hold, which refuses it while the hold lasts. A Customer that must keep a worker's name with its wage or other records should run its payroll exports on schedule, and place a legal hold where it has grounds for one. The deletion is also refused while the person is clocked in, or is the only Owner of an organization.

## 9. Personal Data Breaches

9.1 GHXSTSHIP will notify the Customer of a Personal Data Breach without undue delay, and in any case within 48 hours after becoming aware of it. Notice goes by email to the Customer's Owner and to any other security contact the Customer has given GHXSTSHIP in writing.

9.2 The notice will describe, as far as then known: the nature of the breach, the categories and approximate number of data subjects and records, the likely consequences, the measures taken or proposed, and a contact for more information. GHXSTSHIP will provide further information in stages as it becomes available.

9.3 GHXSTSHIP will take reasonable steps to contain, investigate and remediate the breach and to help the Customer meet its own notification duties to authorities and data subjects. Notifying the Customer is not an admission of fault.

9.4 GHXSTSHIP keeps a record of each Personal Data Breach, with its facts, effects and remedial steps, and will give the Customer the part of that record the Customer needs for its own records.

## 10. Return and Deletion

10.1 **Export during the term.** An Owner, using a second factor, can download an organization export at any time as one archive with a manifest. It holds JSON and CSV of the organization's tables, limited to the columns the Owner may read, and Clean files from the attachments store up to 500 files and 500 MB. The manifest counts what was left out. The export does not include Restricted columns, files in other stores or files past those limits. The Customer can also read Customer Personal Data through the Service and the public API.

10.2 **Return of what the export omits.** At the Customer's choice, on a written request to the legal notices mailbox on the Contact Page made before deletion, GHXSTSHIP will return Customer Personal Data the export omits in a commonly used format within 30 days of the request, subject to security checks. A request made before deletion holds the deletion until the return is complete, as Section 11.2 of the Agreement says. While GHXSTSHIP returns the data, nobody in the Customer's organization can release that hold. To withdraw the request, the Customer writes to legal@ghxstship.tech. A request made after the organization's data has been purged cannot be met, and GHXSTSHIP says so.

10.3 **Deletion.** An Owner's two actions lead to deletion, and so does any other ending of the Agreement:

- **Canceling the organization.** The organization is read-only from the moment of cancellation. For 30 days an Owner can reactivate it, on the Access Plan. The export remains available until deletion. Deletion starts on day 90.
- **Deleting the organization.** This needs an Owner, a second factor and the typed name of the organization. The organization is read-only at once, an Owner can recover it for the rest of that day and 7 whole days, and deletion starts when that window ends. There is no separate 30-day period, so an Owner should export first and make any request under Section 10.2.
- **Any other ending of the Agreement.** Where the Agreement ends in a way Section 11.10 of the Agreement covers, such as termination for breach, the organization is read-only from the end date, with no reactivation unless GHXSTSHIP agrees, and deletion starts on day 90 after the end date. A switch to another provider follows Section 11.3 of the Agreement.

Deletion removes the organization's stored files and then its database rows, and the Owner receives a deletion certificate that records the counts of what was deleted. GHXSTSHIP keeps that certificate record after deletion. GHXSTSHIP also keeps, as controller and not as Customer Personal Data, the billing evidence for the organization: the renewal terms an Owner agreed to, each cancellation sent to Stripe and its attempts, renewal reminders and billing alerts, with the organization's identifier and name. The renewal terms are kept 7 years from the agreement, or until 1 year after that Plan ends if that is later, and the rest 7 years, as the Data Retention and Deletion Policy states. Deletion does not purge it, and the deletion certificate does not cover it. No subprocessor is added for it: it is held with Stripe and the existing email provider. A legal hold the Customer has placed stops deletion until it is released. Ending a paid Plan alone moves the organization to the Access Plan and starts no deletion.

10.4 **Backups and Subprocessors.** Copies in backups expire on their schedule: daily backups and point-in-time history, where it is enabled, within 7 days, and weekly backup archives within 35 days once that job is running (it has not yet completed a run). Copies that a Subprocessor holds in logs or its own short-term stores expire under that Subprocessor's terms. Some copies can last longer. Anthropic's published terms, as the Subprocessor List states, let it keep content flagged for a policy reason for up to 2 years and safety scores for up to 7 years. So data sent to an AI feature can outlast deletion and the deletion certificate. GHXSTSHIP claims no shorter retention arrangement with Anthropic.

10.5 **Retention the law requires.** GHXSTSHIP may retain Customer Personal Data where the law requires it, and will keep it confidential and process it only for that purpose.

## 11. Audits and Information

In either case this happens once in any 12-month period, on at least 30 days' written notice, during business hours, at the Customer's cost, and without access to other customers' data. An audit after a Personal Data Breach does not count toward the annual limit.

11.3 GHXSTSHIP will cooperate with any cybersecurity audit or risk assessment the law requires of the Customer, and will give accurate facts about the processing it performs for that purpose.

## 13. International Transfers

13.1 **Locations.** Customer Personal Data is hosted in the United States. Hosting in the European Union is not offered. GHXSTSHIP sets it up only under a signed Enterprise contract it has accepted that names it. The Subprocessor List names the EU region before any Customer Personal Data is hosted there. Subprocessors may process Customer Personal Data in the locations shown on the Subprocessor List.

13.2 **EU SCCs.** To the extent the Customer transfers personal data subject to the GDPR to GHXSTSHIP in a country without an adequacy decision, the EU SCCs are incorporated into this DPA by reference and completed as follows:

- Module Two (controller to processor) applies where the Customer is a controller. Module Three (processor to processor) applies where the Customer is a processor. Under Module Three, the Customer's instructions are treated as the controller's, and GHXSTSHIP gives notices due to the controller to the Customer, which passes them on, unless the EU SCCs require otherwise.
- Clause 7 (docking clause) applies.
- Clause 9: Option 2 (general written authorization) applies, with the notice period in Section 7.2.
- Clause 11: the optional language on an independent dispute resolution body does not apply.
- Clause 13: the competent supervisory authority is the one determined under Clause 13(a): by the Customer's establishment; or, where the Customer has none in the EU, by its representative under Article 27 GDPR; or, where it need not have one, by the Member State where the data subjects are.
- Clause 17: Option 1 applies. The EU SCCs are governed by the law of Ireland, the EU Member State named as the SCC governing law on the Contact Page.
- Clause 18: disputes are resolved before the courts of Ireland.
- Annex I.A and I.B are completed by Annex I of this DPA. Annex I.C is completed by the Clause 13 entry above. Annex II is completed by Annex II of this DPA. Annex III is completed by the Subprocessor List.
- The Customer's acceptance of this DPA, and GHXSTSHIP's publication and performance of it, count as each party's signature of the EU SCCs and their annexes, dated when the Customer accepts or, for a client organization, when the partner accepts for it under the opening paragraph of this DPA.

13.3 **UK Addendum.** To the extent the transfer is subject to the UK GDPR, the UK Addendum is incorporated by reference. Table 1 is completed with the parties' details in Annex I, and its start date is the date of acceptance under Section 13.2. Table 2 selects the EU SCC modules and options in Section 13.2. Table 3 is completed by Annex I, Annex II and the Subprocessor List. In Table 4, either party may end the UK Addendum as its Section 19 allows.

13.4 **Switzerland.** To the extent the transfer is subject to the nFADP, the EU SCCs apply with these changes: the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner is the competent supervisory authority for such transfers; references to the GDPR are read as references to the nFADP; references to EU Member State law include Swiss law where the nFADP requires; and the term "Member State" does not exclude data subjects in Switzerland from enforcing their rights in their place of habitual residence.

13.5 **Other regimes.**

- **Brazil.** The EU SCCs are not a transfer mechanism under the LGPD. Before the Customer transfers personal data subject to the LGPD to GHXSTSHIP, the parties will sign an addendum that annexes the standard contractual clauses of Resolution CD/ANPD No. 19/2024 in full and without change. Until then, the Customer will not make that transfer under this DPA.
- **Quebec.** GHXSTSHIP will give the Customer the information it needs for an assessment under section 17 of Quebec's private sector privacy act before the Customer communicates personal data outside Quebec.
- **Other laws.** For transfers subject to other Data Protection Laws, such as those of Canada and Australia, this DPA's confidentiality, security, subprocessor, breach and deletion terms are the contractual protection the parties rely on. The EU SCCs as completed above also apply to the extent that law recognizes them, and the parties will sign any additional terms that law requires.

13.6 **Supplementary measures.** GHXSTSHIP encrypts Customer Personal Data in transit and at rest, encrypts the fields listed in Annex II at the field level, minimizes data sent to each Subprocessor, and will challenge any government request for Customer Personal Data that it considers unlawful. GHXSTSHIP will notify the Customer of a government request unless the law forbids it, and will publish the number of such requests it receives each calendar year on a page at /legal, starting with the first full calendar year after this DPA takes effect. It will publish a count of zero as well. GHXSTSHIP will give the Customer the information it reasonably needs to assess the law and practice of the destination country under Clause 14 of the EU SCCs.

13.7 **Data Privacy Framework.** GHXSTSHIP is not self-certified to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and does not rely on it for transfers under this DPA unless it appears on the official Data Privacy Framework List, in which case the Contact Page will say so and the EU SCCs remain in place as a fallback. A Subprocessor may rely on the framework for its own transfers under its own terms.

13.8 **Later clauses.** If the European Commission adopts standard contractual clauses for importers whose processing is itself subject to the GDPR, and those clauses apply to a transfer under this DPA, the parties will put them in place for that transfer. Until then, Section 13.2 continues to apply as far as it can.

## 14. Liability and Precedence

Each party's liability under this DPA is subject to the limits in the Agreement, except where Data Protection Laws or the EU SCCs do not allow a limit. Nothing in the Agreement limits a data subject's rights under Clauses 12 and 18 of the EU SCCs or under Data Protection Laws. If this DPA conflicts with the Agreement, this DPA controls for personal data. If the EU SCCs or the UK Addendum conflict with this DPA, they control.

## 15. Changes to This DPA

Section 15 of the Agreement applies to this DPA as it applies to the Agreement. Each version is shown at /legal and is in effect from the effective date it states, and Section 3.6 of the Agreement covers earlier versions. Until the Owner accepts, the version the Customer last accepted continues to apply to it, unless the law requires the change sooner. ## Annex II: Technical and Organizational Measures

This Annex states the measures as they stand. Where a measure rests on a written policy, or is built but not yet running or not yet enforced, the item says so. GHXSTSHIP's written policies take effect when GHXSTSHIP approves them.

1. **Access control.** Role-based access with capabilities enforced in the database by row-level security, which is on for every table of the application and private schemas; time-boxed memberships and grants; separation of duties rules; an access review feature a Customer can run each quarter, which flags access left unreviewed; break-glass access requiring MFA, a written reason and notice to every Owner and Admin, for one hour at most.
2. **Authentication.** A hosted sign-in setting, which GHXSTSHIP's production setup turns on, that checks new passwords against breached-password lists; TOTP and passkey or security key MFA, which an organization can require for Owners and Admins or for everyone. That requirement is enforced where Atlas, the XOS API and Compass check a session, and is not enforced on direct calls to the underlying Supabase data endpoint (Security Overview, Section 3); step-up authentication for data exports of Restricted data, role changes, API key creation, starting a paid plan, deleting an organization and transferring ownership; SAML SSO and SCIM on the Enterprise plan.
3. **Encryption.** TLS 1.2 or higher, with HSTS on Atlas, Gateway and the operator console; AES-256 at rest; field-level pgcrypto encryption for tax identifiers, bank account and routing numbers, passport numbers, supplier certificate numbers and medical treatment notes; an encrypted on-device database in Compass, whose offline records are deleted on sign-out and, after a remote sign-out, when the app next connects and learns of it, so a device that stays offline keeps them until then. Temporary photos and voice notes waiting to upload, and the shift details and queued punches that the clock widget and Live Activity use, are kept outside that database.
4. **Tenant isolation.** Organization identifiers are derived by trigger, never accepted from callers; cross-organization references are refused; the database test suite in CI checks that row-level security is on for every table and attempts cross-tenant reads and writes across each domain of the schema.
5. **Data minimization and masking.** Column classification (Public, Internal, Confidential, Restricted); masking of Restricted values; watermarking and export controls by classification; external users read only through column-allowlisted views, and pages reached by a link return only named fields.
6. **Logging and monitoring.** Append-only audit ledger retained for 7 years, with the change detail, IP address and user agent redacted after 90 days; error monitoring, built into server code and Edge Functions and active once its production connection is set, that scrubs personal data except a pseudonymous person ID; platform logs with IP addresses and user IDs sent to a log service on the Subprocessor List; anonymous, read-only synthetic checks about every 5 minutes from one US region, with an alert once a required check fails twice in a row on runs at least 4 minutes apart, which reaches a channel once the alert route is connected; written runbooks for alert routing, with one person on call.
7. **Application security.** OWASP ASVS 5.0 Level 2 as the internal verification standard, tracked in a self-assessed matrix that records gaps; static analysis (Semgrep and CodeQL), dependency audit and secret scanning in CI; a dynamic scan against staging when the nightly job can reach it; strict Content Security Policy on Atlas, Gateway and the operator console; SSRF protection; virus scanning of uploads to the stores named in Section 5 of the Security Overview, with files quarantined until clean. Raw inbound email messages are not in those stores.
8. **Availability and resilience.** Daily backups kept 7 days; point-in-time recovery of up to 7 days only where that paid add-on is enabled on the production database; a weekly encrypted logical backup job, designed to keep archives 35 days in separate storage, which has not yet completed a run, and whose archives list stored files but do not contain them; design objectives of a 1 hour recovery point, which depends on point-in-time recovery (daily backups alone give 24 hours), and a 4 hour recovery time, which are not a promise that a given incident will be recovered within them; restore drills on local copies of the database, with a drill against production still to come; 99.9 percent monthly availability target.
9. **Change management.** Pull requests that run CI, as the normal route to the main branch, which repository settings do not yet enforce; signed commits and approval by a second maintainer, which written policy calls for and repository settings do not enforce, and which cannot apply while there is one maintainer; a release runbook that calls for staged rollout of Atlas, with a person checking each stage against the error thresholds of a release gate command and able to abort to the previous deployment. The staged rollout setting is not yet turned on, and the gate does not act by itself; instant rollback of every web application to its previous deployment; feature flags with kill switches.
10. **Incident response.** A written incident response policy with severity levels, roles and a written post-incident review; status page incident notices published by the incident commander with no code change; customer notice of a Personal Data Breach as Section 9 requires.
11. **Vendor management.** A written policy that calls for a security and privacy review of each Subprocessor before use and every year after; written data protection terms as Section 7.4 requires.
12. **Personnel.** Confidentiality as Section 5 requires; written policies that call for background screening where lawful, security and privacy training at hire and every year, and prompt access removal at departure. One person currently holds the Security Lead and Privacy Officer roles.
13. **Data subject rights.** A register for requests for access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection that tracks the 30-day clock and shows legal holds, with self-service account deletion in Gateway and Compass and GHXSTSHIP's help under Section 8.3 for what the Service cannot do.
14. **Deletion.** Automated retention purges of database rows under a per-table schedule, which skip an organization under legal hold; removal of stored files when an organization is purged; legal hold that suspends purge; deletion certificates when an organization is deleted.
