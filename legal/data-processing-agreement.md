# Data Processing Agreement

> **Draft for review by counsel before launch.** This document is a draft. It is not in effect and no one may rely on it. It takes effect only when GHXSTSHIP Industries LLC publishes it at /legal, and its effective date is that date of publication.

This Data Processing Agreement (the "DPA") forms part of the Terms of Service (the "Agreement") between GHXSTSHIP Industries LLC ("GHXSTSHIP" or "Processor") and the Customer named in the Agreement ("Customer" or "Controller"). The Customer's Owner accepts this DPA at signup. It applies whenever GHXSTSHIP processes Customer Personal Data in providing the Service.

## 1. Definitions

- **Data Protection Laws** means all laws on privacy and personal data that apply to a party's processing under the Agreement, including the GDPR (Regulation (EU) 2016/679), the UK GDPR and the UK Data Protection Act 2018, the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (nFADP), the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA ("CCPA"), the other US state comprehensive privacy laws, PIPEDA and Quebec Law 25, the LGPD, the Australian Privacy Act, the APPI, the India Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, the Singapore PDPA, POPIA and the Korean PIPA.
- **Customer Personal Data** means personal data within Customer Data, as defined in the Agreement, that GHXSTSHIP processes on the Customer's behalf.
- **Personal Data Breach** means a breach of security leading to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure of, or access to, Customer Personal Data in GHXSTSHIP's systems or its subprocessors' systems.
- **Subprocessor** means a third party GHXSTSHIP engages that processes Customer Personal Data.
- **EU SCCs** means the standard contractual clauses for the transfer of personal data to third countries adopted by European Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914 of 4 June 2021.
- **UK Addendum** means the International Data Transfer Addendum to the EU Commission Standard Contractual Clauses, version B1.0, issued by the UK Information Commissioner under section 119A of the Data Protection Act 2018 and in force from 21 March 2022, as revised from time to time.
- **Contact Page** means the page at /legal/contact listing GHXSTSHIP's postal address, privacy and security mailboxes, privacy officer and appointed representatives.
- Terms such as "controller", "processor", "data subject", "personal data", "processing" and "supervisory authority" have the meanings Data Protection Laws give them. Under the CCPA, "controller" includes "business" and "processor" includes "service provider".

## 2. Roles and Scope

2.1 The Customer is the controller of Customer Personal Data, and GHXSTSHIP is its processor. Where the Customer is itself a processor for another controller (for example, an agency operating XOS for a client, or a partner organization operating a client organization), GHXSTSHIP is a subprocessor and the Customer warrants that its instructions are authorized by that controller.

2.2 Annex I describes the processing. The subject matter is the provision of the Service. The duration is the term of the Agreement plus the deletion period in Section 10.

2.3 This DPA does not apply to personal data for which GHXSTSHIP is a controller, as described in the Privacy Policy, such as account security data, public marketplace profiles a person chooses to publish, and Customer billing records.

## 3. Customer Instructions

3.1 GHXSTSHIP processes Customer Personal Data only on the Customer's documented instructions. The Agreement, this DPA, the Customer's configuration of the Service, and the actions of its Members and authorized API clients are the Customer's complete instructions. Additional instructions require written agreement.

3.2 GHXSTSHIP will tell the Customer if, in its opinion, an instruction infringes Data Protection Laws, and may suspend the affected processing until the Customer confirms or changes the instruction.

3.3 GHXSTSHIP may process Customer Personal Data otherwise only where the law requires it. In that case GHXSTSHIP will inform the Customer before processing unless the law forbids it.

## 4. CCPA and US State Law Service Provider Terms

GHXSTSHIP will not:

- sell or share Customer Personal Data, as those terms are defined in the CCPA
- retain, use or disclose Customer Personal Data for any purpose other than the business purposes specified in the Agreement, including for any commercial purpose other than providing the Service
- retain, use or disclose Customer Personal Data outside the direct business relationship between the parties
- combine Customer Personal Data with personal data it receives from other sources, except as the CCPA permits for service providers

GHXSTSHIP will comply with the obligations of the CCPA and the other US state privacy laws that apply to processors and service providers, provide the same level of protection those laws require, and notify the Customer if it can no longer meet them. The Customer may take reasonable and appropriate steps to stop and remediate unauthorized use. GHXSTSHIP certifies that it understands these restrictions.

## 5. Confidentiality and Personnel

GHXSTSHIP ensures that everyone authorized to process Customer Personal Data is bound by confidentiality, receives security and privacy training, and has access only as their role requires. GHXSTSHIP personnel never view Customer Data in the Service without a support session granted by the Customer's Owner, which expires within 24 hours and is logged to the Customer's audit ledger.

## 6. Security

6.1 GHXSTSHIP implements and maintains the technical and organizational measures in Annex II. Those measures are designed to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk, taking into account the state of the art, the costs of implementation, and the nature, scope, context and purposes of processing.

6.2 GHXSTSHIP may update the measures in Annex II, provided an update does not materially lower the overall level of protection.

6.3 The Customer is responsible for its own security configuration of the Service, including multi-factor enforcement, role assignment, field classification, IP allow-lists and share-link settings.

## 7. Subprocessors

7.1 **General authorization.** The Customer gives GHXSTSHIP general written authorization to engage the Subprocessors on the Subprocessor List published at /legal/subprocessors.

7.2 **Notice of changes.** GHXSTSHIP will give at least 30 days' notice before a new Subprocessor processes Customer Personal Data, by updating the Subprocessor List and notifying everyone who subscribes to change notifications through the subscription form on that page. The Customer's Owner and Admins are subscribed by default.

7.3 **Objection.** The Customer may object to a new Subprocessor on reasonable data protection grounds within the notice period. The parties will discuss the objection in good faith. If GHXSTSHIP cannot offer a reasonable alternative, the Customer may terminate the affected part of the Service and receive a refund of prepaid fees for the remaining term.

7.4 **Flow-down.** GHXSTSHIP imposes on each Subprocessor, by written contract, data protection obligations no less protective than this DPA, and remains liable to the Customer for each Subprocessor's performance.

## 8. Data Subject Requests

8.1 The Service provides a DSAR workflow that lets the Customer respond to requests for access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, with a 30-day clock, legal hold handling and an evidence record.

8.2 If GHXSTSHIP receives a request about Customer Personal Data, it will route it to the Customer without undue delay and will not answer it except to confirm the routing, unless the Customer authorizes it.

8.3 GHXSTSHIP will give reasonable help with requests the Customer cannot answer through the Service.

## 9. Personal Data Breaches

9.1 GHXSTSHIP will notify the Customer of a Personal Data Breach without undue delay, and in any case within 48 hours after becoming aware of it. Notice goes to the Owner and to the security contacts the Customer sets in Settings.

9.2 The notice will describe, as far as then known: the nature of the breach, the categories and approximate number of data subjects and records, the likely consequences, the measures taken or proposed, and a contact for more information. GHXSTSHIP will provide further information as it becomes available.

9.3 GHXSTSHIP will take reasonable steps to contain, investigate and remediate the breach and to help the Customer meet its own notification duties to authorities and data subjects. Notifying the Customer is not an admission of fault.

## 10. Return and Deletion

10.1 During the term the Customer can export Customer Personal Data at any time through the Service and the public API.

10.2 After the Agreement ends, the Customer's organization is read-only for 30 days, during which a full export is available. On day 90 GHXSTSHIP deletes Customer Personal Data from the live Service, and copies in backups expire on the schedule in the Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery Policy. The Owner receives a deletion certificate.

10.3 GHXSTSHIP may retain Customer Personal Data where the law requires it, and will keep it confidential and process it only for that purpose.

## 11. Audits and Information

11.1 GHXSTSHIP will make available to the Customer the information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA, including this DPA's annexes, its policies, the results of independent security assessments when completed, and, once issued, its SOC 2 Type II report under a confidentiality agreement.

11.2 If that information does not reasonably satisfy a requirement of Data Protection Laws or a supervisory authority's request, the Customer may conduct an audit, or have an independent auditor bound by confidentiality do so, once in any 12-month period, on at least 30 days' written notice, during business hours, at the Customer's cost, and without access to other customers' data. An audit after a Personal Data Breach does not count toward the annual limit.

## 12. Data Protection Impact Assessments

GHXSTSHIP will give reasonable help with the Customer's data protection impact assessments and prior consultations with supervisory authorities about the Service. GHXSTSHIP provides a DPIA template and the processing description in Annex I for this purpose.

## 13. International Transfers

13.1 **Locations.** Customer Personal Data is hosted in the United States by default. On the Enterprise plan the Customer may select the EU region, where all tenant data and backups are held in the European Union. Subprocessors may process Customer Personal Data in the locations shown on the Subprocessor List.

13.2 **EU SCCs.** To the extent the Customer transfers personal data subject to the GDPR to GHXSTSHIP in a country without an adequacy decision, the EU SCCs are incorporated into this DPA by reference and completed as follows:

- Module Two (controller to processor) applies where the Customer is a controller. Module Three (processor to processor) applies where the Customer is a processor.
- Clause 7 (docking clause) applies.
- Clause 9: Option 2 (general written authorization) applies, with the notice period in Section 7.2.
- Clause 11: the optional language on an independent dispute resolution body does not apply.
- Clause 13: the competent supervisory authority is the one determined under Clause 13(a) by the Customer's establishment or, where the Customer has none in the EU, by its representative under Article 27 GDPR.
- Clause 17: the EU SCCs are governed by the law of the EU Member State named as the SCC governing law on the Contact Page.
- Clause 18: disputes are resolved before the courts of that same EU Member State.
- Annex I.A and I.B are completed by Annex I of this DPA. Annex I.C is completed by Section 13.2 Clause 13 above. Annex II is completed by Annex II of this DPA. Annex III is completed by the Subprocessor List.

13.3 **UK Addendum.** To the extent the transfer is subject to the UK GDPR, the UK Addendum is incorporated by reference. Table 1 is completed with the parties' details in Annex I. Table 2 selects the EU SCC modules and options in Section 13.2. Table 3 is completed by Annex I, Annex II and the Subprocessor List. In Table 4, either party may end the UK Addendum as its Section 19 allows.

13.4 **Switzerland.** To the extent the transfer is subject to the nFADP, the EU SCCs apply with these changes: the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner is the competent supervisory authority for such transfers; references to EU Member State law include Swiss law where the nFADP requires; and the term "Member State" does not exclude data subjects in Switzerland from enforcing their rights in their place of habitual residence.

13.5 **Other regimes.** For transfers subject to other Data Protection Laws that require a transfer mechanism, the parties rely on this DPA and the EU SCCs as completed above to the extent that law recognizes them, and will execute any additional terms that law requires.

13.6 **Supplementary measures.** GHXSTSHIP encrypts Customer Personal Data in transit and at rest, encrypts Restricted fields at the field level, minimizes data sent to each Subprocessor, and will challenge any government request for Customer Personal Data that it considers unlawful. GHXSTSHIP will notify the Customer of a government request unless the law forbids it, and publishes the number of such requests it receives each year.

13.7 **Data Privacy Framework.** GHXSTSHIP does not rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework for transfers under this DPA unless it appears on the official Data Privacy Framework List, in which case the Contact Page will say so and the EU SCCs remain in place as a fallback.

## 14. Liability and Precedence

Each party's liability under this DPA is subject to the limits in the Agreement, except where Data Protection Laws or the EU SCCs do not allow a limit. If this DPA conflicts with the Agreement, this DPA controls for personal data. If the EU SCCs or the UK Addendum conflict with this DPA, they control.

**B. Description of transfer**

- **Categories of data subjects:** the Customer's Members; External Parties (clients, vendors, contractors, crew, staff, artists, artist representatives and sponsors) and their personnel; guests, attendees and people on guest lists; people named in incidents, inspections and medical encounters; emergency contacts; and anyone else whose data the Customer enters.
- **Categories of personal data:** identity and contact details; job roles, skills, certifications, training records and qualifications; schedules, shifts, time entries and timesheets; location at clock-in and clock-out against the geofence; photos, voice notes and documents; messages and comments; pay rates, invoices, payments and settlement records; tax forms and tax identifiers; bank and payout details; government identifiers on credentials; background check results and references (never the report itself); emergency contacts; credential and access scans; ratings and references; device and log data.
- **Sensitive data:** medical encounter notes recorded at events, restricted to people holding the Medical capability, encrypted at the field level, excluded from exports and AI features by default. Background check outcomes, which may reveal criminal records data, stored only as provider result and reference. Optional, voluntary equal employment data, stored apart from applications and never shown to selectors. Restrictions: field-level encryption, capability-based access, step-up authentication for export, masking in views and APIs, and audit logging of every access.
- **Frequency:** continuous for the term of the Agreement.
- **Nature of processing:** hosting, storage, retrieval, organization, synchronization to mobile devices, transmission, display, search, export, deletion, notifications by email, SMS and push, document generation, and AI-assisted extraction, drafting and summarization when the Customer turns those features on.
- **Purpose:** to provide the Service under the Agreement.
- **Retention:** for the term of the Agreement and the deletion period in Section 10, subject to the retention settings the Customer configures and the Data Retention and Deletion Policy.
- **Transfers to Subprocessors:** as listed on the Subprocessor List, for the purposes stated there, for the term of the Agreement.

## Annex II: Technical and Organizational Measures

1. **Access control.** Role-based access with capabilities enforced in the database by row-level security on every table; time-boxed memberships and grants; separation of duties rules; quarterly access reviews; break-glass access requiring MFA, a written reason and notice to every Admin.
2. **Authentication.** Passwords checked against breached-password lists; TOTP and passkey MFA, enforceable per role; step-up authentication for billing, data export, role changes and API key creation; SAML SSO and SCIM on the Enterprise plan.
3. **Encryption.** TLS 1.2 or higher with HSTS preloading; AES-256 at rest; field-level pgcrypto encryption for tax identifiers, bank details, government identifiers and medical notes; encrypted on-device storage in Compass with remote wipe.
4. **Tenant isolation.** Organization identifiers are derived by trigger, never accepted from callers; cross-organization references are refused; automated tests attempt cross-tenant reads and writes on every table in every build.
5. **Data minimization and masking.** Column classification (Public, Internal, Confidential, Restricted); masking of Restricted values; watermarking and export controls by classification; external users read only through column-allowlisted views.
6. **Logging and monitoring.** Append-only audit ledger retained for 7 years, with personal data in its text redacted after 90 days; error monitoring with data scrubbing; alerting to an on-call rotation.
7. **Application security.** OWASP ASVS 5.0 Level 2 as the verification standard; static analysis, dependency scanning, secret scanning and dynamic scanning in CI; strict Content Security Policy; SSRF protection; virus scanning of uploads.
8. **Availability and resilience.** Point-in-time recovery with at least 7 days of history; weekly logical backups to separate storage; 1 hour recovery point objective and 4 hour recovery time objective, verified by restore drills; 99.9 percent monthly availability target.
9. **Change management.** Peer-reviewed pull requests; signed commits on the main branch; progressive rollout with automatic rollback; feature flags with kill switches.
10. **Incident response.** A documented incident response plan with severity levels, roles, customer notice within 48 hours of awareness of a Personal Data Breach, and post-incident review.
11. **Vendor management.** Security and privacy review of each Subprocessor before onboarding and annually after, with written data protection terms.
12. **Personnel.** Confidentiality undertakings, background screening where lawful, security and privacy training at hire and annually, and prompt access removal at departure.
13. **Data subject rights.** A DSAR workflow for access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, honoring legal holds and retention.
14. **Deletion.** Automated retention purges per table, legal hold that suspends purge, and deletion certificates at account closure.
