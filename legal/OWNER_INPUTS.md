# Owner Inputs for Legal Documents

The drafts in `legal/` are complete, but some facts can only come from the Owner. No draft invents them. Instead, each document refers to them through a defined term that points to the published Contact page at /legal/contact (`legal/contact.md`), for example "the postal address published on our Contact page". Filling the items below on the Contact page completes every document at once. Wave 2 must not publish /legal until Part A is filled.
