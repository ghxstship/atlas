# Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

> **Draft before launch.** This document is a draft. It is not in effect and no one may rely on it. Showing it at /legal does not put it in effect. It takes effect only on the effective date that GHXSTSHIP Industries LLC states at the top of a final version.

This policy explains how GHXSTSHIP Industries LLC ("GHXSTSHIP", "we" or "us") handles health-related data in XOS 4.0. It has its own page so that the people it covers can find it directly. It adds to our Privacy Policy, which still applies, and it applies wherever you live.

## 1. What This Policy Covers

In this policy, "health data" means:

- allergies
- dietary needs and notes, which can reveal a health condition or religion
- accessibility needs and notes, which can reveal a disability or health condition
- a meal preference given for a project
- dietary and accessibility needs that someone acting for an organization enters for a guest

Organizations that use XOS can also keep medical encounter and injury records about people at their events. Those records are the organization's, held for it under our Data Processing Agreement, and the organization's own privacy notice governs them.

## 2. Who Decides How It Is Used

- **Data you keep in your own account.** When you enter health data in your Work Details, we keep it for you, and we decide how it is handled until you share it.
- **Data you share with an organization.** When you share a category with an organization for an engagement or a project, that organization decides how it uses what it receives, and we process it for the organization.
- **Data an organization enters about someone else.** When someone acting for an organization enters a guest's needs or a rostered person's meal preference, the organization decides how it is used, and we process it for the organization.

## 3. How We Collect It

- **From you, with your consent.** Before you first enter health data in a category, XOS asks for your consent for that category and records it with the version of the words you saw, the language, the app, you, the time and your IP address. Sharing a category with an organization is a further consent, recorded each time.
- **From someone else, with a confirmation.** Someone may enter a guest's needs, or a rostered person's meal preference, only after confirming that they have that person's authority and that the person has had notice. Before they confirm, they are shown what is entered, why, and who reads it. When a verified sign-in matches the entry, including one opened later, we tell that person, who sees the entry and can remove it. A guest with no account or no email gets no notice from us, so for that guest this rests on the confirmation of the person who entered it.
- **Not from anywhere else.** We do not buy health data, collect it from other sources or infer it from other data.

## 4. Why It Is Used

Health data is used only to keep it for you and to show it to the people who need it for catering, safety and access at the work or event it was shared for. It is not used for advertising, it is not sold, it is not used to decide whom to engage, and AI features do not read it.

## 5. Who Can Read It

- You.
- On a project you share a category with, the organization's Owner and the catering lead and on-site medical roles on that project. Every read is logged.
- Hospitality readers see these values locked, and catering planners see counts of five or more with no names.
- A company that rosters you, such as an agency or vendor, cannot read them, including a meal preference it entered for you.
- Our personnel open them only in a support session the organization grants, or in the other cases Section 5 of our Data Processing Agreement lists.
- The providers that host the Service for us, as our Subprocessor List names them, and authorities where the law compels it.

## 6. Your Choices and Rights

- **Withdraw your consent** in one step under Work Details. Use stops at once, the values are cleared, the category leaves every share, and what others entered about you is cleared, including guest list entries matched only by your verified email. If an organization has a legal hold over those records, the values stay hidden from everyone until the hold ends, and are then cleared. Someone else cannot enter that category for you again after you withdraw.
- **See, correct or delete** your own health data at any time in Work Details, or delete your account.
- **Ask us** for a copy of your health data, a list of the organizations and providers we have shared it with, or anything else about it, through the privacy mailbox on our contact page at /legal/contact. We answer within 30 days. If we refuse, we tell you why and how to appeal, and we answer an appeal within 45 days.
- **No penalty.** We do not treat you differently for using these rights.

Data an organization holds about you is the organization's to answer for, so ask it first. If you ask us, we pass your request to it and help it answer.

## 7. How Long It Is Kept

- Your own health data stays until you remove it, withdraw consent or delete your account.
- Values entered before consent was recorded are hidden from everyone but you. We tell you once, and they are deleted 30 days after that notice unless you agree to keep them.
- Guest needs are deleted when the guest is removed or declined, and at most 90 days after the project ends.
- Consent records are kept 7 years after you withdraw, and the records of who entered, read or cleared a value 7 years after it is cleared, as evidence.

Our Data Retention and Deletion Policy has the full schedule.

## 8. Sale and Sharing

We do not sell health data, and we do not share it with anyone for their advertising or marketing. We share it only with the people in Section 5, as you direct, or where the law compels it.

## 9. Security

Health data is held in Restricted fields that cannot be selected directly. Every read is logged, and standard exports refuse them. Our Security Overview describes the rest of our measures.

## 10. Changes and Contact

Each version of this policy is shown at /legal/consumer-health-data-privacy-policy with its version number, and it is in effect from the effective date it states. Questions go to the privacy mailbox on our contact page at /legal/contact.
