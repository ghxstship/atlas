# Copyright and DMCA Policy

> **Draft for review by counsel before launch.** This document is a draft. It is not in effect and no one may rely on it. It takes effect only when GHXSTSHIP Industries LLC publishes it at /legal, and its effective date is that date of publication.

GHXSTSHIP Industries LLC ("GHXSTSHIP") respects intellectual property rights and expects everyone who uses XOS 4.0 to do the same. This policy explains how we respond to claims of copyright infringement under the US Digital Millennium Copyright Act ("DMCA"), 17 U.S.C. § 512, and how we handle notices about illegal content under the EU Digital Services Act.

## 1. Designated Agent

Our designated agent for notices of claimed infringement is the person named as DMCA agent on our contact page at /legal/contact, at the postal address and copyright mailbox listed there. The same details are on file with the US Copyright Office's DMCA Designated Agent Directory.

## 2. Filing a Notice of Infringement

If you believe content in XOS infringes your copyright, send our designated agent a written notice that includes:

1. your physical or electronic signature, as the copyright owner or a person authorized to act for the owner
2. identification of the copyrighted work you claim is infringed, or a representative list if several works are covered by one notice
3. identification of the material you claim is infringing and information reasonably sufficient for us to locate it, such as the URL of the opportunity, profile, portfolio item or document
4. your name, postal address, telephone number and email address
5. a statement that you have a good faith belief that the use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent or the law
6. a statement that the information in the notice is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are the owner or are authorized to act on the owner's behalf

A notice that does not substantially include these elements may not be acted on. Knowingly misrepresenting that material is infringing can make you liable for damages under 17 U.S.C. § 512(f).

## 3. What We Do

When we receive a valid notice we act expeditiously to remove or disable access to the material, notify the user who posted it, and give them a copy of the notice with personal contact details of the notifier removed where the law allows. Where the content sits in a Customer's private workspace, we also notify the Customer's Owner, who controls that workspace.

## 4. Counter-Notice

If you believe material was removed by mistake or misidentification, you may send our designated agent a counter-notice that includes:

1. your physical or electronic signature
2. identification of the material removed and where it appeared before removal
3. a statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good faith belief the material was removed as a result of mistake or misidentification
4. your name, address and telephone number, and a statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the federal district court for the judicial district of your address (or, if your address is outside the United States, any judicial district in which GHXSTSHIP may be found), and that you will accept service of process from the person who filed the original notice or that person's agent

On receiving a valid counter-notice we send a copy to the original notifier. Unless the notifier tells us within 10 business days that they have filed an action seeking a court order, we restore the material between 10 and 14 business days after receiving the counter-notice.

## 5. Repeat Infringers

We terminate, in appropriate circumstances, the accounts of users who are repeat infringers. We consider a user a repeat infringer if they are the subject of more than two valid, uncontested notices in any 12-month period, and we may act sooner for egregious infringement.

## 6. EU Digital Services Act Notices

People in the European Union may report any content they consider illegal, including infringing content, through the Report action on any listing or profile or through the abuse mailbox on our contact page. We confirm receipt, decide in a timely, diligent and objective manner, tell the notifier of our decision, and send the affected user a statement of reasons with information on how to appeal through our internal complaint process.

Code in our public repository is licensed under the AGPL-3.0 or MIT licenses, and canon data under CC BY 4.0, as described on our Open Source Licenses page. Use that follows those licenses is not infringement. Use of our names and logos is governed by our Trademark Policy.
