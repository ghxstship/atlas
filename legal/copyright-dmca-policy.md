# Copyright and DMCA Policy

> **Draft before launch.** This document is a draft. It is not in effect and no one may rely on it. Showing it at /legal does not put it in effect. It takes effect only on the effective date that GHXSTSHIP Industries LLC states at the top of a final version.

GHXSTSHIP Industries LLC ("GHXSTSHIP") respects intellectual property rights and expects everyone who uses XOS 4.0 to do the same. This policy explains how we respond to claims of copyright infringement under the US Digital Millennium Copyright Act ("DMCA"), 17 U.S.C. § 512, and how we handle notices about illegal content under the EU Digital Services Act. It covers XOS and its parts, Atlas, Gateway and Compass, and the websites on ghxstship.tech.

## 1. Designated Agent

Our designated agent for notices of claimed infringement is the person named as DMCA designated agent on our contact page at /legal/contact, at the postal address, telephone number and email address listed there (support@ghxstship.pro, with "Copyright" in the subject line). The contact page also states whether that designation is registered in the US Copyright Office's DMCA Designated Agent Directory at copyright.gov/dmca-directory.

Send only copyright notices and counter-notices to the agent. Every other request goes to the same address with its own purpose in the subject line, as the contact page lists.

## 2. Filing a Notice of Infringement

If you believe content in XOS infringes your copyright, send our designated agent a written notice that includes:

1. your physical or electronic signature, as the owner of the exclusive right you say is infringed or a person authorized to act for that owner
2. identification of the copyrighted work you claim is infringed, or a representative list if several works are covered by one notice
3. identification of the material you claim is infringing and information reasonably sufficient for us to locate it, such as the URL of the opportunity, profile, portfolio item or document
4. your name, postal address, telephone number and email address
5. a statement that you have a good faith belief that the use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent or the law
6. a statement that the information in the notice is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are the owner or are authorized to act on the owner's behalf

Before you send a notice, consider whether the law allows the use, for example as fair use. If your notice substantially covers items 2, 3 and 4 but misses something else, we promptly try to contact you, or take other reasonable steps, so that you can complete it. We may not act on a notice that does not substantially include these elements. Knowingly and materially misrepresenting that material is infringing can make you liable for damages, costs and attorneys' fees under 17 U.S.C. § 512(f).

## 3. What We Do

A person reviews each notice. We do not use automated means to decide one, and we do not search Customers' workspaces for infringement on our own initiative. When we receive a valid notice we act expeditiously to remove or disable access to the material, promptly take reasonable steps to notify the user who posted it, and give them a copy of the notice with personal contact details of the notifier removed where the law allows. Where the content sits in a Customer's private workspace, we also notify the Customer's Owner, who controls that workspace.

## 4. Counter-Notice

If you believe material was removed by mistake or misidentification, you may send our designated agent a counter-notice that includes:

1. your physical or electronic signature
2. identification of the material removed and where it appeared before removal
3. a statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good faith belief the material was removed as a result of mistake or misidentification
4. your name, address and telephone number, and a statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the federal district court for the judicial district of your address (or, if your address is outside the United States, any judicial district in which GHXSTSHIP may be found), and that you will accept service of process from the person who filed the original notice or that person's agent

The consent in item 4 is the one the statute requires, and it applies whatever a forum clause in our other terms says. Section 512(f) also applies to a counter-notice that knowingly and materially misrepresents that material was removed by mistake or misidentification.

On receiving a valid counter-notice we promptly send a copy to the original notifier and tell them we will restore the material in 10 business days. We restore it between 10 and 14 business days after receiving the counter-notice, unless our designated agent first receives notice that the notifier has filed an action seeking a court order to stop the user's activity on XOS. Business days are Monday to Friday, except US federal holidays, in New York time.

## 5. Repeat Infringers

We terminate, in appropriate circumstances, the accounts of users who are repeat infringers. We consider a user a repeat infringer if they are the subject of more than two valid, uncontested notices in any 12-month period, and we may act sooner for egregious infringement. A notice that was withdrawn, or that a valid counter-notice answered without a court action following, does not count.

## 6. EU Digital Services Act Notices

People in the European Union may report any content they consider illegal, including infringing content:

- through support@ghxstship.pro with "Abuse Report" in the subject line, which anyone can use without an account
- through the Report action on a Gateway marketplace listing, if you are signed in

The Report action on a chat message, post or comment goes to the moderators of the organization that runs that workspace, not to us. To reach GHXSTSHIP about that content, use support@ghxstship.pro with "Abuse Report" in the subject line.

Your notice should give:

1. the reasons you consider the content illegal
2. its exact location, such as the URL
3. your name and email address, unless the notice concerns one of the child sexual abuse offences for which the Digital Services Act does not require them
4. a statement that you believe in good faith that the notice is accurate and complete

We confirm receipt, decide in a timely, diligent and objective manner, and tell the notifier of our decision and the ways to challenge it. A person decides each notice, and we do not use automated means to detect the content or decide. If we restrict the content, we send the affected user a statement of reasons that gives the restriction, the facts, the rule or law relied on, whether automated means were used, and the redress available: our internal complaint process, out-of-court dispute settlement where it is available, and the courts. A decision can be appealed within six months as Section 7 of the Acceptable Use Policy describes.

## 7. Open Source and Canon Data

Code in our public repository is licensed under the AGPL-3.0 or MIT licenses, and canon data under CC BY 4.0, as described on our Open Source Licenses page. Use that follows those licenses is not infringement. Use of our names and logos is governed by our Trademark Policy, and reports about them go through Section 7 of that policy.

## 8. Other Illegal or Harmful Content

Anyone, in any country and with or without an account, may use support@ghxstship.pro with "Abuse Report" in the subject line to report other content that is illegal or harmful in XOS. Say where the content is and why you are reporting it. For an emergency, call your local emergency number first.

You can also report an intimate image of a person, real or digitally made, shared without that person's consent. You do not need an account to report one, and you do not need to attach the image. So that we can find it and reach you, please send:

1. your physical or electronic signature, or that of someone authorized to act for you
2. a description of the image and where it is, such as the URL or the profile it appears on
3. a short statement that you believe in good faith the image was shared without consent, with anything that supports this
4. how we can reach you, or the person acting for you

A person reads each report.

## 9. Your Information

We use the information in a notice or counter-notice to handle it, to keep a record of it, and to apply Section 5. We may share it with the people this policy names, and with others where the law requires. The Privacy Policy explains how we handle personal data.
