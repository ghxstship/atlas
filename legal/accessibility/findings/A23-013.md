# A23-013: Gateway settings selects overflow at phone width

## Severity rationale

Moderate. At 375 px, `/settings` and `/settings/profile` scroll horizontally (388 px of content in
375 px), and it is worse at 320 px. The Skill select takes the width of its longest option
("Production Leadership · 0000.01.01..."), pushing the form past the viewport. The rest of the page
reflows.

## Location

- `apps/gateway/components/forms/simple-form.tsx:124` (select in `SimpleForm`), used by the Add skill
  form at `apps/gateway/components/settings/sections.tsx:238`.
- `apps/gateway/components/settings/personal-setting.tsx:55` has the same select classes.

## Reproduction

Sign in to Gateway as `crew@example.com` at 375 by 812 px and open `/settings/profile`.
`document.documentElement.scrollWidth` is 388 while `clientWidth` is 375.

## Suggested fix

Add `w-full min-w-0 max-w-full` to the selects (and `min-w-0` on their flex labels), so long options
are truncated by the control instead of widening the page.

## Resolution

- Fixed in: b0dcab6 (A35).
- Verified by: Gateway sweep overflow check at 375 px on settings and settings.profile. A35 re-ran both sweeps on a fresh `supabase db reset` (2026-10-16): no longer reproduces.
