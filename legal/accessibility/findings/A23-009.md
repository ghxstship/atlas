# A23-009: The run of show countdown floods TalkBack

## Severity rationale

Moderate. On Android the next cue block is a polite live region, and its countdown re-renders every
500 ms (`setInterval(..., 500)`). TalkBack queues an announcement for each change, so a stage
manager hears a continuous stream of numbers and cannot use anything else on the screen. VoiceOver
is unaffected (see A23-004 for the opposite problem there).

## Location

- `apps/compass/src/ui/compass.tsx:406` (`RunOfShowLive` next block, `accessibilityLiveRegion="polite"`,
  wrapping the countdown text at `:413`).
- `apps/compass/src/screens/ShowView.tsx:40` (500 ms tick).

## Reproduction

Android with TalkBack: open Run of show while a cue is live. TalkBack keeps reading the next cue
title and countdown without pause.

## Suggested fix

Keep the live region on the cue title only, and announce the countdown at set points (for example
60, 30 and 10 seconds, and "Go"). Give the countdown text an `accessibilityLabel` that reads as
"2 minutes 10 seconds to Doors" when focused.
