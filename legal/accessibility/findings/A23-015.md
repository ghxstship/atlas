# A23-015: Timeline scroll areas are not keyboard scrollable

## Severity rationale

Serious. A timeline is two-dimensional, so scrolling it sideways at 375 px is allowed, but the scroll
container holds no focusable element and is not focusable itself. A keyboard user cannot reach the
later dates at all. axe reports `scrollable-region-focusable` (serious) on every Owner, Manager and
Field run. A screen reader user still gets the visually hidden table inside the figure, which is
why this is not Critical.

## Location

- `apps/atlas/components/work/record-collection.tsx:381` (Timeline view of records: Schedule
  timeline and the project Schedule tab).
- `apps/atlas/components/projects/project-collection.tsx:451` (portfolio timeline).

## Reproduction

1. Sign in to Atlas as `owner@example.com` at 375 by 812 px.
2. Open `/demo-productions/schedule/timeline`. The timeline is wider than the screen.
3. Press Tab through the page: no stop lands in the timeline, and arrow keys do not scroll it.

## Suggested fix

Give each scroll container `tabIndex={0}`, `role="region"` and an `aria-label` (the existing
`scroll.region` string, "Scrollable content", or the view name), with a visible focus ring, or use
the design system `ScrollRegion` component, which does this already.
