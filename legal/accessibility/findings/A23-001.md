# A23-001: The sticky bottom tab bar hides the focused control in Gateway

## Severity rationale

Serious. On phones and tablets used with a keyboard or switch access, the control that has focus
sits fully under the five-destination bar, so the person cannot see where they are. It hits every
long form on a phone, including Save on every settings page. axe reports the same overlap as a
serious target-size violation, which gate 8 does not allow.

## Location

- `packages/ui/src/Layout/Layout.tsx:134` and `packages/ui/src/Templates/Templates.tsx:316`: the
  bar is `sticky bottom-0`, and nothing reserves its height in the scroll area
  (`scroll-padding-bottom` is `auto`).
- Seen on `/settings/out-of-office` (Save), `/settings/profile` (the About textarea, Save, Remove).

## Reproduction

1. Sign in to Gateway as `crew@example.com` at 375 by 812 px (touch emulation on).
2. Open `/settings/out-of-office` and press Tab until Save in the "Away from" form has focus.
3. Save is fully covered by the bottom tab bar; its focus ring is not visible.
4. The sweep reports `axe target-size (serious) ... partially obscured (45.7 by 11.8 px)`.

## Suggested fix

Give the scroll container a `scroll-padding-bottom` equal to the bar height plus the safe-area
inset (a token such as `--ui-tabbar-height`), and the same bottom padding on the content, so the
browser scrolls a focused control clear of the bar.
