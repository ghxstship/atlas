# A23-002: Tip and tour cards cover the Atlas phone bottom bar

## Severity rationale

Serious. At 375 px the tip card is fixed at the bottom of the viewport, above the bottom bar, and
covers all five destinations (Home, Inbox, Search, My Work, Menu). A keyboard user who tabs into the
bottom bar has focus fully hidden. Escape does not close the card, so the only way out is to find
and press Dismiss tip.

## Location

- `apps/atlas/components/help/help-center.tsx:439` (tip `Card`) and `:492` (tour card): both are
  `fixed bottom-16 end-16 z-toast` with no offset for the phone bottom bar.
- Seen on `/demo-productions/work/all-records` and other list pages that show a tip.

## Reproduction

1. Sign in to Atlas as `owner@example.com` at 375 by 812 px.
2. Open `/demo-productions/work/all-records`. The "Save the view you need" tip appears.
3. Press Tab to the bottom bar. Home, Inbox, Search, My Work and Menu each take focus under the card.
4. Press Escape. The card stays.

## Suggested fix

At phone width, place the card above the bottom bar (offset by the bar height and safe area), or
render tips inline in the page. Close the card on Escape, and return focus to the element that had
it before.

## Resolution

- Fixed in: 54cf675 (orchestrator).
- Verified by: Scroll padding above the phone tab bar; Atlas sweep focus checks at 375 px. A35 re-ran both sweeps on a fresh `supabase db reset` (2026-10-16): no longer reproduces.
