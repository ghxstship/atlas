# A23-010: Kiosk PIN progress and banner dismiss labels

## Severity rationale

Minor. Two small gaps with workarounds:

1. The kiosk PIN display is the string of "•" characters, so VoiceOver reads "bullet bullet
   bullet" and gives no total. A person can count, but cannot tell how many digits remain.
2. `Banner` renders its dismiss button with `label={dismissLabel ?? ""}`, so a banner given
   `onDismiss` without `dismissLabel` has an unnamed button.

## Location

- `apps/compass/app/kiosk.tsx:88` (PIN dots).
- `apps/compass/src/ui/layout.tsx:356` (`Banner` dismiss).

## Reproduction

1. Open Kiosk, enter two digits, and focus the PIN display with VoiceOver: "bullet bullet".
2. Source: `dismissLabel` is optional in `BannerProps` while the button label falls back to "".

## Suggested fix

1. Give the PIN display `accessibilityLabel` such as "2 of 4 digits entered" (an i18n string) and
   hide the dots from assistive technology.
2. Make `dismissLabel` required whenever `onDismiss` is set (a union type), and drop the "" fallback.

## Resolution

- Fixed in: 684526f (A35).
- Verified by: apps/compass/test/a23-moderate.test.ts (A23-010 block): the kiosk PIN reads as a digit count; BannerProps requires dismissLabel with onDismiss.
