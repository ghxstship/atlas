# A23-003: Focus is not returned when the command menu or side peek closes

## Severity rationale

Serious. Opening a record in the side peek and the command menu are the two most frequent keyboard
actions in Atlas (Section 11.5: Record side peek, Command menu). Each time either closes, focus lands
on `<body>`, so the next Tab starts again at the top of the sidebar, about 15 stops from where the
person was. Screen reader users lose their place in the records list on every peek.

## Location

- `packages/ui/src/CommandMenu/CommandMenu.tsx:138` and `packages/ui/src/Overlays/Overlays.tsx:229`
  (`SidePeek`): both run as controlled dialogs (`open` prop, no `RDialog.Trigger`) and set no
  `onCloseAutoFocus`, so focus return depends on the element focused at mount still being in the
  DOM.
- `apps/atlas/components/shell/shell-frame.tsx` (command menu state) and
  `apps/atlas/components/work/record-peek.tsx` (peek driven by `?peek=`): the URL change re-renders
  the list behind the peek.

## Reproduction

1. Sign in to Atlas as `owner@example.com` at 1280 px and open `/demo-productions/home`.
2. Tab to Search and press Enter (or press Cmd+K). Press Escape. `document.activeElement` is `body`.
3. Open `/demo-productions/work/all-records`, focus the records grid, press ArrowDown and Enter.
   The peek opens with focus on "Resize panel" and Tab stays inside it (good).
4. Press Escape. The peek closes and focus is on `body`, not on the row that opened it.
5. In Gateway as `crew@example.com` on `/home`, focus "Command menu" and press Enter, then Escape:
   focus is on `body`. The same happens with "Account menu for Demo Crew Member" (a dialog).
6. In Atlas on `/demo-productions/work/all-records`, focus "New record" and press Enter. The dialog
   (`?create=1`) opens with focus on Title and keeps Tab inside. Press Escape: focus is on `body`.

The pattern is the same in both shells, so the fix belongs in the shared overlay components.

## Suggested fix

Record the opener (`document.activeElement`, or the row key for the peek) when opening, and in
`onCloseAutoFocus` call `preventDefault()` and focus that element, falling back to the row with the
same key after the list re-renders. Start focus in the peek on its title or Close button rather than
the resize handle.

## Resolution

- Fixed in: 54cf675 (orchestrator).
- Verified by: useReturnFocus (packages/ui lib/hooks.ts) and its tests in packages/ui/test.
