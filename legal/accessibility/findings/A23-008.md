# A23-008: Compass does not honour Reduce Motion

## Severity rationale

Moderate. Section 14.1 requires reduced motion on mobile, and gate 22 asks for motion-free Maestro
runs. Compass never reads the setting: `AccessibilityInfo.isReduceMotionEnabled` and
`useReducedMotion` appear nowhere. Every sheet (quick incident, sync conflict, photo) slides up, and
the hold-to-confirm fill animates regardless of the setting.

## Location

- `apps/compass/src/ui/layout.tsx:523` (`Sheet`): `<Modal animationType="slide">`.
- `apps/compass/src/ui/primitives.tsx:154` (`Button` hold to confirm): `Animated.timing` with no
  reduced-motion branch.

## Reproduction

Turn on Reduce Motion (iOS) or Remove animations (Android), open Today, then Quick incident. The
sheet still slides in.

## Suggested fix

Read the setting once in the provider (`AccessibilityInfo.isReduceMotionEnabled` plus its change
event) and expose it through the theme. Use `animationType="fade"` or `"none"` for sheets when it
is on, and replace the hold fill with a stepped progress or a numeric countdown.
