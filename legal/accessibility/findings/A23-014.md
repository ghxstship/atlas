# A23-014: Toasts may not be announced

## Severity rationale

Moderate. `Toast` and `UndoToast` carry `role="status"`, but pages render the `ToastRegion` and the
toast at the same moment (`{undo ? <ToastRegion><UndoToast/></ToastRegion> : null}`). A live region
must be in the DOM before its content changes; one inserted with its content is announced
inconsistently (often silently in VoiceOver with Safari, and sometimes in NVDA). Moving a record
to trash then gives no spoken confirmation and no spoken Undo offer, though the 8 second Undo window
is the only way to reverse it without a trip to Trash.

## Location

- `packages/ui/src/Feedback/Feedback.tsx:271` (`ToastRegion`, a plain section) and `:196`, `:254`.
- `apps/atlas/components/work/record-panels.tsx:944`, also `finance/budget-editor.tsx` and
  `logistics-hospitality/client.tsx`: the region mounts only when a toast exists.

## Reproduction

Source audit. With VoiceOver and Safari, open a record and choose Move to Trash: the toast appears
and VoiceOver says nothing (to confirm on hardware; see the ACR list of pending screen reader passes).

## Suggested fix

Mount one `ToastRegion` in the shell for the life of the page, give it `role="status"`
(`aria-live="polite"`), and render toasts into it, dropping the per-toast roles. Keep keyboard
access to Undo with the existing Cmd or Ctrl+Z shortcut, and announce it in the toast text.

## Resolution

- Fixed in: 5ea245e (A35).
- Verified by: packages/ui/test/foundations.test.tsx: ToastRegion is the polite live region before a toast arrives, and a toast inside it carries no second status role. The VoiceOver with Safari and NVDA confirmation stays on the list of passes owed.
