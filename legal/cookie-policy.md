# Cookie Policy

> **Draft for review by counsel before launch.** This document is a draft. It is not in effect and no one may rely on it. It takes effect only when GHXSTSHIP Industries LLC publishes it at /legal, and its effective date is that date of publication.

This Cookie Policy explains how GHXSTSHIP Industries LLC ("GHXSTSHIP", "we" or "us") uses cookies and similar technologies on Atlas, Gateway, the documentation site and our other websites. It should be read with our Privacy Policy.

## 1. What Cookies Are

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies include local storage, session storage and IndexedDB. This policy uses "cookies" for all of them.

## 2. Our Approach

- **Prior blocking.** Only strictly necessary cookies load before you choose. Every other category stays blocked until you consent to it.
- **Granular choice.** The consent banner offers each category separately, with Accept All, Reject All and a choice per category given equal prominence.
- **Server-side record.** Your decision is stored on our servers in a consent log, not only in your browser, so it applies when you sign in on another device and so we can show what you chose and when.
- **Global Privacy Control.** We treat a Global Privacy Control signal as a refusal of every non-essential category for that browser.
- **No tracking.** We do not use advertising cookies, cross-site tracking or fingerprinting, and we do not sell or share data collected through cookies.
- **White-label domains.** On a Customer's custom domain, the same rules apply. The Customer may not add tracking to Atlas or Gateway through XOS.

## 3. Categories

| Category           | Purpose                                                                                                                                                              | Consent needed | Typical duration                                              |
| ------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Strictly necessary | Keep you signed in (Supabase Auth session tokens), protect forms against cross-site request forgery, balance load, remember your consent choice, enforce rate limits | No             | Session, or up to the session lifetime your organization sets |
| Preferences        | Remember your language, theme, sidebar layout, table views and similar settings when you are signed out                                                              | Yes            | Up to 12 months                                               |
| Performance        | Measure page load and interaction timing (Web Vitals) and record error reports, without identifying you                                                              | Yes            | Up to 12 months                                               |

We do not set marketing cookies. If we ever add a cookie, we list it by name in the consent banner's cookie table before it is used, and a new category always starts blocked.

## 4. Mobile App

Compass does not use cookies for tracking and does not track you across apps or websites, so it does not show an App Tracking Transparency prompt. Compass stores data on your device in an encrypted local database so it works offline. Signing out remotely from Atlas wipes that store.

## 5. Managing Your Choice

You can change your choice at any time through the Cookie Settings link in the footer of every page, or in Settings, Privacy when signed in. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block strictly necessary cookies, sign-in will not work.

## 6. Consent Records

Each consent decision records the categories accepted or refused, the policy version, the time, and the browser signal (such as Global Privacy Control) that applied. We keep consent records for as long as we need to prove the consent, as stated in our Data Retention and Deletion Policy.

## 7. Changes and Contact

We publish every version of this policy at /legal. If we add a category, we ask for your consent again. Questions go to the privacy mailbox listed on our contact page at /legal/contact.
