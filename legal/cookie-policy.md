# Cookie Policy

> **Draft before launch.** This document is a draft. It is not in effect and no one may rely on it. Showing it at /legal does not put it in effect. It takes effect only on the effective date that GHXSTSHIP Industries LLC states at the top of a final version.

This Cookie Policy explains how GHXSTSHIP Industries LLC ("GHXSTSHIP", "we" or "us") uses cookies and similar technologies on Atlas, Gateway and the documentation site. Compass is covered in Section 4. Our operator console is for GHXSTSHIP staff only and has no consent banner. This policy should be read with our Privacy Policy.

## 1. What Cookies Are

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies include local storage, session storage, the browser's Cache Storage and a service worker that uses it. This policy uses "cookies" for all of them. We use no IndexedDB in the web apps.

## 2. Our Approach

- **What runs before you choose.** Cookies needed to run the Service load straight away. Analytics stay off until you consent to Performance. Your language, theme and last workspace are also set before you choose, but only as session cookies that end with your visit, and they last 12 months only if you consent to Preferences. Section 3 lists three items that do not follow this rule today: `xos-prefs`, `xos.system-banners.dismissed` and the page caches.
- **Granular choice.** The consent banner on Atlas and Gateway offers each category separately, with Accept All, Reject All and a choice per category given equal prominence.
- **Your choice and the record of it.** Your choice is kept in a cookie in that browser for 12 months, so it applies only to that browser. We also record the decision on our servers as evidence. We do not read that record back to set your choice, so it does not follow you when you sign in on another device.
- **Global Privacy Control.** On Atlas and Gateway we treat a Global Privacy Control signal as a refusal of every non-essential category for that browser. The banner does not ask, and we record the signal.
- **No advertising or cross-site tracking.** We do not use advertising cookies, cross-site tracking or fingerprinting, and we do not sell or share data collected through cookies. Atlas and Gateway load no third-party scripts, fonts or images, and their security policy blocks them.
- **White-label domains.** On a Customer's custom domain, the same rules apply. The Customer may not add tracking to Atlas or Gateway through XOS.
- **Documentation site.** It has no consent banner and does not act on Global Privacy Control, because it runs no analytics and sets only the two items in Section 3.

The cookie tables on the Atlas and Gateway cookie settings pages are shorter than this one. They leave out items such as `xos-prefs`, and they show the local development name of the sign-in cookie. The table on this page is the complete one.

Features added in a later release can add a grant cookie of the same kind as `xos_link_grant`, limited to one link's path, that lasts from hours to days. We add each one to this table when it is released, and a new non-essential category always starts blocked. Our hosting provider can also set its own cookie for security or routing. We list any we confirm.

**Error reports are not cookies.** When something fails in your browser, Atlas and Gateway send the error message, stack trace, route template, release and, when you are signed in, your user ID to our own server, which passes the report to Sentry, our error monitoring provider. This happens whatever you chose in the banner, because it stores nothing on your device and we rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the Service working (Privacy Policy, Section 14).

## 4. Mobile App

Compass does not use cookies for tracking and does not track you across apps or websites, so it does not show an App Tracking Transparency prompt. It has no advertising identifier and no analytics or crash reporting library. Compass stores data on your device so it works offline: an encrypted local database for records, the device's secure storage for sign-in and settings, and app group storage outside the encrypted database for the clock widget and Live Activity. Signing out remotely from Atlas wipes the offline records the next time the app learns of it, so a device that stays offline keeps them until then.

## 5. Managing Your Choice

You can change your choice at any time at /legal/cookie-settings on Atlas or Gateway. The banner links to that page while it is showing, and neither app has a permanent link to it yet, so once you have chosen you need this address. Changing your choice does not shorten `xos-prefs` or remove the other items Section 2 names. You can also delete the `xos-consent` cookie in your browser settings, and the banner asks you again on your next visit. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block strictly necessary cookies, sign-in will not work.

## 6. Consent Records

Each consent decision on Atlas and Gateway records a hash of the random visitor ID, the categories accepted or refused, the banner version, the time, and the browser signal (such as Global Privacy Control) that applied. We keep cookie consent records and privacy signals for 25 months, as stated in our Data Retention and Deletion Policy.
