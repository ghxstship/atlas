# ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Statement of Applicability

> **Draft for review by counsel before launch.** This Statement of Applicability is a draft. It takes effect when the Owner approves it, and its effective date is that date of approval. GHXSTSHIP Industries LLC does not hold ISO/IEC 27001 certification.

## 1. Scope of the ISMS

The information security management system of GHXSTSHIP Industries LLC covers the design, development, operation and support of the XOS 4.0 platform (Atlas, Gateway, Compass, the public API, SDKs, CLI and MCP server), the Customer Data it processes, the source repository and CI, and the personnel and vendors involved. Physical controls for production are inherited from infrastructure subprocessors and verified through their independent reports.

## 2. How to Read This Statement

Each Annex A control lists whether it applies, the justification and how it is implemented, the owning agent in the XOS build, and its state: **Drafted** where the governing document exists in `legal/`, or **Planned** with the build wave in which the implementation and its evidence land. Control titles are the short identifying titles of ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A; the standard's control text is not reproduced.

Summary: 93 controls, 92 applicable, 1 excluded. States: Drafted 54, Planned (Wave 2) 16, Planned (Wave 5) 12, Planned (Wave 6) 4, Planned (Wave 7) 5, Planned (Wave 4) 1, Planned (Wave 3) 1.

## 4. ISO/IEC 27701 Extension

GHXSTSHIP designs its privacy information management to ISO/IEC 27701 for both of its roles:

| Area                                                  | Role                     | Implementation                                                                             | State                                               |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Conditions for collection and processing              | Controller               | Lawful basis registry and records of processing (`legal/privacy/records-of-processing.md`) | Drafted                                             |
| Obligations to PII principals                         | Controller               | Privacy Policy, DSAR workflow, consent records                                             | Drafted (policy); Planned (Wave 2) for the workflow |
| Privacy by design and by default                      | Controller and processor | Secure Development Policy, DPIA template, data classification                              | Drafted                                             |
| PII sharing, transfer and disclosure                  | Controller and processor | DPA with EU SCCs, UK Addendum and Swiss amendments; Subprocessor List                      | Drafted                                             |
| Customer conditions and obligations                   | Processor                | Data Processing Agreement and Annex II measures                                            | Drafted                                             |
| Processor records and support for data subject rights | Processor                | Records of processing Part B; DSAR workflow for Customers                                  | Drafted (record); Planned (Wave 2) for the workflow |

## 5. Exclusions

Control 7.6 (Working in secure areas) is excluded because GHXSTSHIP operates no secure areas of its own; production facilities belong to infrastructure subprocessors, whose controls are covered under 5.19 to 5.23.
