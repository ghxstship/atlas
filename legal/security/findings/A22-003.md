# A22-003: Viewer writes to comment threads

## Finding

`comment_threads_update` allowed anyone who could read a thread's subject to update the thread.
`private.guard_comment_thread` pinned the subject and stamped the resolution, but it never checked
who was acting. A Viewer, the read-only band, could therefore resolve a thread, or move it to Trash
and hide the discussion from every member.

## Fix

A thread is updated only by a person who can read its subject and who also meets one of these:

- opened the thread;
- authored a comment in it;
- holds `org.members.manage`.

The check is `private.may_update_comment_thread`, and pgTAP 0937 covers both the refusal and the
opener's resolve.
