# A22-005: Supabase entity name in the subprocessor list

## Severity rationale

Low. No data is exposed. A subprocessor list must name the legal entity that processes the data,
because customers rely on it for their own records of processing and transfer assessments. Naming
the wrong entity weakens the DPA's notice and its transfer documentation, which counsel will catch
before launch, but it misleads customers until then.

## Location

`legal/subprocessors.md:27` names "Supabase, Inc.".

## Reproduction

Compare the list against Supabase's own documents, as read on 2026-10-16:

- Supabase Terms of Service, Section 1(j), at https://supabase.com/terms: the contracting party is
  "Supabase Pte. Ltd., a Singapore corporate entity". "Supabase, Inc." is the party only for
  purchases made through the AWS Marketplace.
- Supabase Data Processing Addendum, at https://supabase.com/legal/dpa: the Supabase party and data
  importer is "Supabase Pte. Ltd".

## Impact

Customers would record the wrong processor and the wrong country of establishment (Singapore, not
the United States) for their transfer impact assessments. The SCC module and the docking details
in `legal/data-processing-agreement.md` that rely on this list would point at an entity that is not a
party to the Supabase DPA.

## Suggested fix

Name "Supabase Pte. Ltd." in `legal/subprocessors.md`. If XOS buys Supabase through the AWS
Marketplace, name "Supabase, Inc." and record that purchase route in `legal/OWNER_INPUTS.md`. Check
the transfer basis wording in the DPA against a Singapore-established processor.
