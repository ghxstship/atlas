# A22-004: Active content in public buckets

## Finding

The public buckets `org-brand` and `profile-media` allowed `image/svg+xml`, and nothing in the
repository sanitizes SVG. Section 9 forbids serving active content inline from public buckets. Any
signed-in person could upload a script-bearing SVG to their own profile folder and share its public
URL.

## Fix

- Both buckets drop `image/svg+xml` from their allowlists.
- Their insert and update policies refuse SVG, HTML, XML and script, by extension and by declared
  type, through `private.is_passive_public_object`. The policies matter because a direct insert into
  `storage.objects` skips the storage API's type check.
- The Atlas logo upload and its copy now offer PNG, WebP and ICO.
- pgTAP 0938 covers the refusals and a passive PNG upload.
