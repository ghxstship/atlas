# Worker Terms

> **Draft for review by counsel before launch.** This document is a draft. It is not in effect and no one may rely on it. It takes effect only when GHXSTSHIP Industries LLC publishes it at /legal, and its effective date is that date of publication.

These Worker Terms apply when an organization that uses XOS 4.0 (the "Organization") invites you to create an account so you can work with it as crew, staff, a contractor, an artist or in another external role, and you use Gateway or Compass for that work. They are an agreement between you and GHXSTSHIP Industries LLC ("GHXSTSHIP", "we" or "us"), which operates XOS. You accept them when you create your account.

## 1. Who Does What

- **The Organization engages you, not GHXSTSHIP.** Your offer, agreement, pay, schedule, classification as employee or contractor, and any background check decision are between you and the Organization. GHXSTSHIP is not your employer, staffing agency or client, and is not a party to your agreement with the Organization.
- **The Organization controls your work data.** For the information processed about your engagement with the Organization, the Organization is the controller and GHXSTSHIP is its processor. That includes your application, onboarding documents, certifications you share, shifts, time entries, location at clock-in and clock-out, timesheets, messages, photos, incidents, ratings, invoices and payments. The Organization's privacy notice explains how it uses that information, and requests about it should go to the Organization. If you send such a request to us, we pass it to the Organization and help it respond.
- **Your account is yours.** You have one XOS account across every organization you work with. You own your profile and choose, field by field, what each Organization sees.

## 2. Your Account

- You must be at least 16 years old. An opportunity may require a higher minimum age.
- Keep your sign-in details secure. Turn on multi-factor authentication, which some organizations require.
- Give accurate information, keep your certifications current, and do not create an account for someone else.
- You may also be invited by token link without an account, for example to complete an advance form or sign a document. After you finish, you can claim an account, and your earlier submissions are linked to it by your verified email address.

## 3. Your Profile and Consent to Share

- Each profile field has a visibility you set: Private, Shared on application, or Public in the marketplace.
- When you apply to or accept an engagement, you choose which fields go to that Organization. It receives a snapshot, plus live updates while the engagement is active.
- If you revoke sharing after the engagement closes, future updates stop. The Organization keeps the engagement records it must retain by law.
- Certifications and other items you own carry over to a different organization only with your consent.

## 4. What the Organization Can and Cannot See

- The Organization sees only what you share with it and what you create while working for it.
- Other organizations never see your work for this Organization, its pay rates, or its internal notes about you.
- You never see the Organization's internal notes, internal ratings, margins, budget or other people's rates.
- Tax identifiers and bank details are entered through secure forms, encrypted, and shown afterwards only as the last four digits.

## 5. Time, Location and Devices

- When you clock in or out in Compass, the app checks your location against the work site's geofence. Location is sampled only while needed for the shift, not continuously in the background outside it.
- If you clock in offline, the app records the device time and location quality, and the server checks them when you reconnect.
- If the Organization uses a shared kiosk, you may punch in with a PIN and, if the Organization enables it, a photo. XOS does not use facial recognition and does not create biometric templates from these photos.
- Biometric unlock of the app is handled by your device. GHXSTSHIP never receives your fingerprint or face data.
- If your access ends or the Organization signs you out remotely, data stored on your device for that Organization is wiped.

## 6. Background Checks

Where an Organization's onboarding requires a background check, it runs through Checkr, a consumer reporting agency. Checkr gives you the required disclosure and asks for your authorization, and handles pre-adverse and adverse action notices, under the Fair Credit Reporting Act and applicable state and local fair-chance laws. XOS stores only the result and a reference, never the report. Questions about a report go to Checkr. Questions about a decision go to the Organization.

## 7. Pay and Taxes

- The Organization pays you through its own payroll or accounts payable, or, if it enables it, through Stripe Connect payouts. GHXSTSHIP never holds your money.
- Where the Organization enables self-billing and you agree, invoices can be generated from your approved timesheets.
- You may be asked for a W-9, W-8BEN or W-8BEN-E. The Organization uses them to meet its own tax reporting, including 1099-NEC where it applies.
- Employment eligibility checks, such as Form I-9 or right-to-work checks, stay with the Organization or its payroll provider. XOS records only that they are complete.

## 8. Ratings and Reviews

After an engagement is complete, you and the Organization can rate each other. Neither side sees the other's rating until both are in or 14 days pass. You can reply publicly to a rating about you. Ratings may not be bought, incentivized or suppressed.

## 9. Your Rights

You may ask the Organization for access to, correction of, or deletion of your work data, and exercise the other rights your local law gives you. For your account itself, you can:

- download your profile and your own records in Gateway, Settings, Data and Privacy
- correct your profile at any time
- delete your account in Compass (Profile, Settings, Delete Account), in Gateway, or from the page at /legal/account-deletion

When you delete your account, each Organization keeps the records it must retain by law, such as timesheets, payments and tax documents. Our Data Retention and Deletion Policy has the details.

## 10. Rules

You must follow the Acceptable Use Policy and, for the app, the Mobile End User License Agreement. Report a problem with an Organization's listing or conduct using the Report action, and report a safety concern to the Organization and, if needed, to the authorities.

## 11. Ending These Terms

You can stop using XOS and delete your account at any time. We may suspend or close an account that breaks the Acceptable Use Policy, and will send you a statement of reasons and a way to appeal. Engagement access ends on the Organization's schedule: it narrows at Complete and closes 18 months after Complete, apart from your own tax documents and payments.

## 12. Liability

XOS is provided to you free of charge. To the extent the law allows, GHXSTSHIP provides it "as is" and is not liable for indirect or consequential damages, and its total liability to you is limited to fifty US dollars. GHXSTSHIP is not responsible for the Organization's acts, decisions or payments. Nothing in these Terms limits rights you have under employment, consumer or data protection law that cannot be limited by contract.

## 13. Changes, Law and Contact

We publish every version of these Terms at /legal and tell you in the app before a material change takes effect. These Terms are governed by the laws of the state of the United States identified as our governing jurisdiction on our contact page at /legal/contact, except where the law of your place of residence gives you protections that cannot be waived. Questions about your account go to the support mailbox listed on that contact page. Questions about your work go to the Organization through Messages.
