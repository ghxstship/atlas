# Worker Terms

These Worker Terms apply when you hold an XOS account to work with, or look for work with, an organization that uses XOS (the "Organization") as crew, staff, a contractor, a vendor, an artist or in another external role, and you use Gateway or Compass for that work. They apply to anyone who holds an engagement or a roster entry with an Organization, and to anyone whose account has no active membership of an Organization. They are an agreement between you and GHXSTSHIP Industries LLC ("GHXSTSHIP", "we" or "us"), which operates XOS. You accept them by ticking the acceptance box when you create your account in Gateway, when Gateway asks after you first sign in with a provider such as Google, or at your first sign-in to Compass, which asks before anything else. If you use an account for a company, such as a vendor, you accept for yourself and confirm that you may act for that company. XOS records your acceptance, not a separate acceptance by the company. If an Organization adds you as one of its internal members, these Worker Terms do not cover that work, and you are not a party to the Organization's agreement with us. That agreement binds the Organization, which must pass our rules on to you, and the Acceptable Use Policy and the Mobile End User License Agreement still apply to your use of XOS.

XOS is offered to organizations in the United States. An Organization that invites you may let you use your account to work with it, and that invitation is not an offer of XOS to you or to your country. A company account on Gateway is an organization, so it needs the same statement of country as any other: a company established outside the places XOS is offered needs a recorded exception from GHXSTSHIP, and its people can still accept invitations under their own accounts.

## 1. Who Does What

- **The Organization engages you, not GHXSTSHIP.** Your offer, agreement, pay, schedule, classification as employee or contractor, and any background check decision are between you and the Organization. GHXSTSHIP is not your employer, staffing agency or client, and is not a party to your agreement with the Organization.
- **These Terms do not decide your legal status.** Your status follows the law that applies to your real working relationship. Nothing in these Terms, or any label in XOS, takes away rights that law gives you.
- **The Organization controls your work data.** For the information processed about your engagement with the Organization, the Organization is the controller and GHXSTSHIP is its processor. That includes your application, onboarding documents, certifications you share, Work Details you share, shifts, time entries, location recorded with punches and incidents, credential scans, timesheets, messages, photos, incidents, ratings, invoices and payments. A rating is the one exception: GHXSTSHIP sets the rule that shows it only if you opt in (Section 8), and you can dispute it with us as Section 8 describes, as well as raise it with the Organization. The Organization's privacy notice explains how it uses that information, and requests about it should go to the Organization. If you send such a request to us, we pass it to the Organization and help it respond.
- **Your account is yours.** You have one XOS account across every organization you work with. You control your profile and your Work Details, and you choose what each Organization sees when you share them. The Privacy Policy explains how we handle your account and public profile.

## 2. Your Account

- You must be at least 18 years old. Gateway and Compass ask you to confirm this, and we do not verify your answer. If you answer that you are under 18, you get no account, and an existing account is closed once you confirm that answer. Until you answer, you cannot create or join an organization, accept a company account membership or use AI features. An opportunity may require a higher minimum age.
- Keep your sign-in details secure. Turn on multi-factor authentication, which some organizations require.
- Give accurate information, keep your certifications current, and do not create an account for someone else. If an Organization has verified a certification, it may need to make any change to that record, so ask it. A "verified" mark means that Organization recorded a check, not that GHXSTSHIP did.
- You may also be invited by token link without an account, for example to complete an advance form or sign a document. After you finish, you can claim an account, and your earlier submissions are linked to it by your verified email address. A typed name on a signing link records who typed it and the text they agreed to. XOS does not check identity beyond the link and code.
- Parental control tools are commercially available, and the US Federal Trade Commission publishes information about them at consumer.ftc.gov/identity-theft-and-online-security/protecting-kids-online. XOS is not directed to children and does not offer them.

## 3. Your Profile, Work Details and Consent to Share

- Your profile and each of its sections have a visibility you set: Private (you and organizations you have shared it with), Network (people and organizations that share a membership or engagement with you) or Public (everyone in the marketplace). Where the two differ, the narrower one applies.
- When you apply to or accept an engagement, you choose which fields go to that Organization. It receives a snapshot, plus live updates while the engagement is active.
- **Work Details** are the information you keep for your work. They can be sensitive: dietary needs, which can reveal your health or religion; allergies; accessibility needs; emergency contacts; loyalty numbers; passport details; and contact details. Give an emergency contact's details only if that person agrees.
- You share Work Details engagement by engagement, category by category, under a consent you can revoke at any time. Revoking takes effect at once. The Organization can read what you shared only while the engagement is Onboarding, Blocked, Active or Closing, and only the people whose role covers that category can read it, such as safety for emergency contacts. Dietary needs, allergies and accessibility needs follow the narrower rule below.
- Before you first enter dietary needs, allergies or accessibility needs, XOS asks for your consent to keep them for that category, and records it with the wording you saw, the language, the app, the time and your IP address. These are Restricted: only you, and the Organization's Owner and the catering lead and on-site medical roles on a project you share that category with, can read them, and every read is logged. A meal preference you give for a project goes to those same people on that project, with no separate share. Hospitality readers see these locked, and catering planners see counts of five or more with no names. A passport number is encrypted and masked to its last four characters, and revealing it takes a separate, audited step.
- You can withdraw that consent in one step under Work Details, or on your profile for supplier diversity. Use stops at once, the values are cleared, the category leaves every share, and what others attested about you is cleared, including guest list entries matched only by your verified email. If an Organization has a legal hold over those records, the values stay hidden from everyone until the hold ends, and are then cleared.
- Values you entered before XOS recorded consent are hidden from everyone but you. We tell you once, and they are deleted 30 days after that notice unless you agree to keep them.
- Reads of what you shared are written to your own access log and the Organization's audit ledger.
- If a company rosters you, such as an agency or vendor, and you consent to share with it, it can read your shared categories as well, except your dietary needs, allergies, accessibility needs, meal preferences and your full passport number. It cannot read those, including a meal preference it entered for you.
- For a project, you or, if the Organization allows it, your company may also give travel requests, seat and meal preferences, uniform sizes and vehicle details, including a plate number. Those facts belong to that project.
- If you revoke sharing after the engagement closes, future updates stop. The Organization keeps its engagement records, as Section 9 describes.
- Certifications and other items you own carry over to a different organization only with your consent.
- You keep your rights in your profile, photos, portfolio, press kit and other content you add. You give GHXSTSHIP a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free license to host, copy and display that content, only as needed to run XOS and only at the visibility you set, including to search engines if you allow that. The license ends when you remove the content or delete your account, apart from copies in backups until they expire and what an Organization has already received from you.

## 4. What the Organization Can and Cannot See

- The Organization sees what you share with it, what you create while working for it, and what it records about your work, such as shifts offered, accepted or declined, attendance, hours, scans and notes. It can build reports from those records.
- Planners may see an ordered list of candidates for a shift. The order comes from fixed rules and uses no AI: whether you are free of a conflicting shift, training due where that applies, the availability you stated, whether you have worked the role before, your hours that week, and then your name. A person chooses who is assigned, and the Organization is responsible for that choice.
- Other organizations never see your work for this Organization, its pay rates, or its internal notes about you. They never see its rating of you unless you opt in, and even then nobody with hiring, booking or staffing access in XOS sees it (Section 8).
- You never see the Organization's internal notes, internal ratings, Do Not Engage flags, margins, budget or other people's rates. A Do Not Engage flag can stop you from seeing or applying to that Organization's opportunities without telling you. To ask what an Organization holds about you, including any such flag, ask the Organization, as Section 9 describes.
- Tax identifiers and bank details are entered through secure forms and encrypted. Most people at an Organization see only the last four digits. People the Organization allows to make its year-end tax export can export your full tax identifier, with a second factor and an audit record, for its tax reporting. Payout details you add under Settings, Payout and tax show only the last four digits. If an Organization holds a bank account for you or your company as a vendor, people it gives the banking capability can read the full account and routing numbers.
- If the Organization turns on AI features, people there may use them on records about you, within what those people are allowed to see, and that can include text about you going to our AI provider. AI features do not decide whom to engage, what you are paid or how you are classified. If AI helps with a recommendation about your hiring, shifts, hours, pay or performance, the Organization must have a qualified person review it and tell you. The AI Features Notice has the details.

## 5. Time, Location and Devices

- Compass uses your location only while the app is open and never in the background. Sampling is off away from shifts, coarse and infrequent on shift, and precise only when you are about to punch.
- When you clock in or out in Compass, the app checks your location against the work site's geofence where the project has one. Your coordinates, their accuracy and the result are sent with the punch and kept with your time records, which the Organization can see and keeps as its retention rules and the law require. GHXSTSHIP has set no fixed end date for them, as the Data Retention and Deletion Policy says.
- Location can also be sent with an incident report, a form that requires it, a punch a supervisor records for the team, and a photo the Organization keeps location on.
- You can refuse location permission. A project that requires location, or a punch outside the geofence, may then be refused, so ask your supervisor how to record your time.
- If you clock in offline, the app records the device time and location quality, and the server checks them when you reconnect.
- If the Organization uses a shared kiosk, you may punch in with a PIN and, if the Organization enables it, a photo. XOS does not use facial recognition and does not create biometric templates from these photos, and our Acceptable Use Policy forbids organizations from doing so.
- If you hold a credential, each scan at an access point records the time, the place and the decision, online or offline, and those scans are kept for 7 years. A badge photo is used only with your consent. If you add a pass to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet, your name, role, organization, project and times go to that wallet provider at your request.
- Biometric unlock of the app is handled by your device. GHXSTSHIP never receives your fingerprint or face data.
- If your access ends or you are signed out remotely, Compass's encrypted local store is wiped when the app next connects and learns of it. Small items outside that store, such as a lock screen widget's queue, can stay until you remove the app.
- If you turn on text messages in your settings, you agree to get work notifications from that Organization at the number you verified. Message frequency varies, and message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop, which applies to that number for every organization, or HELP for help. We do not share your mobile number or texting consent with anyone for their marketing.

## 6. Background Checks

Where an Organization requires a background check, it runs the check through a consumer reporting agency of its choice, outside XOS. The Organization, or the agency on its behalf, must give you the required disclosure, ask for your authorization and give you the pre-adverse and adverse action notices, under the Fair Credit Reporting Act and applicable state and local fair-chance laws. If the Organization records the outcome in XOS, XOS stores only the result and a reference, never the report. Questions about a report go to the agency that prepared it. Questions about a decision go to the Organization.

## 7. Pay and Taxes

- The Organization pays you through its own payroll or accounts payable, and may record the payment in XOS. XOS records payments and does not move money. GHXSTSHIP never holds your money, and owes you nothing for your work.
- If you record an expense or a reimbursement in XOS, that is a record for the Organization to act on, and not a payment or promise by GHXSTSHIP.
- Where the Organization enables self-billing and you agree, invoices can be generated from your approved timesheets.
- You may be asked for a W-9, W-8BEN or W-8BEN-E. The Organization uses them to meet its own tax reporting, including 1099-NEC where it applies. We do not give tax advice.
- Employment eligibility checks, such as Form I-9 or right-to-work checks, stay with the Organization or its payroll provider. XOS does not run these checks, and you should not upload copies of identity documents as general files.

## 8. Ratings and Reviews

After an engagement is complete, you and the Organization can rate each other. Neither side sees the other's rating until both are in or 14 days pass. You can reply to a rating about you.

**Who can read a rating of you.** An Organization's rating of you can be read only by that Organization and by you. It is shown to anyone else only if you opt in to showing your ratings on your profile. Your rating of an Organization, and a rating of a company or vendor, can be read by signed-in XOS users.

**Opting in.** Showing your ratings is off by default. You choose all of your ratings or none, and you can withdraw at once. Only you can change it, in your own signed-in session, and never through a connected app. XOS records the version of the notice your page showed, so a choice made from a page that is out of date is refused, and you reload. Even when you opt in, nobody with hiring, booking or staffing access in XOS sees your ratings. That means anyone who holds such a capability in an Organization or on a project, anyone who runs an external account, a live Advancing Lead, an artist's representative and anyone engaged as an Artist. XOS knows only the access it grants, so someone who hires outside XOS could still read a profile you share.

**Disputing a rating.** In Gateway, open your Profile and choose Dispute this rating. The grounds are abuse, personal data, or a statement that is wrong or false. A low score alone is never a ground. The Organization may answer once. The platform team decides and gives its reasons, and the decision stays visible to you in Gateway, including after an upheld dispute removes the rating. An Organization deleting the engagement does not remove a rating, hide it from you or take it off your profile. Only a decision by the platform team on a named ground removes one.

Ratings may not be bought, incentivized or suppressed. Nothing in these Terms stops you from giving an honest review or rating. You keep your rights in your rating text and reply, and give GHXSTSHIP a non-exclusive license to host and show them as this section describes, for as long as the rating is kept. An Organization may not use a rating as a background check or screening report. Deleting your account does not remove a rating about you or by you. A rating of you stays in the rating Organization's own record, without your full name, and is no longer shown to anyone else. A rating you gave stays where it was shown, without your full name, along with whatever its comment says.

## 9. Your Rights

You may ask the Organization for access to, correction of, or deletion of your work data, and exercise the other rights your local law gives you. For your account itself, you can:

- ask for a copy of your account data through support@ghxstship.pro with "Privacy" in the subject line
- correct your profile and Work Details at any time in Gateway or Compass, apart from a record a verifier has to change
- delete your account in Compass (More, Settings, Delete Account) or in Gateway (Settings, Account), which needs a connection and your typed sign-in email. The Delete Your Account page at /legal/account-deletion explains what else you can do if you cannot sign in

Deletion runs at once, unless you are clocked in, you are the only Owner or Account Owner of an organization or account, or a legal hold covers your records. In those cases it is refused until that is resolved, and nothing is deleted.

Deleting your account erases your sign-in, full name, profile, Work Details, devices and preferences, ends your memberships, cancels engagements still Onboarding, Blocked or Active, and tells each Organization by recording your erasure request. **A gap we are telling you about:** separate given and family names, which you can add and which Sign in with Apple can add for you, are not yet removed when you delete your account. Clear them first in your account settings in Compass. The Delete Your Account page has the details.

Time and pay records, tax forms, and engagement records and signed agreements stay with each Organization. They are kept until the Organization deletes them from its live records and then for 7 more years, or until the Organization's data is purged if that comes first. A tax form keeps the legal name on it, and a signed agreement keeps the signed name. Records you created for an Organization, such as incident reports, stay with it without your name. The audit trail is kept for 7 years, with your personal details removed after 90 days. A form or document you completed or signed, or a copy an Organization already took, can still show your name. Our Data Retention and Deletion Policy has the details.
