# Text Message Terms

> **Draft before launch.** This document is a draft. It is not in effect and no one may rely on it. Showing it at /legal does not put it in effect. It takes effect only on the effective date that GHXSTSHIP Industries LLC states at the top of a final version.

These terms cover the text messages that GHXSTSHIP Industries LLC ("GHXSTSHIP", "we" or "us") sends through XOS 4.0.

## 1. The Program

- **Program name:** XOS notifications.
- **What it sends:** updates about your work from the organizations you belong to or work with in XOS, such as shift changes, approvals and safety messages. Texts are about your work and are never marketing.
- **Who it is for:** people who turn texts on in their own XOS settings. An organization cannot turn them on for you.
- **When it starts:** texts reach real recipients only after the messaging campaign is approved by the carriers.

## 2. How You Agree

In your personal settings in XOS, you can agree to get work notifications from an organization at a phone number you have verified. We record your agreement for that organization and that number. Agreeing is not a condition of working with any organization or of using XOS.

## 3. What to Expect

- Message frequency varies.
- Message and data rates may apply.
- Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
- We hold messages during quiet hours, 21:00 to 08:00 by default, except for safety messages, a change to your sign-in security, a recovery code used on your account, emergency access taken in your organization, and a shift assigned or changed that starts within 12 hours.

## 4. Stopping and Help

- **Reply STOP to stop.** We also accept QUIT, END, REVOKE, OPT OUT, CANCEL, UNSUBSCRIBE or STOPALL when the word is the whole message. A request to stop applies to every organization that had your consent for that number. You can also turn texts off in your personal settings.
- **Reply START, YES or UNSTOP** to turn messages back on.
- **Reply HELP for help.** We also accept INFO when the word is the whole message. For support, write to support@ghxstship.pro with "Support" in the subject line.

## 5. Privacy

Mobile information is not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. We do not sell or share text messaging opt-in data or consent with anyone for those purposes. Twilio delivers the messages as our service provider and receives your number, the message and its delivery status for that purpose only. We keep records of consent and opt-outs for 5 years. Our Privacy Policy, Section 17, has more.
