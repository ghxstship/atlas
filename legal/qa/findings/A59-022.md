# A59-022: Blank card under the shift timer

## Finding

After Clock In, Today shows the On Shift card (timer, "You're at Pier Site", Start Break, Clock
Out) and below it a half-width white card about 360 x 360 pt that contains only the words "Current
shift" at its bottom. Nothing else renders in it. Below that, the shift the person just clocked in
to still carries the chip "Waiting for you" (the acceptance state), which reads as if the shift
still needs action. The card disappears again after Clock Out.

## Steps

1. Build Compass for the simulator (`legal/qa/visual-review/scripts/compass-build.sh`), sign in as
   tech@example.com with location set to the Pier Site.
2. Today > Clock In.

- `legal/qa/visual-review/evidence/A59-022-1.jpg`
- Full resolution: `legal/qa/visual-review/shots/compass/field/field-light-clock-in.png`

## Suggested fix

Render the current shift's details (role, times, venue, next break) in that card or remove it,
and change the shift chip to "On shift" once a punch is open.

## Fix (A62)

The square card came from the Live Activity layout's second panel, which fell back to the bare
caption "Current shift" when the clock found no accepted shift. Today now passes the shift being
worked (`currentShift`: one of mine whose window, opened two hours early, includes now, in any live
state) with its role, times and venue, and the panel is full width; with no shift to name it is left
out. While a punch is open that shift's card reads On Shift (or On Break) instead of Waiting for you,
and open shifts in Schedule read Open.
