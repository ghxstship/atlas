# A65-020: AI features without a door

## Finding

Section 4.7 lists six features. The API serves all of them (`aurora.ask`, `.filter`, `.extract`, `.draft`,
`.summarize`, `.mapColumns`, `.bindCode`), but the shells call only `ask` (the assistant panel) and `extract` (riders
and insurance certificates):

- **Ask in plain language**: no view offers a sentence-to-filter input.
- **Drafting** of SOPs, advance packets, emails, day sheets and change order descriptions: no entry point.
- **Summaries** of a record's activity, a project's week and gate readiness: no entry point.
- **Mapping help** in the import wizard and catalog bindings for unknown scanned codes: neither the wizard nor the
  intake queue calls them.
- **Extraction** of invoices, receipts and quotes: no entry point (riders and certificates are done).

## Why it matters

Customers pay for an Aurora allowance on every paid plan (ADR 0020); most of it has no button.

## Suggested fix

Add the entry points behind the AI Features switches: a plain-language field in the view filter bar, Draft
buttons in the SOP, advance packet and change order editors, a Summarize action on records and project Overview,
Suggest mappings in the import wizard, Suggest binding in the scan intake queue, and Extract on invoice, expense and
RFQ response forms. Each confirms before writing, as the guardrails require.
