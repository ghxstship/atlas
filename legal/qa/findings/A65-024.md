# A65-024: Navigation cannot be personalized as specified

## Finding

- Favorites cannot be reordered by drag or keyboard (`app.favorites.sort_order` exists, no control).
- The Projects entry does not show the five most recent projects (Section 4.5.2).
- People cannot hide, reorder or pin sidebar items, and there is no "Reset to role default" (Section 4.5.2).
- The context switcher's role lens ("All roles" or one role, Section 4.8.1) exists in no shell; View as role is an
  Admin preview, not a personal lens.

## Resolution (A81, A113)

- **Hide, reorder and Reset to role default:** A81, migration 1371 and Personal settings, Preferences, Sidebar.
- **Favorites reorder:** A113. The Favorites group (`apps/atlas/components/shell/sidebar-favorites.tsx`) renders the
  plain list, and when the pointer or focus first reaches it, `favorites-reorder.tsx` takes its place (keeping the
  focused favorite focused) and moves each favorite by dragging its move button, or from the keyboard on that button:
  Space picks up, the arrow keys move, Space drops and Esc puts back, and an assertive live region announces each step
  in en-US and es-US. The drag uses pointer events rather than HTML drag and drop, since Firefox starts no native drag
  from a button, so it works the same in every browser and by touch. A drop saves at once through `reorderFavoritesAction`,
  which sends only the caller's own favorites to `app.reorder_favorites` (migration 2090), one update under the owner
  policies; a failed save puts the previous order back and says so once, in an alert beside the Favorites heading.
- **Recent projects:** A113. Projects join `app.recent_views` (migration 2090, kept apart from the command menu's last
  50 records), the project frame notes each opening, and the pinned Projects entry lists the five projects the person
  opened most recently, then All Projects, from `app.recent_projects` under their own row level security. The list
  updates as projects open, without a reload.
- **Still open:** the role lens in the context switcher (Section 4.8.1).

## Why it matters

Section 11.10 item 15 lets people pin, reorder and hide; the role lens lets a person check their work as a
Finance Lead or as Crew.

## Suggested fix

Store per-person sidebar layout as a personal setting or a small table, render it in `nav-model.ts`, add the
recent-projects list, and add a role lens that narrows capabilities client-side to one held role (the database
still decides every row).

## Resolution: the role lens (A128, RRR-173, 2026-10-27)

Each shell's context switcher now has a role lens, "All roles" or one of the person's roles (ADR 0107). It narrows
navigation, Home, My Work, the Inbox and the controls a shell hides by capability to the chosen role's capabilities
intersected with the person's own (`app.role_lens_capabilities`, migration 2210), and never widens them; row level
security and the RPCs decide every act as before. Atlas shows it under the org switcher, Gateway in the Switch org
dialog (role types of the person's engagements) and Compass in the org switcher sheet. The choice lasts for the
session: a cookie in Atlas and Gateway, memory in Compass.

Regression tests: `packages/data/test/role-lens.test.ts` (the intersection is never wider than the person's own),
pgTAP 2210, `apps/atlas/test/role-lens.test.ts`, `apps/gateway/test/role-lens.test.ts`, `apps/compass/test/role-lens.test.ts`, the journeys
`apps/atlas/e2e/role-lens.spec.ts` and `apps/gateway/e2e/role-lens.spec.ts`, and the Maestro flow
`apps/compass/.maestro/flows/role-lens.yaml`.
