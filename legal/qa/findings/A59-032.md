# A59-032: Empty states that contradict other pages

## Finding

- Settings > Usage: "No usage recorded yet. Seats, engagements and storage appear here." with a
  View members button, while People > Directory says "5 seats in use · 2 Field members" and the
  org has four projects and several engagements.
- Settings > Templates: "No org templates yet. Save templates for projects, documents and
  packets." while Projects > Templates lists "Weekend Festival, Version 1 · 5 items", from which
  the template workflow created project WAE.

## Steps

1. Sign in as owner@example.com; open the three routes.

- `legal/qa/visual-review/evidence/A59-032-1.jpg` (Usage), `A59-032-2.jpg` (Settings > Templates),
  `A59-032-3.jpg` (Projects > Templates)

## Suggested fix

Read seat and engagement counts into Usage from the same source as the Directory banner, and either
list project templates in Settings > Templates or link to Projects > Templates.
