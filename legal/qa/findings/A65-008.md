# A65-008: Promised notifications with no sender

## Finding

- **Saved-search alerts** (Section 4.4.3): `app.saved_searches` and `app.opportunity_alerts` exist (0720) and
  Gateway has an Alerts page, but nothing writes `opportunity_alerts`: the "platform matcher" named in the table
  comment does not exist, and no notification kind exists for a match. Alerts are always empty.
- **Recommendations** (Sections 4.4.3, 4.5.8 Home): Explore ranks by deadline and start date only
  (`packages/data/src/gateway-marketplace.ts`); the For You feed and Home strip have no matching on skills,
  certifications, location or availability.
- **Discovery filters** (Section 4.4.3): role type, dates and pay only; no discipline and category, distance or
  required-certification filters (distance was left open in ADR 0019).
- **Onboarding expiry reminders** (Section 4.4.4): `app.onboarding_item_status.reminder_due` is computed (0730)
  but never sent.
- **Certification and personal document expiry reminders** (Sections 4.3 item 13, 4.9 Documents): no notification
  kind and no job.

The 32 notification kinds in `private.notification_templates` include none of these.

## Why it matters

External users rely on these to come back; Section 11.12 sets "first relevant opportunity in under 10 s". An
expired certification or insurance item that nobody is reminded about blocks the next engagement.

## Suggested fix

A pg_cron matcher that writes `opportunity_alerts` for newly published listings visible to each saved search's
owner and notifies by email and push (new kinds `opportunity.matched`, `onboarding.item_expiring`,
`certification.expiring`, `document.expiring`); a recommendation score in the marketplace RPC from profile skills,
certifications, location and availability, listed on the How ranking works page; the missing filters, with venue
coordinates for distance.

## Resolution (A101, RRR-147): saved-search alerts reach the person by push

A89 built the matcher and its `opportunity.matched` notification (1461, 1462). The claim then suppressed the push,
since a marketplace user has no device registered with the posting org, and suppressed the email too when the person
works with no org. Since 1671 both go out while the listing is open to them, the push to the person's own Gateway
browser and Compass device (ADR 0056), and a browser push opens the listing or Explore Alerts. Regression tests:
`supabase/tests/1671_person_push_delivery.test.sql` and `apps/gateway/e2e/push-reach.spec.ts`.
