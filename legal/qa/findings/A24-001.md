# A24-001: Confirmation lost when the row leaves the list

## Finding

On `/{org}/approvals` the decision form, and on `/{org}/trash` the restore form, render their
success message ("Decision recorded.", "Restored 1 item.") inside the list row, next to the button.
The server action revalidates the page, the decided approval leaves "Waiting on me" and the restored
row leaves Trash, so the row and its `aria-live` region unmount before anyone sees or hears the
confirmation. Sighted users see the row vanish with no message; screen reader users hear nothing.

## Repro

1. Sign in as owner@example.com on the demo seed, open Approvals.
2. Approve the "Owner sign-off" step. The item disappears; no confirmation shows.
3. As admin@example.com open Trash, Templates, and restore "Winter Gala Format". Same result.

## Expected

The confirmation is announced from a region that outlives the row: a page-level status region or
the shell's toast (UndoToast offers Undo for the restore).

## Test

The journeys assert the durable outcome (the item leaves the waiting list, the row leaves Trash)
and, since the fix, the confirmation message as well.

## Fix (A38, RRR-82)

The approvals and Trash pages wrap their lists in `PageStatus` (apps/atlas/components/workflow/kit.tsx),
an always-mounted live region above the list. The decision, withdrawal and restore forms announce
success there from inside the server action call, so the message survives the revalidation that
removes the row; refusals still show beside the button, whose row stays. The journeys assert
"Decision recorded." and "Restored 1 item." again, and axe runs on the page after each.
