# A65-022: First-run and feedback features are missing

## Finding

Section 11.7 first run: "Each user can open a private Northwind Live sandbox to explore" and "The feedback widget
sends a message and an optional screenshot to the platform team." Section 11.14 asks for consent-gated product
analytics tracking each workflow funnel. Section 7.3 lists `sandbox_orgs`, `feedback_submissions` and
`onboarding_progress`.

None of the three tables exists. `FeedbackWidget` is in `packages/ui` and used by no app. No analytics pipeline or
funnel events exist. (The eight-step setup checklist and the guided tours are built; A59-014 is fixing who sees
the checklist.)

## Why it matters

New users have no safe place to try the product, and the platform team has no in-product feedback or funnel
data to check the Section 11.11 to 11.13 targets in production.

## Suggested fix

Sandbox: clone the demo seed into a private org per person with an expiry, behind a Help menu entry. Feedback:
`feedback_submissions` plus the widget, reusing the support-screenshot upload path (1034). Analytics: a consented
event table or provider, decided by the owner (a new subprocessor if external).

## Resolution (A96)

ADR 0052 records the decisions.

- **Sandbox (1530).** Try a Sandbox in Atlas Help opens one private Northwind Live sandbox per person.
  - It is filled from a copy of the demo seed's core, kept in a migration because production has no demo tenant.
  - It is named as a sandbox and shows a banner with its end instant.
  - It is never billed, records its email and SMS Suppressed, and refuses invitations, joins, shares, links,
    integrations and webhooks.
  - It is purged through the 1270 tenant purge 14 days after it opens (`xos_purge_expired_sandboxes`).
- **Feedback (1531).** Send Feedback in the Atlas and Gateway help panels uses the design system's FeedbackWidget.
  - It sends the message, the page context it shows before sending, and an optional screenshot the person chose,
    uploaded through the support screenshot path (1034).
  - Platform staff triage it at `/support/feedback`, through A90's `private.is_platform_staff` or the stand-in roster
    until A90 lands.
- **Product analytics (1532).** First-party, with no outside provider. Events hold a route template, a workflow step
  and a random session key, and nothing that identifies a person.
  - They are recorded only after each app's consent seam allows it, honoring Global Privacy Control.
  - They are kept 13 months, with hourly funnel aggregates for staff.
  - The seam reads A82's cookie consent: a yes to performance cookies, and no Global Privacy Control.

Tests: pgTAP 1530 to 1532, `packages/data/test/a96-*.test.ts`, `packages/ui/test/feedback-widget.test.tsx` and
`pwa-analytics.test.ts`, and the Playwright journeys in `apps/atlas/e2e/a96-first-run.spec.ts`.

## Resolution (A146)

ADR 0052, section 6, records the decisions.

- **Funnel events.** Every Atlas and Gateway workflow of Sections 11.11 and 11.12 sends Workflow Started, Workflow
  Step and Workflow Completed events, named by one registry (`packages/data/src/product-workflows.ts`) and sent
  through the same consent seam and `/api/product-events`.
- **Funnels and targets (2340).** `private.product_funnels` gives each of the 26 workflows of 1532 an owning team, its
  steps and a target completion rate.
- **Regression alerts.** `xos_check_product_funnels` checks every funnel each hour over the hourly aggregates. It
  raises an alert in `app.product_funnel_alerts` for the owning team when the last 24 hours fall below the target or
  well below the 7 days before, and closes it on recovery. The operator console shows funnels and alerts at
  `/funnels`. No outside service is involved.
- **Left.** Compass has no analytics consent choice yet, so its eight workflows have funnels and targets but send no
  events.

Tests: pgTAP 2340, `packages/data/test/a146-workflow-funnels.test.ts` (with a check that every web workflow sends its
start, each step and its completion) and `packages/ui/test/pwa-analytics.test.ts`.
