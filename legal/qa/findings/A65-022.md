# A65-022: First-run and feedback features are missing

## Finding

Section 11.7 first run: "Each user can open a private Northwind Live sandbox to explore" and "The feedback widget
sends a message and an optional screenshot to the platform team." Section 11.14 asks for consent-gated product
analytics tracking each workflow funnel. Section 7.3 lists `sandbox_orgs`, `feedback_submissions` and
`onboarding_progress`.

None of the three tables exists. `FeedbackWidget` is in `packages/ui` and used by no app. No analytics pipeline or
funnel events exist. (The eight-step setup checklist and the guided tours are built; A59-014 is fixing who sees
the checklist.)

## Why it matters

New users have no safe place to try the product, and the platform team has no in-product feedback or funnel
data to check the Section 11.11 to 11.13 targets in production.

## Suggested fix

Sandbox: clone the demo seed into a private org per person with an expiry, behind a Help menu entry. Feedback:
`feedback_submissions` plus the widget, reusing the support-screenshot upload path (1034). Analytics: a consented
event table or provider, decided by the owner (a new subprocessor if external).
