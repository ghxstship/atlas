# A59-020: The partner-org condition depends on having client orgs

## Finding

The partner Owner's Atlas shell (Demo Partner Agency) shows the same sidebar as any org: Home,
Inbox, My Work, Projects, module groups, Activity, Settings. No item leads to the Partner Console,
and Settings > Partner answers 404. The console itself works when typed
(/partner/demo-partner, Client Orgs, Default Brand, Usage, Wholesale Billing all render). The
`partner-org` condition is set in `apps/atlas/lib/shell/membership.ts` (line 80) from
`hasClientOrganizations`, so an agency that has not yet created a client org, which is the
first thing the console is for, never sees its entry points. Home still shows the generic producer
checklist ("Create your first project", "Invite your field team").

## Steps

1. Sign in as partner@example.com.
2. Look for Partner Console in the sidebar, account menu and Settings; open
   /demo-partner/settings/partner (404).

## Suggested fix

Derive `partner-org` from the partner agreement (seed 1060 records one) rather than from client
orgs, and add a sidebar or account-menu entry to the console for partner Owners and Admins.

## Resolution (A61, RRR-106)

The partner-org condition also counts a partner agreement, so a new agency sees Settings > Partner, which shows client orgs and the wholesale discount with the way into the Partner console; partner Owners and Admins also see a Partner console card on Home.
