# A59-002: Tips sit above dialogs and hide what the person came to do

## Finding

The first-visit tip card (bottom right, about 440 x 140 px at desktop, full width on a phone) is
drawn above the side drawer and dialog layer. When a person opens a create drawer before dismissing
the tip, the card covers the drawer's footer:

- All Projects, New project: the tip covers "More fields" and the Create project button; only a
  sliver of the button shows (desktop and phone).
- Crew > Shifts, New shift (phone): "Read the resource schedule" covers the New shift button.
- Project Gates: "Gates guard each phase" covers the right end of the criteria and follows the
  person to Home > Gate readiness.
- Work > All Records: "Save the view you need" covers the last rows of the list.

Section 11.7 says a guided tour is offered, never forced; here it blocks the primary action until
the tip is dismissed, and the tip itself is about something else (saved views).

## Steps

1. Sign in as owner@example.com (fresh session) and open Projects > All Projects.
2. Click New project and expand More fields.
3. The "Save the view you need" card covers the drawer footer and its Create project button.

## Suggested fix

Hide tips while a dialog, drawer or peek is open (or render them below the overlay layer), and
place them so they never cover the page's primary action.

## Fix (A60, RRR-105)

- While a dialog, drawer, peek or the shell's navigation drawer is open, the tip card and the
  "Take a quick tour?" offer stay hidden; they show again once it closes. The offer is still
  recorded once, so it is queued, not lost, and Escape no longer reaches a hidden card.
- A showing card or tour step reserves its own height under the page
  (`--xos-help-card-space`), so the page's last rows and actions scroll clear of it.
