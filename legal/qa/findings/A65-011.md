# A65-011: Compass is missing several required features

## Finding

- **NFC** (Sections 4.3 item 7, 13): `src/domain/scan.ts` decodes NDEF and `ScanSheet` takes an `nfc` prop, but no
  screen passes it and no NFC native module is installed.
- **Expenses** (Section 4.3 item 11): receipt capture with OCR prefill posting to a budget line does not exist; the
  More entry opens Atlas.
- **External More items** (Section 4.5.7): timesheets, per diem, travel and lodging, onboarding, purchase orders,
  deliveries, submittals, time entries, deliverables, advance, rider, settlement, reports and proof of performance
  all hand off to Gateway (`app/pending/[page].tsx`). A59-026 (in progress) improves those pages, not the screens.
- **Combined Today across orgs with an org switcher and brand chips** (Section 4.5.7): absent.
- **Tenant brand at runtime** (Section 12) and the **Enterprise branded build** through per-tenant EAS profiles:
  absent; there is no `eas.json` at all.
- **Home screen widgets** (Section 13) and the **next cue in the Live Activity** and an **Android ongoing
  notification**: absent (the Live Activity shows the clock only).
- **Wallet passes** for credentials and shift passes (Sections 11.10 item 14, 11.13): absent.
- **Resumable uploads** and an org option to keep photo location (Section 13 Media): absent.

## Why it matters

Compass is "purpose-built for field use"; external crew and staff are told to use another app for their daily
work, and the Section 11.13 credential target (badge in one tap from the lock screen) cannot be met.

## Suggested fix

Split into four agent-sized parts: (1) NFC module and scan wiring, receipt capture with OCR through Aurora
extraction; (2) native screens for the external role items, reading the `api_external` views already synced;
(3) org switcher, combined Today and runtime brand from `org_brands`, plus `eas.json` with build and update profiles
and a per-tenant profile generator; (4) widgets, Live Activity next cue, Android ongoing notification, wallet passes
and resumable uploads.

## Resolution of parts 1 and 2 (A92, RRR-137)

Decisions in [ADR 0048](../../../docs/adr/0048-compass-nfc-receipts-external-screens.md).

- **NFC.** `react-native-nfc-manager` 4.0.0-beta.10 with its config plugin. Read NFC tag in every camera mode of the
  Scan tab feeds the same path as a camera scan (credentials at access points, asset custody, receiving, lookup,
  intake); Lookup writes an asset tag to a blank sticker. Maestro `field-nfc-scan` runs on the test tag of
  `EXPO_PUBLIC_XOS_FAKE_NFC`, which `app.config.ts` refuses in EAS builds.
- **Expenses.** Receipt from the camera or the system photo picker, read by Aurora's document extraction when the org
  has it on, confirmed by the person and posted to one budget line, offline-replayable through
  `app.submit_expense_receipt` (migration 1490). Maestro `field-expense-receipt`.
- **External More items.** Every entry the finding lists is a Compass screen over the api_external projections, offline,
  with dates on the project's calendar and the engagement's Gateway tab for actions. Maestro `crew-external-work`.
- **Left for later:** capturing proof-of-performance photos from Compass (a write path for external parties), and
  moving to `react-native-nfc-manager` 4.0.0 once it is two weeks old. Parts 3 and 4 belong to A93.
