# SIM-003: No voice note on an incident report

## Finding

Journey 54 files an incident offline "in three steps with a voice note in under 30 s, transcribed after
reconnecting". Compass has no voice note anywhere:

- `apps/compass/src/domain/incidents.ts` drafts carry photos and parties only; the report screen
  (`app/incidents/new.tsx`) offers Add Photo and nothing to record.
- No audio module is installed (no `expo-audio`), and `app.json` declares no microphone permission
  (`NSMicrophoneUsageDescription`).
- `app.add_incident_media` and the `field-media` bucket take images only; no column or job holds a
  transcript.

The rest of the journey works on the simulator: a three-step report with two photos, filed while the server
was unreachable, replayed once after it came back (see journey 16 in `legal/qa/journey-coverage.md`).

## Decision needed

1. Approve a pinned audio dependency (for example `expo-audio` at the version ADR 0001 allows) and a native
   rebuild with the microphone permission string.
2. Where a voice note lives: a media kind on `incident_media` with an audio content type the bucket accepts
   (A22-004 forbids only SVG, HTML, XML and script), or its own table.
3. Who transcribes after reconnecting: an A25 job reading the uploaded audio, writing the transcript beside
   the incident, with the model seam the assistant uses.

## Resolution (A42, RRR-86)

Fixed. Owner decisions 2026-10-17: expo-audio 57.0.5 (pinned), a media kind on `incident_media`, and
transcription deferred past 4.0 because it needs a new subprocessor.

- Migration 1113: `incident_media.media_kind` ('Photo', 'Video', 'Voice Note'; default Photo) and
  `duration_ms` (voice notes only, at most ten minutes); field-media accepts AAC audio (audio/mp4,
  audio/aac, audio/x-m4a) and MP3; `app.add_incident_media` takes the kind and length and keeps one
  signature for the sync wrapper. pgTAP `1113_incident_voice_notes`.
- Compass: step 3 of the incident report has Hold to Record a Voice Note, with playback and delete per
  note (`src/screens/VoiceNotes.tsx`). Notes are stored in field-media under
  `<org>/incidents/<incident>/<note>.m4a`, uploaded before the RPC and queued offline with the report,
  like photos. VoiceOver users double-tap to start and stop. `NSMicrophoneUsageDescription` and the
  expo-audio plugin are in `app.json`.
- The spec describes the voice note as transcribed but sets no copy for it, so the report shows no
  transcription text while transcription is deferred.
- Maestro `field-incident-report` records, plays and deletes notes and checks the report filed once with
  two photos and one voice note.
