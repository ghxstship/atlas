# A59-012: Right-hand columns are clipped mid-word

## Finding

At a 1280 px viewport the content column is about 1000 px. Tables wider than that are clipped at
the right edge, mid-word, with no fade, scrollbar or column picker hint:

- Finance > Expenses: "Vendor" column reads "Coastline Freig".
- Finance > Invoices: "Due on" reads "Nov 18," and "Nov 5,".
- Finance > Journal: "State" reads "Prepar" and "Postec".
- Finance > Payments and Change Orders: last column cut.
- People > Requisitions: state "Waiting for approv".
- Help Desk: "First ..." column header cut.
- Work > Recurring: the occurrence list runs off the row ("Mar 1, 2027, 10:0").
- Home > Gate readiness: "Missing evidence" cut after "G2.ACCOUNTABLE_".
- Safety > Dispatch Board: the fourth column is a sliver; the left list has no heading, and an
  empty column says only "None".

Section 11.2 (density with clarity) and the Collection template expect a column picker for wide
tables; today the most important trailing columns (state, due date, amount) are the ones lost.

## Suggested fix

Size columns to content with truncation and a tooltip, keep state and amount columns pinned, and
show an edge fade when a table or board scrolls horizontally.

## Fix (A60, RRR-105)

- ScrollRegion, which holds every DataTable, fades its start or end edge while content is hidden
  past it (both edges once scrolled to the middle, in right-to-left too). The fades hide in print
  and forced colors; the region keeps its label and keyboard reach.
- The @xos/ui Board and the operations board (Dispatch Board, Issued credentials) scroll in a
  ScrollRegion, so they fade too. An empty dispatch column says "No incidents in this state."
  rather than "None".
- Column pinning per module (state and amount) is left to the modules; the cut-off columns now
  read as scrollable.
