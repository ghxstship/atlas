# A38-002: No notification path for external parties

## Finding

Journey 56 has the vendor receive a push notification at each step from Submitted to Paid.
`private.notify` (migration 0520) writes a notification and its Email, SMS and Push deliveries only
for a recipient with a live membership in the org; it returns null for anyone else. External
parties (vendors, contractors, artists, clients, sponsors) hold no membership, so no step of their
invoice, offer or document ever creates a notification or a push delivery for them. The domain
events exist (`invoice.approved`, payment state transitions), and Gateway's own notification
settings page exists, but nothing connects the two.

## What the journey covers today

The Money tracker steps are automated: the invoice submitted in Gateway shows Submitted, Approved
after Finance codes and approves it, then Paid with a zero balance once the payment is applied, on
the engagement's Invoices tab and on Money, Invoices. The push itself also needs a device.

## Proposal

A person-level notification path for external parties: `private.notify_person` that skips the
membership lookup and uses the person's Gateway notification preferences, called for the external
party from the invoice, payment, offer and engagement document transitions. Push deliveries then
reach Compass through the existing `push_subscriptions`. Needs an owner decision on which events
notify external parties by default.

## Fix (A44, RRR-88)

- `private.notify` gains the external party path: a person who works with the org through an engagement or an account membership gets the in-app notification and an Email and a Push delivery as their own `app.person_notification_preferences` allow; anyone else still gets nothing.
- The notification read policy, `private.is_my_notification` and `app.claim_notification_deliveries` accept party orgs; Push to a party with no registered device is recorded and then suppressed with its reason.
- Invoice triggers notify the billing account's Owner, Admin and Finance members at `invoice.submitted`, `invoice.approved`, `invoice.scheduled` and `invoice.paid` (the Money tracker steps on main 5bd927f; coordinate with A43's invoice states when they land).
- The device step remains: external parties register no push device (push subscriptions are per membership).
