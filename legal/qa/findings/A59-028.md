# A59-028: Hours-only countdown

## Finding

The open shift on Sun, Mar 14 (2027) shows "Starts in 3492h 5m". A countdown is useful within a day
or two; beyond that, hours are hard to read. The date is already on the card.

## Steps

1. Sign in to Compass as tech@example.com, open Schedule.

## Screenshots

- `legal/qa/visual-review/evidence/A59-028-1.jpg`

## Suggested fix

Show the countdown only within 24 or 48 hours, otherwise "in 145 days" or nothing.

## Fix (A62)

Shift countdowns show hours and minutes within 24 hours of the call time and whole days beyond
it ("Starts in 146 days"), and nothing once the shift started.
