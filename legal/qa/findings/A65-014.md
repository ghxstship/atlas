# A65-014: Compliance rows that need product work

## Finding

Section 14 rows whose legal text is drafted but whose product flow is missing:

- **Age 16 or older** (Sections 14.3, 14.9): the Terms say so; neither sign-up form asks or records it.
- **Cookie consent** (Section 14.3): `app.cookie_consent_events` exists and `legal/cookie-policy.md` describes a
  banner with granular categories; no shell shows one.
- **Global Privacy Control** (Section 14.3 CCPA): `app.privacy_signals` exists; no shell reads the `Sec-GPC` header.
- **Re-acceptance after a material change with 30 days notice** (Section 14.4): `current_legal_acceptances`
  supports it; no prompt or notice exists.
- **Subprocessor change notification subscription** (Section 14.4): no subscription mechanism.
- **Certified payroll in WH-347 format** and **OSHA Forms 300, 300A and 301** (Section 14.5): no exports.
- **State overtime** (Section 14.5, "state overrides including California daily overtime"): `xpms.wage_hour_rule`
  holds only the federal FLSA row, and the payroll calculation (0461) applies weekly overtime only.
- **Export-control screening at sign-up** (Section 14.5): absent.
- **EEO questions stored apart and hidden from selectors**, **worker classification questionnaire with the org's
  recorded basis**, **I-9 completion state**, **FCRA workflow through a provider** (Section 14.9): absent or
  partial.
- **EU Data Act switching** (Section 14.8): the export exists; the docs-site article on format and process does not.
- `legal/compliance-matrix.md` still shows Wave 2 to 6 states ("Planned") for rows that have since shipped.

## Why it matters

These are the parts of Section 14 an auditor or regulator would test in the product, not in `legal/`.

## Suggested fix

Ship them as small vertical slices: sign-up age confirmation and screening; consent banner writing
`cookie_consent_events` plus GPC capture in the proxies; re-acceptance prompt in the shell; WH-347 and OSHA exports
over payroll and incident data; EEO store separate from applications; classification questionnaire as an onboarding
item type; the Data Act article; then refresh the compliance matrix states.

## Resolution of the Data Act article (A150, RRR-213)

The docs site now has both articles, in en-US and es-US, under Guides, Operate:

- **Full export** (`apps/docs/content/guides/<locale>/full-export.md`): how the Owner downloads the archive, its
  paths, every `manifest.json` field, the JSON and CSV conventions, the file limits, generated PDFs and what is left
  out, with links to the data dictionary.
- **Switching providers** (`apps/docs/content/guides/<locale>/switching.md`): Terms Section 11.3 as a process: how to
  ask for transition support, the 30 days, no switching fees from 12 January 2027, the final export and cancellation.

`apps/docs/test/data-portability.test.ts` checks them against the export code (`packages/data/src/lifecycle.ts`), the
export route's manifest hash header and the Terms, and that their data dictionary links exist; the docs Playwright
journey renders both in both locales and themes. The rest of this finding is unchanged.
