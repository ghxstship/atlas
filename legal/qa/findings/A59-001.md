# A59-001: The phone Menu tab is inert

## Finding

At phone width the Atlas bottom bar offers Home, Inbox, Search, My Work and Menu. Tapping Menu
changes nothing on screen: no drawer, no sheet. The handler in
`apps/atlas/components/shell/shell-frame.tsx` (the `TabBar` `onSelect` for `action === "menu"`)
clicks the first `button[aria-controls][aria-expanded]` in the document. On every page that is the
hidden desktop sidebar's "Production" group toggle (`aria-controls="..-group-1"`, not visible), so
the tap expands a group nobody can see. The only way to the module list on a phone is the small
hamburger in the top bar, which the bottom bar was meant to duplicate in thumb reach (Section 11.10,
"thumb first"; at most 5 phone destinations, one of which is this Menu).

## Steps

1. Sign in as field@example.com at 375 px wide.
2. Open Home and tap Menu in the bottom bar.
3. Nothing opens. In the DOM, the first `button[aria-controls][aria-expanded]` is the sidebar group
   button "Production", hidden at this width.

## Suggested fix

Give the drawer trigger a stable id or a ref the bottom bar can call, instead of a document-wide
selector, and add a phone e2e step that taps Menu and expects the navigation drawer.
