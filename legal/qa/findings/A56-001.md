# A56-001: What decision 25 leaves to the owner

## Finding

Owner decision 25 (2026-10-19, RRR-101) settles every Playbook to Bible difference of RRR-64, the canon content of
RRR-65 and the CSMIA26 rows of RRR-67. It says, in so many words, that Bible inconsistencies and unstated relationships
(RRR-66) are not covered. A56 applied the decision and did not decide any of the items below. Each is listed so the
owner can rule on it. Nothing here blocks `canon:check`: the open differences are listed as deferred, with this
finding as the reason, and the check fails if one changes or disappears.

## Open: Bible inconsistencies (RRR-66)

1. **"Show" and "Procurement" named as phases.** 18 touchpoints (Bible tab 10, lifecycle gate) and 54 items (Item
   Catalog default phase) name "Show"; 46 items name "Procurement". Neither is one of the nine gated phases. They carry
   no phase and wait in `xpms.intake` as Proposed (118 rows). Question: does Show mean Operate (OPR) and Procurement
   mean Procure (PRC), or should the Bible and catalog be corrected?
2. **Tab 27 labels department 4000 as "Build" on five roles** (4000.50.01, 4000.51.01 to 4000.51.04), while tab 01
   names it Environment. The code is stored, the label is not. Question: correct tab 27?
3. **33 items labeled "4000 Environment" carry 6000 URIDs** (Item Catalog lines 612 to 675, 6000.01.01-XOS-001 to
   -048). The URID wins in canon today. Question: are these Operations items, or do they need 4000 URIDs?
4. **Empty or headless statements.** Tab 23 (silence rule) has headings and no statement text; five tab 34 budget
   template rules (rows 8 to 12) are saved as formulas with no value; tab 24 repeats tab 25 headers in its
   `record_kind#6` columns; tab 25's phase columns hold the nine phases on the first nine rows only. Question: supply
   the statements, or remove the columns?

## Open: rulings that conflict or are unstated (RRR-66)

5. **Bible tab 27 roles against the Roles Library (ruling D12).** The Roles Library holds three role codes tab 27
   lacks: 2000.50.02, 5000.50.03 and 6000.50.03. Decision 25 says the Bible wins on codes; ruling D12 says the Roles
   Library owns role codes. These three are deferred in `decision-25.yaml`. Question: retire tab 27 in favor of the
   Roles Library, or renumber it to match?
6. **Does D13 (derived GL posting) cover item-level and counterparty default GL accounts?** Balance-sheet items
   cannot be derived by class.
7. **Relationships canon never states.** The tables exist and are empty: unit dimensions (so the Standard Library
   units Hour and Week have no canon unit, and are deferred in `decision-25.yaml`, `xpms.label_mapping` basis
   deferred), element tags, permit triggers, metrics, GTINs, the discipline-to-team link, and price band confidence
   and source dates (every band, Bible or Playbook, has no assertion word and resolves as no answer).

## Decided, flagged for review (no ruling needed)

These follow decision 25 as written; they are listed because the owner may want to look at them.

- **106 item prices where both files price an item and differ** (36 base, 35 elevated, 35 premium). The Bible's
  (Item Catalog's) price is kept: the decision imports only values the Bible lacks. Several look like a one-row shift
  in the Playbook's Procurement Catalog (for example 6000.08.02-XPMS-002 to -GHXST-001, where each Playbook price is
  the Item Catalog price of the next item). The full list is the price rows marked `bible-states-value` in
  `canon/reports/playbook-bible-diff.md`.
- **2,570 Playbook prices imported** where the Item Catalog leaves an item unpriced (857 base, 856 elevated, 857
  premium) and five cost center values. Each is marked in `xpms.playbook_sourced_value` for review.
- **Closest-value mappings that needed judgment** (`xpms.label_mapping`, basis closest-value or closest-state): the
  27 Locations categories, the nine Staffing Requisition event phases (FOH Sound Tuning & Show and Lighting Rig & Show
  Cues map to OPR, which agrees with reading Show as Operate in item 1), Work Orders Scheduled to Awarded, Projects
  Scheduled to Active, and Landlord to Facilities Services.

## Suggested fix

Rule on items 1 to 7 (RRR-66). Each ruling becomes data in `canon/rulings/` and an importer change; the matching
deferred entry in `decision-25.yaml` is removed, `canon:check` confirms nothing is left unexplained, and a forward
migration brings staging to the regenerated seed, as 1230 did for decision 25.

## Resolution (owner decision 39, WP18)

On 2026-10-23 the owner gave all seven rulings as proposed (ADR 0033 decision 39). Each is data in
`canon/rulings/decision-39.yaml`, which the importer applies (`canon/import/src/decision39.ts`); canon:check fails
on an entry the canon files no longer match. Migration 1610 brings staging to the regenerated seed, as 1230 did for
decision 25; a reset with the old 0101 and 0150 plus 1610 matches a fresh reset.

1. **Show and Procurement.** "Show" means Operate (OPR) and "Procurement" means Procure (PRC). The 18 touchpoints and
   100 items carry that phase; their 118 intake rows stay as Complete with the ruling as the reason. The Playbook's
   Procurement Catalog names the same phases, so its 100 cells stay in intake as Canceled for the round trip.
2. **Department 4000 on tab 27** is Environment, as tab 01 says. The code was always stored; the conflict is settled.
3. **The 33 items labeled 4000 Environment** keep their 6000 URIDs and take the label their code gives.
4. **Empty statements.** Tab 23's headings are no longer loaded into `xpms.canon_statement`; tab 24's two stray
   columns and tab 25's phase columns stay unloaded; the five tab 34 rules saved as empty formulas (Estimate, Budget,
   Forecast, Variance, Rate) are not loaded, and the other rules keep their ordinals.
5. **Role codes.** Tab 27's role codes are retired in favor of the Roles Library (ruling D12), which settles
   2000.50.02, 5000.50.03 and 6000.50.03. `xpms.dim_role` keeps tab 27's rows for their staffing ratios and kit
   bases, which role assignments and API v1 read.
6. **D13** allows explicit GL defaults on an item and on a counterparty type. Five balance-sheet items carry one
   (0000.51.01-XOS-001 to -005). Migration 1611 resolves a posting's account from the item's own account, then the
   URID class, then the counterparty type's default (`private.gl_account_for_line`).
7. **Time units.** The time dimension is ratified: hour, day, week and year are time units, so the Standard Library
   units Hour and Week resolve to canon units.

Decision 25 now defers nothing: canon:check reports 2,948 differences (2,575 imported, 237 Bible kept, 33 mapped to
an existing value, 103 ruled), none deferred or unexplained.

**Still open, not differences:** canon states no element tags, permit triggers, metrics, GTINs or discipline-to-team
links (the bridge tables stay empty), no dimension for the seven non-time unit bases, and no confidence or source
date on any price band. These are listed as unresolved references in `canon/reports/doctrine-findings.md` and wait
on canon data the owner supplies.
