# SIM-007: Field reads as Field Supervisor in Compass

## Finding

`apps/compass/src/data/identity.ts` treats a member holding any of `crew.timesheet.approve`,
`safety.incident.dispatch` or `crew.shift.write` as the `field-supervisor` audience (Section 4.5.7 supervisor
mode). The platform Field role holds `safety.incident.dispatch`, so every Field member, the Demo Field Lead
and the Demo Field Tech alike, gets the Crew tab and a Dispatch button on every reported incident. The Maestro
flow `field-sign-in` asserted that a Field Lead sees five tabs and no Crew tab; it failed on the simulator
and no longer asserts it.

## Decision needed

Either Field crew may dispatch incidents (then supervisor mode should key on `crew.timesheet.approve` or
`crew.shift.write` alone, and the sign-in flow asserts no Crew tab again), or dispatch belongs to Field
Supervisor and above (then the capability seed drops it from Field).
