# A65-018: Section 4.6.5 feeds stop at the database

## Finding

`app.calendar_feeds` (hashed tokens, revocable) and `api_public.calendar_feed_events` (0550, pgTAP 0550) return
exactly the shifts, meetings, milestones and deadlines a token's owner may see. But:

- No route serves `/ical/{feedToken}.ics`. The sitemap marks it delegated "to A18"
  (`apps/atlas/ia/routes.config.ts`) and no handler exists. A Next.js segment cannot be `[feedToken].ics`, so the
  route needs a sitemap or route-test adjustment (an ADR 0006 amendment).
- No UI creates or revokes a feed: Atlas Personal settings, Calendar Feeds is an empty state and Gateway's points
  to Upcoming.
- Project inboxes: `app.create_project_inbox` and `inbound_addresses` exist, but no operation or page shows or
  creates a project's address.

## Why it matters

People cannot subscribe to their schedule from any calendar app, which Section 4.5.8 Work also promises.

## Suggested fix

Route handler at `/ical/{token}` (accepting the `.ics` suffix) that calls the token RPC with the anon key and
writes RFC 5545 with zone-aware times; create, list and revoke in Atlas and Gateway settings; project inbox address
in Project Settings with `projectInbox` operations.
