# A59-004: Phone top bar breadcrumb overflows

## Finding

At 375 px the top bar holds the menu button, the breadcrumb, the Aurora and help buttons and the
"Account" button with its label. The breadcrumb gets about 110 px, so "Demo Productions > Inbox"
wraps to three lines. The breadcrumb box measures 60 px tall starting at y = -9 px: the first line
("Demo") is cut off above the screen. On deeper pages (a project tab, a settings section) the
third line spills below the 44 px bar and overlaps the page heading, for example "Advancing" drawn
over "Northwind Summer Series" on /p/NWS/advancing and "Organization Profile" over the settings list.
The "Account" button keeps its text label and chevron, taking a third of the bar.

## Steps

1. Sign in as any demo user at 375 x 812.
2. Open /demo-productions/inbox, then /demo-productions/p/NWS/advancing.
3. The breadcrumb's first line is clipped and its last line overlaps the title.
