# A65-013: Connectors without a runtime

## Finding

ADR 0024 left the integration worker schedule, the SCIM, Slack and Teams inbound endpoints, the chat token minter
and the verifier schedules without an owner. They still have none: `runIntegrationWorker`
(`packages/integrations/src/worker.ts`) is called by no Edge Function, route or schedule, so `integration.sync` jobs
are queued and never run. The connector libraries and their pgTAP are complete (0600 to 0639).

Consequences across Section 17: accounting, calendar, storage, payroll and carrier tracking never sync in a deployed
system; Slack and Teams unfurls and slash commands have no endpoint; domain verification (Section 4.8.3) has no
scheduled verifier; Zoom, Meet and Teams meeting links are not created (Section 4.10); storage import is not wired
into the import wizard. Zapier and Make apps (Section 17) do not exist.

## Why it matters

Every integration in Section 17 is configurable and none runs.

## Suggested fix

An `integration-worker` Edge Function on a schedule (pg_cron with pg_net, like the other workers) claiming
`integration.sync`; Edge Functions for Slack, Teams and SCIM inbound; the verifier schedule; Zapier and Make app
definitions generated from the OpenAPI document and the webhook catalog. Live provider checks wait for owner
sandbox accounts (Section 19.8).
