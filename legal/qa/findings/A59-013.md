# A59-013: Phone tables show two columns and hide the rest

## Finding

Section 11.6 says that below `md` "tables switch to stacked cards with the three most important
fields". At 375 px the Table views keep their columns: All Projects shows Key and Name, with
State, Phase, Jurisdiction and Venue off screen to the right; project Assets shows Asset tag and
Asset class with State and Held by cut; Offers shows "Unnamed person" and a truncated opportunity
name. Some list views do switch to cards (Maintenance, Logistics Shipments), so the behavior
differs page by page. Many pages default to Table even on a phone.

## Steps

1. Sign in as owner@example.com at 375 x 812.
2. Open /demo-productions/projects/all.

## Suggested fix

Implement the stacked-card mode in the shared table below `md`, driven by each collection's
declared primary fields, or default phones to the List view.
