# A53-001: Pseudo-locales format like their own locale

## Finding

`formattingLocale()` in `apps/atlas/i18n/negotiate.ts` and `apps/gateway/i18n/negotiate.ts` says
that pseudo-locales "format like their source" (en-US), but no code calls it. The negotiated
locale (`en-XA` or `ar-XB`) goes to next-intl and to `getFormatters`, and from there into `Intl`.
`Intl` resolves `ar-XB` to Arabic, so under ar-XB the client-formatted dates on My Work read
`13‏/03‏/2027، 10:00 م` (Arabic day-month order, Arabic comma and the Arabic PM marker), while the
server-formatted dates elsewhere on the same page read `Mar 12, 2027, 5:00 PM`. `en-XA` resolves to
generic English, which is close to en-US but not guaranteed to match it.

Found by the A53 pseudo-locale truncation pass (gate 16), at 375 px under ar-XB.

## Why it matters

Development builds only: pseudo-locales never reach a customer. However, ar-XB is meant to test
mirroring with the source copy, so a reviewer cannot tell a layout problem from a formatting
difference. The RTL visual baselines (gate 9) mask dates, so they are unaffected.

## Suggested fix

Pass `formattingLocale(locale)` wherever a locale reaches `Intl`: `getFormatters` in
`apps/atlas/lib/platform/format.ts`, the `useLocale()` callers that build their own formatters
(`components/work/record-collection.tsx` and the others `grep useLocale` lists), and Gateway's
`lib/format.ts`. next-intl's `useFormatter` takes its locale from the provider, so either give the
provider a `formats` and time zone that pin en-US for pseudo-locales, or format through the app's
own formatters. Then add an ar-XB case that expects an en-US date to the pseudo-locale spec.
