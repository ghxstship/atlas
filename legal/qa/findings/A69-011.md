# A69-011: A coverage gap, and a policy helper without its grant

## Finding

The catalog suites 9901 to 9903 try every app table with `org_id`, but only where the shared world holds a victim row.
173 of 504 tables hold none, among them every resource added since the A22 review: legal entities, the IP allow-list,
access review campaigns and items, break-glass sessions, project shares and their scope nodes, price books and cue
calls. Each migration suite checks that another org reads nothing, but none tried to change or delete another org's
rows, or to call the new RPCs with another org's ids.

Closing that gap turned up a defect: `private.visible_project_share_ids()`, the read policy helper of
`app.project_share_scope_nodes`, was revoked from `authenticated` in 1205. Policy helpers run as the caller, so every
signed-in read of that table failed with 42501. It failed closed (nothing leaked) but broke reading a share's nodes
directly, through the API included.

## Fix

- `9909_security_a69_new_resources.test.sql` gives each new resource but cue calls (which need a run of show and a
  record, and whose reads 1180 checks) a Northwind row and checks that another org's Owner
  and a person with no org read, change, soft-delete and delete none of them; that `end_break_glass`,
  `start_break_glass`, `set_org_legal_entity_tax_identifier`, `network_access`, `shared_projects` and
  `shared_project_scope_nodes` refuse or answer nothing for Northwind's ids; and that a share's partner reads the share
  but cannot raise its role, change its end, widen its scope or write the owner's project.
- Migration 1298 grants `private.visible_project_share_ids()` to `authenticated`, and 9909 asserts that every private
  helper a policy calls is executable by `authenticated`.

The cross-tenant checks found no isolation flaw in the new resources.

## Test

`9909_security_a69_new_resources.test.sql` (22 assertions). Without the grant it stops at the first read of the scope
nodes.
