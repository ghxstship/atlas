# A59-025: Sign-up and sign-in polish

## Finding

- Both shells put two Email fields on one card: one above "Email me a sign-in link", and another
  with Password below "or". People type their address twice or into the wrong one.
- Atlas sign-in shows two filled primary buttons (Email me a sign-in link, Sign in with password);
  Gateway styles the password button as secondary. One primary per screen (Section 11.10).
- Gateway's "Create your Gateway account" has no terms or privacy acceptance; Atlas asks for it
  only on the next step (Create your organization).
- The terms link on Create your organization leads to legal pages such as
  /legal/acceptable-use-policy that say "Version 1.0.0-draft is in review and not yet in effect.
  The published text appears here when it takes effect." People are asked to accept text they
  cannot read. This depends on the owner's legal decisions (`legal/OWNER_INPUTS.md`) but the
  sign-up flow should not ship pointing at it.
- Passkeys, Apple and Google sign-in (Section 11.10 item 12) are not offered on either card.

## Steps

1. Sign out; open /auth/sign-in and /auth/sign-up on Atlas (3059) and Gateway (3159).
2. Follow Create your organization's Terms of Service link.

## Suggested fix

One Email field with two actions (link, or reveal password), terms acceptance at account
creation in both shells, and block sign-up until the legal documents are published.

## Fix (A62)

Gateway part. Sign-in and sign-up each have one Email field with the sign-in link as the primary
action and "Use a password" revealing the password field (Section 11.10 rule 12); the Email field
stays mounted, so what was typed carries over, and only the shown method's button submits. Sign-up
asks for the Worker Terms (accepted; legal/index.yaml marks them
`external_party_at_account_creation`) and links the Privacy Policy (read) before any account exists,
for the link and the password alike. The accepted versions travel in the new user's metadata and are
written to `app.legal_acceptances` (personal, with version, time, IP and person) as soon as a session
exists: at once after a password sign-up, or in the link's callback. Gateway serves
/legal/worker-terms and /legal/privacy-policy, whose versions a unit test checks against the
registry.

Still open: Atlas's two Email fields and two primary buttons, passkeys and OAuth on the cards, and
the draft legal text itself (owner inputs). OAuth sign-up, when a provider is enabled, does not yet
pass through the terms box.
