# A65-012: Only email leaves the platform

## Finding

`private.notify` (0520) resolves preferences, quiet hours, digests and SMS consent into deliveries for In App,
Email, SMS and Push. The dispatcher (`packages/platform/src/notifications/dispatch.ts`, the `notification-dispatch`
Edge Function) sends email only:

- **SMS** (Sections 5, 14.5): `twilio` is not installed; no sender, no 10DLC configuration, no inbound handler for
  STOP and HELP (the `STOP` suppression reason exists).
- **Push** (Sections 4.3 item 12, 5, ADR 0030): Compass registers Expo push tokens and handles actionable push, but
  no server sends to the Expo push service. Acting from a push (Section 11.10 item 5, journey 57) works only with a
  locally delivered push.
- **Chat translation** (Section 4.9): the language is stored; no provider (ADR 0026 consequences).

## Why it matters

SMS and push deliveries are created and never leave, so field staff miss shift changes and approvals arrive only
by email.

## Suggested fix

A push sender that claims Push deliveries (`app.claim_notification_deliveries`) and posts the ADR 0030 contract
to Expo, with receipts; an SMS sender behind a port with a Twilio adapter, STOP and HELP inbound webhook, quiet
hours by the recipient's zone; an owner decision on a translation subprocessor.

## Resolution (A101, RRR-147): push reaches parties, and every tap opens its target

A80 built the push sender (migration 1361) and A96 browser push (1533). Both reached only devices registered per
membership, so external parties and marketplace users never received a push, and only an approval request's tap
did anything in Compass. A101 closes the push half of trace row S4.3-15 (ADR 0056):

- `app.person_push_devices` (1670) holds a person's own Compass devices and Gateway browsers. The claim delivers to
  them when the recipient has no device registered with the org and works with it as a party, or the push is a
  marketplace match open to them (`private.person_push_fallback`, 1671); a member's push is unchanged.
- The push data carries the record key of migration 1600. Every Compass tap, the one that launched it included,
  opens that record, else the deep link through `compassPathForIncoming`; the Compass Inbox lists the person's
  notifications and opens them the same way.

`apps/gateway/e2e/push-reach.spec.ts` and the Maestro journey `apps/compass/.maestro/journeys/push-tap-opens-record.yaml`.
