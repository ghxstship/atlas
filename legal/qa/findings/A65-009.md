# A65-009: The internal console does not exist

## Finding

Section 4.10 and Section 6 define `apps/console`: tenant search, plan overrides and feature flags, support sessions
under the Section 8 consent rules, the marketplace moderation queue and abuse reports, system banners and platform
metrics. Section 8 says the console opens a tenant only through a support session the Owner grants, at most 24
hours, every action logged.

There is no `apps/console`. The database has `private.is_platform_operator` (0643) and an append-only
`app.moderation_actions` that only the platform writes (ADR 0032), but nothing writes it. Support sessions
(`app.support_sessions`, 1060) admit only a partner org (`grantee_org_id` is required), so a platform operator has
no consented path into a tenant. System banners for maintenance and incidents have no source; Atlas shows only the
cancellation and View as banners.

## Why it matters

Notice-and-action with statements of reasons (EU Digital Services Act, Section 14.9) cannot be carried out, so
reported listings can never be removed. Support has no audited, consented way to see a tenant.

## Suggested fix

Build `apps/console` (Next.js, SSO-gated to the platform team) with: tenant search; plan overrides and flags over
`feature_flag_overrides`; platform support sessions (extend `support_sessions` with a platform grantee, the Owner's
grant and 24-hour limit kept); the moderation queue over `listing_reports` writing `moderation_actions` with the
statement of reasons and notifying the affected party; a `system_banners` table read by every shell.

## Resolution (A90, RRR-135)

`apps/console` (ADR 0046) is built. Operators sign in with their own XOS account and pass a second factor before any
page, and every console RPC checks `private.require_platform_operator` in the database (1470).

- **Tenant search, plan overrides and flags:** 1471. Billing and identity facts only; records stay closed without a
  support session.
- **Platform support sessions:** 1472. The tenant's Owner grants the platform 1 to 24 hours with a reason; each read
  is logged to the tenant's audit ledger and the console ledger, and the Owner sees each access.
- **Moderation queue:** 1473. Removals and restrictions carry a statement of reasons in parts (Digital Services Act
  article 17), the affected party and the reporters are notified, and another operator decides appeals (article 20).
- **System banners:** 1474. Maintenance, incident and plan notice banners shown in Atlas, Gateway and Compass.
- **Platform metrics** beyond each org's usage are not built: they need a metrics source the platform has not chosen
  (ADR 0046).

Tests: pgTAP 1470 to 1474, `apps/console/e2e`, `packages/data/test/a90-console.test.ts`.
