# A24-006: Gateway role journeys

rating and reply, token signing and advance claim, public profiles and three-tap apply. These
Section 20.2 journeys have no automated test yet:

- 4 (Gateway half): the sealed line-item bid is now covered (vendor-bid.spec.ts); the vendor's PO

its role type, and the demo seed holds only a Draft vendor bid posting.
