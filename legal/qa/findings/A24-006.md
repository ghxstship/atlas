# A24-006: Gateway role journeys

## Finding

Gateway journeys covered the crew path (find, apply, withdraw, sign, upload, message), the vendor's
rating and reply, token signing and advance claim, public profiles and three-tap apply. Journeys 4
(PO acknowledgment), 42, 43, 44, 45, 46, 47, 56 and 60 had no automated test.

## Resolution (A38, RRR-82)

Each journey now runs end to end across Atlas and Gateway against production builds, with the
org's side in Atlas where a screen exists and through the same lifecycle engine and RPCs Atlas calls
where it does not (`apps/gateway/e2e/role-fixtures.ts`), and the party's side in Gateway. Seed
`supabase/seeds/1080_a38.sql` adds the external parties: a staffing agency and its roster, a
contractor studio, an artist and her representing agency, a client, a sponsor, a Finance Lead, a
multi-org person and a vendor company with history. Device steps (Compass scans and punches) are
covered by their server calls, run as the person.

personal onboarding steps carry over by person only (migration 1082, found by journey 42); and an
external company account can start Atlas as itself (migration 1081, journey 60).
