# A54-002: Playbook field targets missing from the OpenAPI document

## Finding

Section 18 gate 14 asks that "every `field` target exists in the schema and the OpenAPI document". A51 checked the
schema half (`canon/import/test/field-targets.test.ts`). The OpenAPI half was not measured.

`canon/import/test/field-targets-openapi.test.ts` now measures it. A target `schema.table.column` is exposed when
an operation tagged `x-xos-table: schema.table` returns a row field named `column` in
`packages/api/openapi/v1.json`. When it isn't, the column map entry's new `api` key has to say either "Exposed as
schema.table.field" (a resource returns it under another name) or "Not exposed: reason".

Measured on main 7ef369f: 823 field targets, of which 805 name a real column (the other 18 are A47-001's owner
rulings). 478 of the 805 were returned by a resource of their table, and 327 were not accounted for.

| ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------- |
| Returned by a resource of the target's table                        | 538     |
| Exposed under another name (`api: Exposed as ...`)                  | 20      |
| Deliberately not exposed, with the reason (`api: Not exposed: ...`) | 191     |
| Not exposed yet (this finding)                                      | 56      |

What A54 fixed:

- **Corrections that add the columns.** Migration 1180's columns now reach their resources, through ADR 0023
  corrections in `packages/api/src/catalog/modules/parts/playbook-field-homes.ts`. These cover shipments (13
  columns), the gate queue, the marshalling log, spaces, purchase orders, vendors, budget lines, change orders,
  cues, runs of show, shifts and inspections. Records get `project_id`, `parent_record_id`, `wbs_code`,
  `description`, `sop_id` and `team_code`. Work orders get `work_order_number`, `location_text`,
  `scope_of_work`, `scheduled_starts_at` and `estimated_hours`. Projects get `anchor_on` and
  `budget_envelope_minor`.
- **Exposed under another name (20).** Some targets are already returned under another name. The activity feed
  returns `actor_person_id` as `actor_id` and the domain event's project, time, subject and type. Record
  dependencies return `predecessor_record_id` as `predecessor_id`. Schedule actuals return
  `actual_start_at`/`actual_end_at`, and work orders return `due_at` for `scheduled_ends_at`. The canon
  Labor Rate Cards are read through `xpms.rate_card_resolved`.
- **Deliberately not exposed (191).** The Production Template store (114, read and written by the Playbook import
  and export). The source workbook's bookkeeping columns on canon tables (status, flag, notes, who and when; 73).
  Two soft-delete markers. The Restricted encrypted TIN. The domain event payload.

## Suggested fix

For each row, either add the field to the resource that reads the table (an ADR 0023 correction while v1 is a
draft, or an additive field after release) and lower the count in `NOT_YET_EXPOSED`, or record in the column map
entry why it stays out (`api: "Not exposed: ..."`). The views and ledgers may be better served as read-only fields
on their parent resource (for example `actual_minor` and `account_code` on budget lines) than as new resources.

## Fix (A63, RRR-108)

All 56 are exposed; none needed a "Not exposed" reason, because none is Restricted or internal (the vendor bank
account returns only the last four digits, which `app.vendor_bank_accounts_masked` shows to every vendor reader).
`NOT_YET_EXPOSED` is empty, and gate 20 attributes every change to its correction.

| How                                                                                                            | Targets |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------- |
| A column of the resource's own table added to its read shape (ADR 0023 correction)                             | 22      |
| A new resource for the table: `cueCalls` (`app.cue_calls`), `projectAuthorities`, `canonStdEmergencyProtocols` | 11      |
| A read-only field on the parent resource, recorded as `api: Exposed as ...` in the column map                  | 23      |

That is 33 targets returned by a resource of their table and 23 under another name:

- **Columns added:** `domain_event_id` on activity events; `counterparty_id` on engagements; `website_url` and
  `contact_email` on organization profiles and `preferred_name` on person profiles (read and accepted on update);
  the canon category `xyz`, four counterparty type columns, three department columns, three element columns, and
  the Document & Asset Library and SOP Library organization, project, file size and storage URL.
- **New resources:** `/cue-calls` (list, get, create, update, delete; one call per department of a cue),
  `/project-authorities` (list, the responder agencies of the project's venue jurisdiction) and
  `/canon/std/emergency-protocols` (list and get, each with its numbered `steps`).
- **Read-only fields from views, ledgers and sidecars**, read once per page with `in` (the `enrich` option of
  `handlers/support/table.ts`): `actual_minor` and `account_code` on budget lines (`app.budget_line_facts`),
  `account_code` and `cost_center_id` on change order lines, `approved_at` and `approved_by` on change orders,
  `sent_at`, `invoiced_minor` and `paid_minor` on purchase orders (NULL-aware over the distinct matched invoices),
  `closed_at` on incidents, `meal_out_at` and `meal_in_at` on time entries (Break Start and Break End punches),
  `assignee_membership_ids`, `progress_pct`, `permit_identifier` and `inspection_result_enum_id` on records,
  `bank_account_last4` on vendors, `normal_balance` on canon GL accounts and `account_code` on canon category GL.
