# A38-006: Journey 44's year-end and legal data

## Finding

**1099-NEC export.** Section 4.4 and journey 44 call for a 1099-NEC data export for US payees. Tax
forms are collected (`app.tax_forms`, W-9 with an encrypted TIN and its last four), and payments
are recorded per counterparty, but nothing aggregates a calendar year's payments to US
independent contractors with a W-9 on file, and no Atlas page or API operation exports it. Gateway
already tells payees "US payees get 1099-NEC data from each organization at year end".

**Pay-transparency rules.** `private.guard_opportunity` refuses publishing Contractor, Crew and
Staff work without a pay range where `private.pay_transparency_rules` says one is required, but the
table ships empty: no jurisdiction requires a range, so the refusal never fires outside tests. The
rows are legal reference data (jurisdiction, effective date, citation) that must come from the owner
or counsel; A38 did not invent them. contractor.spec.ts inserts a local test rule for the journey
and deletes it afterwards.

## What the journey covers today

The refusal without a pay range and the publish with one, in Atlas; the milestone delivered in
Gateway and accepted by the Producer; the invoice against it from the contractor's company.

## Proposal

A15: `app.form_1099_nec_rows(p_org_id, p_year)` (security definer, finance capability, Restricted
step-up for the TIN) returning payee, TIN last four, address and the year's applied payments for
US payees under INDEPENDENT_CONTRACTOR engagements, a Finance, Year-end page with a CSV export, and
pgTAP. Owner: the pay-transparency rule set to load (for example the US states with posting laws in
force), with citations, as a platform data migration.

## Fix (A43, RRR-87)

- **1099-NEC.** W-9s gain the form's name and mailing address, which the payee keeps on Gateway's tax
  documents page (`app.set_tax_form_address`). `app.form_1099_nec_rows(org, year, include_tin)` lists the
  year's approved USD payments from $600 to people and accounts engaged as independent contractors, with
  whether a W-9 and its address are on file. The full TIN is a Restricted export: data.export.restricted and
  step-up (aal2), audited as `finance.form_1099_nec_exported`. Atlas, Finance, Year-End lists the payees and
  downloads the file in the IRS FIRE "B" record field layout (CSV); the API serves the rows without TINs
  (`form1099Nec.rows`).
- **Pay transparency.** `private.pay_transparency_rules` is keyed by jurisdiction code (no longer canon
  only), carries effective dates, whether remote roles are covered and `needs_counsel_review`; a rule covers
  every jurisdiction beneath its code, and the latest rule in force wins. Seeded for CA, CO, WA, NY, IL, MD,
  HI, MN, VT, MA, NJ and DC, every row marked for counsel review and shown so on Settings, Gateway and
  marketplace. Opportunities gain `is_remote`; publishing without a range where a rule applies is refused
  (XR001 pay_range_required).
- **Open owner decision.** The seeded list, its dates, citations and remote coverage wait on counsel; employer
  size thresholds are not modeled. Canon has no US state below Florida, so on-site postings in the seeded
  states apply once canon adds those jurisdictions; remote postings in the US are covered now.
