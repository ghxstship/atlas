# SIM-005: No native Live Activity

## Finding

Journey 52: "A crew member clocks in from the Live Activity, takes a break and clocks out without opening
Compass." Compass's `LiveActivity` (from `@xos/ui-native`) is a card on the Today screen styled like a Live
Activity. It works on the simulator: after Clock In it shows the running timer, Start Break, End Break and
Clock Out, and each punch replays through `app.record_punch` (journey 10 flow). But nothing exists outside
the app: `app.json` has no widget extension or Live Activity plugin, there is no ActivityKit target, no App
Group for the extension to share the punch queue, and no push token for remote Live Activity updates. The
journey's "without opening Compass" part cannot run.

## Decision needed

1. Approve a pinned Expo module or config plugin for a widget extension with ActivityKit (Swift), and a
   native rebuild.
2. How the extension punches without the app: App Intents that write to a shared App Group queue the app
   drains on next launch (offline-safe), or a direct authenticated call to `app.record_punch` from the
   extension.
3. Whether Atlas pushes Live Activity updates (shift end, supervisor notes) through APNs.

## Resolution (A42, RRR-86)

Fixed. Owner decision 2026-10-17: @bacons/apple-targets 5.0.0 (pinned) for the widget extension and a
local Expo module for ActivityKit. expo-widgets was rejected because it delivers a button press only
in-process, so a press while Compass is not running would be lost.

- `apps/compass/targets/ClockActivity`: the SwiftUI Live Activity on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic
  Island (compact, minimal and expanded). It shows on shift since, the break with its own timer, and the
  worked time, with Start or End Break and Clock Out buttons.
- `ClockPunchIntent` (a LiveActivityIntent linked into the app, so it runs in the app's process) queues
  the punch in the App Group `group.pro.ghxstship.compass` and moves the activity to its new state.
  Compass drains the queue into the sync outbox as the same `app.record_punch` an in-app punch sends,
  keyed by the intent's UUIDv7, so a repeated drain replays rather than duplicates.
- `modules/compass-live-activity` starts the activity at clock in, updates it on a break and ends it at
  clock out. The in-app Live Activity card on Today stays the clock where Live Activities are
  unavailable.
- On the iOS simulator (iPhone 17 Pro): after Clock In, the compact Dynamic Island showed the clock icon
  and the running worked time. The expanded island showed On Shift, Since 9:55 AM, the project, the
  timer, Start Break and Clock Out. Start Break pressed in the island and End Break pressed on the Lock
  Screen each reached the server as `clock_punches` rows without Compass in front, and the activity
  switched to On Break with a break timer and frozen worked time, then back.
- Not decided here: pushing Live Activity updates through APNs (shift end, supervisor notes). No push
  token is requested.
