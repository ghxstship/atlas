# SIM-001: Future-dated demo time entries block clock-in

## Finding

Seed `supabase/seeds/0113_people_crew.sql` gives the demo Field Lead two completed time entries on
2027-03-12 and 2027-03-13, because the demo event is set in March 2027. A clock-in creates an open time
entry (no clock-out yet), and `time_entry_overlap` correctly treats an open entry as running until it
closes, so every clock-in today overlaps those future entries. The server refuses the punch and Compass
shows it as "Needs your decision".

The overlap rule is right: worked time cannot be in the future. The demo data is wrong.

## Repro

1. Sign in to Compass as field@example.com on a fresh local reset.
2. Tap Clock In. The punch replays through `app.apply_sync_mutation` to `app.record_punch` and is refused
   with `time_entry_overlap`.

## Suggested fix

Anchor worked time (time entries, timesheets, attendance) in the past relative to the reset date, or move
the whole demo world's dates relative to `now()`. Many Playwright and API tests assert the fixed March 2027
dates (projects, schedule, show, logistics, hospitality, safety specs; a04p, a09, a13 and a16 handler
tests), so the change must update them together.
