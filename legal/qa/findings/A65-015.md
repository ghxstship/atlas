# A65-015: Lifecycle steps recorded but never carried out

## Finding

- **Trial** (Section 14.7): 14 days of Pro without a card, then Access. No trial logic exists; Stripe trials only
  count as active (0560).
- **Day 90 deletion and certificate** (Section 14.7): `org_cancellations.purge_after` is recorded (1063) but no job
  deletes the org or issues a deletion certificate; there is no `deletion_certificates` table.
- **Ownership transfer** (Section 14.7): new Owner accepts and passes MFA, old Owner becomes Admin. No RPC,
  operation, UI or `ownership_transfers` table.
- **Org deletion** with step-up and a 7-day recovery window: cancellation needs the typed name, but there is no
  step-up and no separate deletion with recovery.
- **Dunning** (Section 16): `private.run_dunning` sets read-only on day 21 and notifies, but the Atlas banner shows
  only the cancellation state (`org-banners.tsx`).
- **Downgrades** (Section 16): over-limit objects are not made read-only with an explanation; new ones are refused.
- **Metered billing** (Section 16): usage events never reach Stripe meters.
- **Enterprise invoicing** (Section 16): no net-30, purchase order number or bank transfer configuration.

## Why it matters

Data the policy promises to delete at day 90 stays forever, and an Owner who leaves cannot hand the org over.

## Suggested fix

A daily lifecycle job (trial expiry to Access, day-90 purge with a certificate row and email); transfer and
deletion RPCs with step-up; the dunning banner; over-limit read-only through the plan-limit view; Stripe meter
reporting where a price is metered; Enterprise invoice settings on the subscription.

## Resolution (A107, downgrades)

The downgrade item is fixed in migrations 2070 and 2071 (RRR-152, ADR 0081). When a trial ends or a plan goes down,
the projects past `active_projects` and the seats past `seats` or `field_members` become read-only: Owners' seats
first, then what the Owner chose in Settings, Billing and Plan, then the oldest. The read-only row guard refuses a
person's writes to them with `plan_limit_read_only`, naming the plan, the limit and how to lift it. The guard reaches
everything of a read-only project, however deep: budget lines, PO lines, comments and attachments on its rows, custom
field values. Atlas shows the same explanation in banners. Triggers on subscriptions, console plan overrides, projects, memberships, roles and
plan limits recompute the set, so archiving, ending a membership or upgrading lifts the guard at once. Nothing is
deleted (pgTAP 2070, journey `apps/atlas/e2e/a107-plan-limits.spec.ts`). The other items of this finding are outside
A107.
