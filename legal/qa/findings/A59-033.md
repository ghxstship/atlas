# A59-033: Worker framing shown to buyers

## Finding

The engagement Overview property card is the same for every role type: Organization, Project,
Role, Dates, "Your rate" and "Pay basis". For the client's and the sponsor's engagements it reads
"Your rate $950.00, Pay basis Day Rate" (and a crew role title as the Role), as if the client were
paid by the org. Clients and sponsors pay or contribute; Section 4.5.8 gives them approvals, change
orders, run of show, entitlements and proof of performance, not a rate.

Their Settings also list Work Details ("Your work details come from your profile: skills,
certifications, rates and availability") and Payout and Tax ("Where organizations pay you"),
which apply to crew, staff, contractors and vendors.

## Steps

1. Run `legal/qa/visual-review/scripts/run-workflows.sh client` (journey 46) or `sponsor`.
2. Sign in to Gateway as client@example.com, open the engagement Overview and Settings.

## Suggested fix

Choose the property rows per role type (contract value and billing terms for clients, package and
entitlements for sponsors), and hide Work Details and Payout and Tax for standings and role types
that are never paid.
