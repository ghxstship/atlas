# A59-021: Lifecycle gate copy ignores later states

## Finding

The vendor's completed engagement (state chip "Complete", stepper Offer, Onboarding, Engaged and
Close-out all checked) shows on its Deliveries tab: "This opens once the engagement is Active.
Finish onboarding to get there." with a "See onboarding" button. Onboarding is long finished, so
the copy is wrong and the button leads nowhere useful. The same engagement's Purchase Orders tab
lists POs 44 and 38 with "Acknowledge" buttons, so some tabs treat the engagement as live while
others treat it as not yet started. The Work > Engagements list files the same engagement under
"Past" although its dates are Jan 10 to 12, 2027, in the future.

## Steps

1. Sign in to Gateway as vendor@example.com.
2. Work > Engagements > Audio Engineer - A1/FOH > Deliveries.

## Suggested fix

Gate tabs on "before Active" only, show a read-only history after Complete, and group engagements
by state rather than calling a future-dated Complete engagement "Past".

## Fix (A62)

Every closed operational tab now renders one component, `AreaClosed`, whose copy comes from
`closedArea(state, tabs)` (Section 8.7 time box): before Active it names the way there (Onboarding
where the role type has it, otherwise the organization starting the work); at Complete it says the
section has closed and that invoices and documents stay open for 18 months, with See invoices; at
Canceled it links to the documents. The action always opens a tab in the viewer's own set (the old
button pointed at Overview while saying "See onboarding"). Purchase Orders offers Acknowledge only
while the engagement is Active or Closing, and Work > Engagements groups closed engagements as
"Complete or canceled" by state, so a future-dated Complete engagement is no longer called Past.
