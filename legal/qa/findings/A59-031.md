# A59-031: Compass header spacing

## Finding

- Screens pushed from More (Settings, Delete Account, Offline Queue, Aurora, Forms, Workforce,
  the "is in Gateway" pages) show the back button at the top and then about 120 pt of empty space
  before the large title, so a third of the first screen is blank on an iPhone 17 Pro.
- Sign-in: the "Use Password" link is indented about 16 pt from the left edge that every other
  element uses.
- Settings > Theme labels the system option "Device", while Atlas and Gateway say "System".

## Steps

1. Sign in to Compass, open More > Settings.

## Screenshots

- `legal/qa/visual-review/evidence/A59-031-1.jpg` (Settings), `A59-031-2.jpg` (sign-in)
- Full resolution: `legal/qa/visual-review/shots/compass/field/`

## Suggested fix

Use the native large-title header (title in the navigation bar, collapsing on scroll), align the
sign-in link with the field, and use "System" in all shells.
