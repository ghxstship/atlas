# A65-030: Measurement against objectives is missing

## Finding

Section 4.10: waste, energy, water and transport figures per project, reported against the Bible sustainability
tags, NULL shown as Unmeasured; gate 8 audience, earned media and sentiment figures entered or imported against the
objectives recorded at gate 1. Ticketing attendance imports exist (0636). There is no project figures table, no
earned media or sentiment figures and no store of gate 1 objectives.

## Why it matters

Gate 8 (Amplify) has nothing to measure against.

## Suggested fix

A `project_measurements` table (metric from `xpms.dim_metric`, value nullable, unit, source) and
`project_objectives` set at gate 1; report pages and the Unmeasured rendering.
