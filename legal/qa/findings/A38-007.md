# A38-007: The crew lifecycle stops at approved time

## Finding

Journey 41 has the freelancer paid after timesheet approval. For a person engaged as an
individual (no company account), no path exists:

- `app.generate_self_billed_invoice` refuses with `invoice_needs_account` ("Self-billed invoices
  bill an external account; the engagement has none"), `app.submit_engagement_invoice` refuses the
  same way, and `app.payments.counterparty_id` is required;
- the engagement's party is immutable (`engagement_party_immutable`), so a freelancer who later
  creates a company account cannot attach it;
- the Gateway Crew tab set has no Invoices tab, which is where the self-billing agreement and the
  payment tracker live, so crew cannot agree to self-billing or follow pay on the engagement;
- self-billing is off by default (`finance.self_billing_enabled`) and has no visible control in
  Atlas settings.

Payroll export covers employees; independent contractors engaged as individuals are the gap.

## What the journey covers today

crew-lifecycle.spec.ts chains every other stage with a person new to the org: crew call,
application, offer accepted, Blocked until the background check passes and verified in Atlas,
shift accepted and worked (record_punch as the person), timesheet submitted in Gateway and approved,
both sides rated, and the credential gone from the person's Gateway at close.

## Proposal

Let self-billed invoices and payments name the engaged person as payee when the engagement has no
counterparty (a nullable `payee_person_id` with a check that exactly one payee is named), add the
Invoices tab to the Crew and Staff tab sets, and give `finance.self_billing_enabled` a control on
Settings, Legal entities and fiscal.
