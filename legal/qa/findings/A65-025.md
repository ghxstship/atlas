# A65-025: Import steps 1 to 3 are partial

## Finding

Section 4.6.2: upload .xlsx, .csv or a Google Sheets link through the Drive integration; detect sheets, header
rows and ranges, proposing a target per sheet; suggest mappings from the Playbook column map, header similarity and
the assistant.

- The worker reads only `workbook.worksheets[0]` (`packages/data/src/import-worker.ts`).
- `Google Sheets` is an `ImportSource` value with no import path.
- Suggestions come from header similarity only; the Playbook column map (`canon/map/playbook-columns.yaml`) and
  `aurora.mapColumns` are not consulted.

(.xls was dropped by owner decision 28.)

## Why it matters

Tenants bringing an older multi-sheet playbook, the case Section 4.6.2 names first, must split it by hand.

## Suggested fix

Detect every sheet and create one job per sheet with a proposed target; add a Drive picker through the storage
connector once the integration worker runs (A65-013); seed suggestions from the Playbook column map; call
`aurora.mapColumns` when the org has AI on.
