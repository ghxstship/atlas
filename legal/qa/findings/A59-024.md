# A59-024: No explanation for a bad invite

## Finding

Opening `/auth/invite/not-a-real-token` while signed out redirects to
`/auth/sign-up?next=/auth/invite/not-a-real-token`: "Create your Atlas account" with no message.
The token is only checked after the person has created an account, so they learn the invitation is
unknown, used or expired too late. `apps/atlas/ia/routes.config.ts` describes this route as
"preview and accept an invitation", and Section 11.10 item 11 asks errors to say how to fix. The
valid path (accept invitation) is walked in `shots/workflows/atlas-crew-atlas` and works.

## Steps

1. Sign out, open /auth/invite/not-a-real-token.

## Suggested fix

Show the System template with "This invitation link is not valid any more. Ask the person who
invited you to send a new one." and keep sign-in available.

## Resolution (A61, RRR-106)

api_public.invite_token_open tells the invitation page, before sign-up, only whether a token is still an open invitation. A closed link shows "This invitation link is not valid any more" with next steps and Sign in.
