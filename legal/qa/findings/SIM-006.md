# SIM-006: Asset units are invisible at "assigned" reach

## Finding

The Field role holds `assets.asset.read` and `assets.custody.write`, but at "assigned" reach.
`asset_units_read` admits only `private.orgs_with_capability('assets.asset.read')`, which counts orgs at full
reach, and asset units are org-level records with no project, so for a Field member no unit is visible at all
(REST returns `[]` for `asset_units` and `asset_identifiers` as the Demo Field Lead). The sitemap gives Field
the Asset scan mode, so on the simulator the Field Lead scanned AUD-0042 (GTIN 09501101530003), a unit in the
Field Lead's own custody, and Compass answered "Unknown code sent to the desk to identify" and queued a
scan intake row.

The same journey run as the gear desk (the Producer, manager reach) works end to end: look up, check out,
return, custody ledger (Maestro `desk-asset-custody`, journey 19).

## Decision needed

What "assigned" reach means for org-level gear. Options:

1. Units whose current custody is the person (their membership or engagement), plus units whose home venue is
   a venue of a project they can read; a policy helper taking the unit id (ADR 0016 item 14).
2. Or give Field full read on assets (`asset_units` holds no restricted columns) and keep custody writes
   assigned.
3. Or drop the Asset mode from the Field sitemap entry and leave gear to the desk.

## Resolution (A42, RRR-86)

Fixed, option 1. Migration 1111 adds `private.asset_unit_assigned_to_me(unit)`, which takes the unit id
(ADR 0016 item 14). It is true when the unit's current custody is the caller (membership or operational
engagement) or a project where the caller holds `assets.asset.read`, and false for an API-key session
without the assets scope. `asset_units` and `asset_identifiers` admit those units beside org-wide
readers, and `app.resolve_asset_code` answers the same way, so a Field member reads the gear assigned to
their project or checked out to them and no other. pgTAP `1111_field_asset_visibility`. Run as the Demo
Field Lead's session on the seeded stack, the read returns the six units in their custody or on their
project, AUD-0042 and its GTIN among them. The new Maestro flow `field-asset-scan` scans AUD-0042 by its
GTIN as the Field Lead.
