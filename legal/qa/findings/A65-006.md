# A65-006: Editing at spreadsheet speed is not there yet

## Finding

Components that carry spec behavior exist in `packages/ui` but no app renders them, and the behavior is absent:

- **Co-editing** (Sections 5 and 11.7): `RichTextEditor` with the Yjs collaboration extension is used by no app;
  there are no `doc_snapshots` or `doc_updates` tables. Journey 33 tests a refused stale save, not a merge.
- **Live updates and presence** (Section 11.7): no Supabase Realtime subscription in Atlas or Gateway. Show mode
  polls every 2 seconds (`apps/atlas/components/show/runs.tsx`), against the 500 ms cue sync target (Section
  11.11). Presence appears only in workforce chat.
- **History tab on every record built on the DiffViewer** (Section 4.10): records show their state history only;
  `DiffViewer` is unused.
- **Selection and bulk edit** (Section 11.7): `DataTable` has no multi-select, X, Shift-click or Cmd-A;
  `BulkActionBar` is unused; there is no bulk update path.
- **Undo and redo** (Section 11.7): no 50-action stack or Cmd or Ctrl+Z; `UndoToast` appears on a few views only.
- **Quick add** (Section 11.7): `QuickAdd` with chrono-node is used by no create field.
- **Context menus** (Section 11.7): `ContextMenu` is unused; no right-click or Shift+F10 on records.
- **Hover cards on record keys** (Section 11.7): `HoverCard` is used only for canon terms.
- **Filter builder**, **file previewer** and **View Transitions**: unused or absent.
- **Custom objects** (Section 4.6.3): the record peek offers no comments, attachments or activity, and there is no
  per-plan custom object limit.
- **Exports everywhere** (Section 4.6.4): reports and saved views export; collection pages do not.
- **Archive versus delete** (Section 4.10): archive exists only for projects, notifications, groups and help desk
  requests; records and other entities can only be deleted to Trash.

## Why it matters

These are the interaction model of Section 11.2 and 11.7 and several leadership targets in Section 11.11
(create and classify in 10 s, approve 20 in 60 s, cue sync under 500 ms).

## Suggested fix

Sequence it: Realtime subscriptions for lists and records (shared hook in `packages/ui`), then multi-select and
bulk edit in `DataTable` with a transactional bulk RPC per resource, then the undo stack, context menus and hover
cards, then Yjs co-editing for SOPs and documents with snapshot tables. Each step can land alone.

## Resolution, in part (A115, RRR-160)

Selection, context menus and hover cards now behave the same in the table, list, board and calendar
views:

- `DataTable` moves rows with J and K as well as the arrow keys (single keys follow
  `me.shortcuts_enabled`). `RecordList`, `Board` and `Calendar` take a selection with DataTable's
  gestures (a list row's checkbox, X and Shift+X, Cmd or Ctrl+click, Shift+click, Cmd or Ctrl+A,
  Esc) and an `onItemContextMenu` for right-click, Shift+F10 and the Context Menu key
  (`packages/ui/src/lib/selection.ts`, `packages/ui/test/selection.test.tsx`).
- The record collections, the collection kit, assets, finance and My Work select for bulk edit in
  those views through the existing bulk RPCs and open the same `useRowMenu` menu as their table
  rows; the crew boards and calendars open it too, and a record page's title row opens the record's
  menu (Copy link and the changes).
- The 400 ms hover card (`components/hover/use-hover-cards.tsx`, timing in `lib/hover/intent.ts`)
  shows on list rows, board cards and calendar events, on the shift, invoice, asset and
  collection-kit tables, on My Work, on Activity links and on the records a record page names; a
  key alone is read on demand (`recordCardAction`).
- The Inbox selects with X and Shift+X, an item's checkbox or a modified click, and Done and Snooze
  act on the selection at once.

Journeys: `apps/atlas/e2e/a115-hover-cards.spec.ts` and the A115 tests in
`apps/atlas/e2e/a81-keyboard.spec.ts`. Selection in Gateway and in collections without a bulk RPC
is not part of this change.
