# A65-005: Search finds pages, not work

## Finding

Section 11.7 search supports quoted phrases and the operators `kind:`, `state:`, `phase:`, `dept:`, `urid:`,
`owner:`, `project:`, `is:blocked`, `due:<7d`, groups results by type, and shows the last 50 records each user
viewed before they type. Section 11.10 item 1 has search find records, people, pages, settings and actions, and
Section 5 puts pgvector semantic search over documents and SOPs in the stack.

The command menu (`apps/atlas/components/shell/shell-command.tsx`) lists navigable sitemap pages and help articles
only. A59-015 (in progress: A60 to A62) adds record and people results and a few verbs. Beyond it:

- no operator grammar or quoted phrases;
- no record of recently viewed records;
- individual settings are not searchable (Section 11.11 Settings target "any setting reached in 1 search");
- no `vector` column or embedding anywhere; full text exists only for the handbook (0804);
- Gateway and Compass have no record search.

## Why it matters

Search first is the first shared habit in Section 11.10. Without operators and recents the 11.11 targets for
records and settings cannot be met.

## Suggested fix

A search RPC over records, people and documents that reads with the caller's own RLS, parsing the operator
grammar in `@xos/ia` or `@xos/data`; a `recent_views` table (or reuse of `activity_events`) for the last 50;
setting keys and labels in the command index; embeddings for documents and SOPs in a `vector` column filled by a
worker, with the semantic query behind the same RPC.
