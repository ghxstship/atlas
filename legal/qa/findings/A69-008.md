# A69-008: Link tokens in logged paths

## Finding

`observe` writes one completion record per request with `url.path`, and Sentry events carry the path in `extra`. The
redactor drops query strings and replaces JWTs, bearer headers and `xos_live_` keys, but several links carry their bearer
token in the path itself:

- Gateway `/advance/xadv_<64 hex>` and `/sign/xagr_...` or `xdoc_...`;
- Atlas `/auth/invite/<token>`, which serves invite tokens `xinv_...` and join links `xlnk_...`;
- calendar feed tokens `xcal_...` and SCIM tokens `xos_scim_...` wherever they are echoed.

None matched a redaction pattern, so they reached the log drain and Sentry in clear.

## Impact

Anyone who reads logs or error reports (staff, the log drain, the error tracking subprocessor) could pick up working
links. A join link admits anyone who opens it to the org with its role; an advance or signing link needs its 6-digit
access code too, which A69-001 made guessable; invite tokens also need the invited, confirmed email.

## Fix

The redactor replaces `xadv_`, `xagr_`, `xcal_`, `xdoc_`, `xinv_`, `xlnk_` and `xos_scim_` tokens of 16 or more hex
characters with `[token]`, in logs, traces and error reports alike. The Edge bundle, which carries the redactor, is
regenerated.

## Test

`redact.test.ts` checks every prefix and the advance, signing and invite paths.
