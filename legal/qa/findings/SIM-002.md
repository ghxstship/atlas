# SIM-002: Queue decision sheet shows internals

Opening a refused clock punch in Offline Queue shows "Your version" as
`monotonic at: 1297914530.3655 · boot id: 01a1478a-…`. A crew member needs "Clock in at 10:51 PM at
Northwind Summer Series". Render each RPC's payload through a per-RPC summary (punch kind, wall time,
project) and keep the raw fields out of the sheet.

## Resolution (A39)

`apps/compass/src/domain/queue-summary.ts` turns each queued write into one sentence chosen per RPC
(`app.record_punch` reads "Clock in at 10:51 PM, Northwind Summer Series"; kiosk punches, shift accept and
claim, incident report, photo and party, credential and asset scans, cue calls, inspections, logistics and
workforce writes each have their own), and the server's version of a conflict reads the same way. Arguments
that feed no sentence never show, so the monotonic reading, boot id, PIN, local file paths and ids stay out.
The queue cards show the sentence too. Covered by `apps/compass/test/queue-summary.test.ts`, which also checks
that every sentence key exists in en-US and es-US and that no rendered sentence contains an id or a device
field. Checked on the iPhone 17 Pro simulator: the refused punches read "Clock in at 10:51 PM, Northwind
Summer Series" in the card and in "Your version".
