# A59-008: Avatar overlaps the next date

## Finding

In record lists the right side of each row shows the kind chip, the next date and the owner's
initials. The initials chip ("DP", "DF") is positioned over the end of the date, so rows read
"Mar 10, 2027, [DP] AM" and "Mar 9, 2027, 3 [DP] 'M": the hour is hidden on every row with a
time. Rows without an owner show the full date. Section 11.10 item 4 asks every row to show the
next date; here it is unreadable.

## Steps

1. Sign in as owner@example.com at 1280 px.
2. Open /demo-productions/work/all-records (List view).
3. Compare the date column of NWS-1 to NWS-18.

## Suggested fix

Give the date and the avatar their own grid columns (or a flex gap) instead of absolute placement,
and truncate the date with an ellipsis only when space runs out.
