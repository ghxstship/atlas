# A59-023: Primary gate action text cut off

## Finding

On the project Overview at 375 px, the gate card's footer puts "1 blocking criterion open." and
the primary button "Pass gate 2 and enter Advance" (with its shortcut hint) on one row. The button
is narrower than its label, so the text is cut on the left and right: it reads "s gate 2 and enter
Advance" and the keyboard hint is half visible. The label also has six words where Section 11.10
allows three for a button.

## Steps

1. Sign in as owner@example.com at 375 x 812.
2. Open /demo-productions/p/NWS/overview.

- `legal/qa/visual-review/evidence/A59-023-1.jpg`
- Full resolution: `legal/qa/visual-review/shots/atlas/owner/013-demo_productions_p_NWS_overview.phone.*`

## Suggested fix

Stack the footer below `sm`, hide the shortcut hint on touch screens, and shorten the label to
"Pass gate 2" with the destination phase in the dialog.

## Fix (A60, RRR-105)

- The gate card's footer wraps: on a narrow card the action takes a full-width line under the
  blocking count.
- The Overview button reads "Pass gate 2" (three words, Section 11.10); the Gates tab's dialog
  still names the phase it enters.
- A button's shortcut hint is hidden on touch screens (pointer: coarse).
