# A65-028: Section 15 beyond en-US and es-US

## Finding

- **Beta locales**: fr-FR, de-DE, it-IT, pt-BR and ja-JP are to ship marked Beta. `locales` lists en-US and es-US
  only; no catalogs exist. (`packages/i18n/REVIEW.md` now records their state.)
- **Canon labels**: translatable "through `xpms.dim_locale` and translation tables". `dim_locale` exists; no
  translation tables, so canon labels render in English in es-US.
- **Names**: optional given and family names are absent (`people.full_name`, `preferred_name` only).
- **Addresses**: no per-country formats or validation (`libphonenumber-js` and address metadata are not installed,
  though ADR 0001 lists libphonenumber).
- **Phone numbers**: E.164 only on SMS consent; other phones are free text, never shown in national format.
- **Units by jurisdiction** and **viewer time zone toggle**: units never follow the project jurisdiction
  (`locale.units` is inert, A65-001); schedule views have no toggle to the viewer's zone.
- **Exchange rates**: no scheduled ECB fetch; rates are entered by hand (`app.fx_rates`).

## Why it matters

International customers see English canon terms and US-shaped forms.

## Suggested fix

Generate the five Beta catalogs (machine-assisted, marked Beta in the picker until reviewed); add canon label
translation tables keyed by code and locale; add name, address and phone formatting with libphonenumber and the
address metadata; a daily ECB fetch into `fx_rates`; the units and time zone toggles.

## Resolution

- **A78:** names, postal addresses and phone numbers (`@xos/i18n/formats`, libphonenumber).
- **A97 (RRR-142, ADR 0053):**
  - **Beta locales:** fr-FR, de-DE, it-IT, pt-BR and ja-JP ship marked Beta, load per locale and fall back to en-US
    key by key. Their catalogs come from the Google Translate pipeline once the owner sets up credentials.
  - **Canon labels:** translation tables `xpms.canon_vocabulary` and `xpms.canon_label`, read through
    `xpms.canon_labels` (1541). es-US is seeded from the catalogs; the rest come through the pipeline.
  - **Exchange rates:** a daily ECB fetch into `fx_rates` with recorded failures (1542). Converted figures show
    their rate date, cross rates and stale rates.
  - **Units and time zones:** `locale.units` follows the project jurisdiction, and `me.time_zone_display` shows
    schedule times in the viewer's zone (1540).
- **A139 (RRR-202, ADR 0115):**
  - **Addresses:** venue, vendor and org legal entity addresses lay out and validate by country in Atlas, and the
    Gateway W-9 address checks against the US format. Vendors gain a mailing address with its country and legal
    entities a registered address (2320). The database refuses a new or changed postal code that does not fit its
    country, with the same patterns as `address.ts`.
  - **Phones:** the work phone is read into E.164 by `toE164` on save in Atlas, Gateway and Compass and shown with
    `formatPhone` in the crew directory and settings. Stored values whose form is unambiguous were rewritten to E.164,
    and a new or changed one must be E.164 (2321). Stored values that do not fit either check are listed in
    `private.contact_format_exceptions`.
