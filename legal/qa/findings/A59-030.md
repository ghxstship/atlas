# A59-030: "Demo Field Tech clocked in on a time entry"

## Finding

Activity rows read "Demo Owner issued a credential · NWS", "Demo Field Tech clocked in on a time
entry · NWS", "Atlas verified a domain of the organization". The object is a generic article plus
kind ("a credential", "a shift") set in the monospace code font, rather than the record's key and
title, so the line cannot be scanned or opened. Identical rows repeat (three "Demo Producer changed
the state of a shift" in a row). Section 11.10 item 2 reserves monospace chips for codes.

## Steps

1. Sign in as owner@example.com, read Home > Recent activity and the NWS Overview.

## Screenshots

- `legal/qa/visual-review/evidence/A59-030-1.jpg` (NWS Overview, Recent activity)

## Suggested fix

Name the record ("clocked in on NWS-17 Stagehand · Load-in") as a link in the body font, and
collapse consecutive identical events.

## Resolution (A61, RRR-106)

Activity names what each entry acted on (a shift or time entry by its record key and title, a credential by number and holder, an engagement by party, an approval by document type and reference, a domain by host, a shift without a record by its role and start) in the body font, links records to the peek, and folds runs of identical events into one line with a count.
