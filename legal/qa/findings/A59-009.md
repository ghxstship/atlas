# A59-009: UUIDs as row keys

## Finding

Each maintenance and damage row shows a 36-character UUID (for example
`01a15673-0725-7bb5-9482-0e505cbbbe98`) in the monospace key slot. At 1280 px the column is narrow,
so the UUID wraps over five lines and makes every row 70 px tall; on a phone it is a second line
under the title. Other lists show a short key (NWS-12, CR-000001) or nothing. Section 11.10 item 2:
codes appear next to a plain name, never alone, and these are not human codes at all.

## Steps

1. Sign in as owner@example.com.
2. Open /demo-productions/assets/maintenance and /demo-productions/assets/damage.

maintenance record, and never fall back to the row id.
