# A24-008: Journeys not run in Firefox and WebKit

## Finding

Section 18, gate 6 runs the critical journeys in Chromium, Firefox and WebKit plus mobile viewports.
The Atlas and Gateway configs defined `chromium` and a Pixel 7 `mobile` project; docs runs Chromium
and phone width.

## Fix (A38, RRR-82)

- **Projects.** Both configs define `chromium`, `firefox` and `webkit` (Desktop Chrome, Firefox and
  Safari devices) plus `mobile`. `E2E_ENGINES` narrows the desktop engines; each engine takes its own
  executable override (`PLAYWRIGHT_CHROMIUM_EXECUTABLE`, `PLAYWRIGHT_FIREFOX_EXECUTABLE`,
  `PLAYWRIGHT_WEBKIT_EXECUTABLE`). Gateway's config also starts Atlas for the cross-app journeys,
  and Atlas's starts the `XOS_AI_FAKE=1` development server for journeys 35 and 36.
- **Run locally** against production builds, with the Firefox 155 and WebKit 27.2 builds already on
  the machine (Playwright 1.64 pins Firefox 157 and WebKit 27.2; nothing was downloaded).
- **Engine-specific failures fixed**, all in the specs, none in the product:
  - Firefox aborts a `goto` or `reload` that lands on Next's post-action route refresh
    (NS_BINDING_ABORTED), and WebKit reports it interrupted. The shared `engine-test.ts` makes
    `page.goto` and `page.reload` let the page go quiet first and retry while that refresh cuts them
    off, for the test's page and every page of a context a spec opens.
  - axe sampled a drawer mid-fade in Firefox: Atlas's `expectAccessible` waits for the page to go
    quiet, paint and finish its finite animations.
  - WebKit follows Safari's keyboard model: the keyboard journey reaches buttons with Option+Tab.
  - WebKit never treats the re-filtering category picker as stable enough to click; the places
    journey picks with Enter, the picker's keyboard path.
  - A controlled lookup field typed into before hydration (slow engines): the custody journey waits.
  - WebKit runs the same steps about twice as slowly (axe on long pages most of all): its tests get
    three times the spec's timeout.
- **Results.** First full passes: Atlas Firefox 121 of 133, Atlas WebKit 130 of 142 (two skipped by
  design), every failure one of the items above or a timeout. After the fixes, every spec file that
  had a failure passes on rerun in its engine (and in the other two), and the full Gateway suite and
  the full Atlas Chromium and phone suites are rerun on a fresh reset. On main 049334d merged: Gateway
  89 of 89 across the three engines and phone, then 31 of 31 Chromium and phone after the last change;
  Atlas Chromium and phone 145 of 147 (one skipped by design) with one failure, the crawl's unknown
  saved view answering 200 instead of 404 since A41 added `app/[org]/views/loading.tsx` (the loading
  boundary streams a 200 before `notFound()` runs; A41's stack, not changed here).
- **Live pages.** A show run or chat keeps a request open and never reaches networkidle, so the quiet
  wait before a navigation and before axe is capped at five seconds (`settle` in `engine-test.ts`),
  and looping animations are not awaited. A36's import journey needs `E2E_SUPABASE_SERVICE_KEY`, the local stack's development key.

## CI proposal

`packages/testing/ci/proposed-jobs.yml` gives the `e2e` job an `engine` matrix dimension
(`E2E_ENGINES` per job, docs on Chromium only), installs that engine, builds and starts Atlas for
Gateway's job, and names the artifacts per engine. About three times the minutes of a Chromium-only
job; `.github` is unchanged pending the owner's decision.
