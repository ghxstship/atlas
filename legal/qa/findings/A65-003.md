# A65-003: Personal work details have nowhere to live

## Finding

Section 4.5.6 personal setting 10, Work Details, holds "emergency contacts, uniform sizes, dietary and
accessibility needs for hospitality (sensitive; shared per engagement only with consent), travel profile (loyalty
programs, seating; passport details stored as Restricted)". Section 4.3 item 13 puts emergency contact and uniform
sizes on the Compass profile, and Section 4.4.4 lists them in the crew onboarding packet.

On a fresh reset no column in `app` or `private` stores an emergency contact, a uniform or shirt size, a dietary or
accessibility need, a loyalty program, a seat preference or passport details. `app.people` has `full_name`;
`app.person_profiles` has the public profile fields. Atlas Work Details shows only `workforce.work_phone`; Gateway
Work Details points to the profile; the Compass profile shows certifications only.

## Why it matters

Emergency contacts are a safety requirement on site, and hospitality cannot fulfill dietary or accessibility needs
it cannot record. Section 9 field-level encryption of government identifiers has nothing to encrypt because
passport details have no home.

## Suggested fix

- A person-owned `person_work_details` table (and child rows for emergency contacts and loyalty programs), read only
  by the person, with per-engagement sharing through `profile_shares` consent.
- Passport number and expiry as Restricted columns encrypted with pgcrypto under a Vault key, masked through a
  `*_masked` view and registered in `private.column_classifications`.
- Forms in Atlas, Gateway and Compass Work Details; read access for the engaging org's hospitality and safety roles
  only while consent stands.
- Optional given and family names beside the full name (Section 15 names), in the same change.

## Fixed

A78 (RRR-123, WP4) added the Work Details tables in migration 1340, owner-only with sharing per engagement and the passport number encrypted and masked (Restricted). pgTAP 1340 and the Work Details journey cover them, and the journey passes in CI. The release record of 2026-10-25 found this file still marked Open.
