# A59-005: Settings content starts about four screens down on a phone

## Finding

The Settings template keeps its left section list at phone width and stacks it above the content.
Org settings has 40 sections, so the list is about 1,700 px tall and the section the person opened
(for example Numbering) starts below it: the Numbering page is 2,218 px tall with its two fields in
the last 500 px. Every section switch lands at the top of the list again. Section 11.6 says Atlas
is fully usable from 375 px, with drawers below `lg`.

## Steps

1. Sign in as owner@example.com at 375 x 812.
2. Open /demo-productions/settings/numbering.
3. The first four screens are the section list; the Numbering form is at the bottom.

- `legal/qa/visual-review/evidence/A59-005-1.jpg` (whole Numbering page, scaled)
- Sheet: `legal/qa/visual-review/sheets/atlas-owner.phone.light.jpg` routes 180 to 230

## Suggested fix

At phone width show the section list only on /settings itself, and on a section page replace it
with a back link (or a select) above the form.
