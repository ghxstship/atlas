# A59-026: Destinations that do not exist yet

## Finding

For external crew the Compass More tab lists Timesheets, Per Diem, Travel and Lodging and
Onboarding Items first. Each opens a page that says, for example, "Timesheets is in Gateway. Open
Gateway to see Timesheets. Compass shows it here in a later release." with an Open Gateway button.
Four of the seven More items are therefore links out of the app, drawn with a plain chevron where
the other items have icons. Section 4.5.7 gives crew these as Compass destinations; if they are not
built, the menu should not promise them.

For the internal Field Tech, Profile and Certifications shows only the role and "Certifications
are in Gateway.", sending an internal member to the external app.

Several empty states offer "Refresh" as their only action instead of the teaching action of
Section 11.10 item 10: Compass Schedule ("No upcoming shifts ... Refresh"), Atlas People > Chat
("No conversations yet ... Refresh") and Site Operations > Guest Check-In ("No approved guests ...
Refresh").

## Steps

1. Sign in to Compass as crew@example.com; open More, then Timesheets.
2. Open Schedule.

## Suggested fix

List only built destinations (group the Gateway links under one "Open in Gateway" row), and give
each empty state the action that fills it ("Find open shifts", "Message a teammate").
