# A59-014: Home asks people to do what their role cannot

## Finding

Home shows "Get Demo Productions ready" with setup steps and a "Your plan: Demo Productions is
on the Team plan" card to every role. The steps are trimmed by role but still name actions the
person cannot take: the Viewer (read only) is asked to "Create your first project", "Review
jurisdictions" and "Set the schedule" (0 of 3 done); the Field Lead is asked to "Set the
schedule". The plan card has no action for anyone but the Owner. Section 11.7 scopes the onboarding
checklist to a new org's setup.

The Viewer, who has no project shared, then meets a loop: My Work says "Nothing is assigned to you
yet" with "Open Projects"; Projects says "No projects are shared with you yet. Ask a producer to
add you to a project" with "View my work", which leads back to My Work.

## Steps

1. Sign in as viewer@example.com.
2. Read Home, then follow My Work > Open Projects > View my work.

## Suggested fix

Show the setup checklist and plan card only to Owner and Admin. For a Viewer with nothing shared,
make the empty state name who can share a project (the Owner or a Producer) instead of linking
between two empty pages.

## Resolution (A61, RRR-106)

The setup checklist and plan card show only to Owners and Admins. A reader who cannot create a project and has none shared is told who can add them (the org's Owners, Admins and Producers, by name) and is sent Home, ending the My Work and Projects loop.
