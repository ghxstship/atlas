# A65-007: Keyboard first, partly

## Finding

Section 11.2 principle 1: Cmd or Ctrl+K everywhere (done), every action has a shortcut, `?` shows the shortcut
sheet, G then a letter navigates modules, C creates a record in the current context, J and K move, X selects.
Section 4.10 adds a global Create menu (+ and C) offering every kind the person may create. Section 11.7 lets people
rebind shortcuts with conflict and reserved-key checks. Section 3.15 makes `packages/schemas/shortcuts.yaml` the
single source of key bindings, the shortcut sheet, command hints and docs.

Found:

- `packages/schemas/shortcuts.yaml` does not exist. Bindings are hard-coded in `shell-frame.tsx`, `aurora-center.tsx`,
  `inbox-view.tsx`, `RecordList` and the `SHORTCUTS` constant in `help-panel.tsx`.
- No G sequence handler in any shell.
- The `C` shown on create buttons is a label: `Button`'s `shortcut` prop renders keys and binds nothing.
- No global Create menu.
- Personal settings, Keyboard Shortcuts holds only `me.shortcuts_enabled`, which nothing reads; `ShortcutEditor`
  is unused. `shortcut_bindings` (Section 7.3) does not exist.

## Why it matters

Keyboard first is the first design principle and the basis of the 11.11 target "create and fully classify a
record in under 10 s, keyboard only".

## Suggested fix

Create `shortcuts.yaml` with a generator for the binding map, the shortcut sheet and command hints; a shell-level
dispatcher (G sequences, C, +) driven by it; the global Create menu listing creatable kinds from capabilities;
`shortcut_bindings` per person with `ShortcutEditor` in Personal settings; honor `me.shortcuts_enabled`.
