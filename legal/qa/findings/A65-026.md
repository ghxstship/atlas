# A65-026: Reseller exit and billing are incomplete

## Finding

Section 4.6.1:

- **Exit**: "A client org can leave its partner and become a direct customer. Its data, members and brand stay
  intact." No RPC, operation or UI clears `parent_org_id` or moves billing to the client.
- **Standing grants**: partner access may come from "a standing grant written into the partner and client
  agreement, recorded as a record_grants row with scope and expiry". Only Owner-granted support sessions exist.
- **Billing**: "The partner bills its clients through Stripe Connect (Standard accounts)". Partner client prices are
  recorded (1060); no Connect billing exists. (The platform-to-partner side is decided: Stripe Billing invoices,
  ADR 0033 decision 21.)

## Why it matters

A customer is locked to its reseller, which the spec explicitly rules out.

## Suggested fix

`app.leave_partner(org)` by the client Owner (ends support sessions and grants, keeps brand as the org's own,
starts a direct subscription); standing grants on `partner_agreements` producing `record_grants`; Connect Standard
onboarding for partners, decided with the owner.
