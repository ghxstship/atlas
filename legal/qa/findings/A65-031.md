# A65-031: Smaller Gateway requirements not met

## Finding

- **Supplier diversity certifications** (Section 4.4.1): optional, self-declared, with separate consent, used
  only where an org runs a program. No table or field exists.
- **Background checks through Checkr** (Sections 4.4.4, 5, 14.9): `app.background_checks` holds the result and
  reference; no Checkr connector runs the FCRA workflow.
- **Spend authority before publishing** (Section 4.4.3): open in ADR 0019; no publish path calls
  `private.approve_under_spend_authority`.
- **Swipe to save and pass** on phones (Section 11.12 Discover) and the **profile strength meter** and **social
  preview image** (Section 11.12 Reputation): not found.

## Why it matters

Each is small, but together they are visible to external users and to orgs with diversity or screening
programs.

## Suggested fix

A person- and org-owned diversity certification table with its own consent; a Checkr connector behind the
integration framework; spend authority in the opportunity publish RPC once the owner settles ADR 0019's open item;
the Gateway gestures and profile strength meter.
