# A59-015: Search first, but only for navigation

## Finding

The command menu's field says "Search pages and settings", and that is all it returns:

- "incident" returns the Incidents page, the Incidents report, "Locations and Venues Defaults"
  (a weak match) and a Help article; none of the four seeded incidents (NWS-32 to NWS-34) appear.
- "approve" returns only Settings > Approval Policies; there is no "Approve timesheets" action and
  no pending approval, although the Owner has one in the Inbox.

Section 11.10 item 1 says search finds records, people, pages, settings and actions ("approve
timesheets", "new incident"), and Section 11.7 lists record operators (`kind:`, `state:`) and the
last 50 viewed records before typing. On phones the menu opens as a top dialog rather than a bottom
sheet (item 7).

## Steps

1. Sign in as owner@example.com, press Cmd or Ctrl+K.
2. Type "incident", then "approve".

## Suggested fix

Add record and people results (grouped by type, as Section 11.7 describes) and a small set of
verbs ("New incident", "Approve timesheets") routed to their create or approval flows.
