# SIM-004: No account deletion behind Delete Account

## Finding

Journey 38: "Delete a crew account from inside Compass and receive confirmation." In Compass, Settings,
Delete Account opens `<gateway>/settings/account` in the browser (on the simulator, Safari). That Gateway
page renders the generic `settings.account` section and has no deletion control. No RPC, Edge Function or
job deletes a person's account: the database knows account lifecycle only as a retention purge method
(`app.purge_method` 'Account Lifecycle', 0500) and a privacy statement (0501). So the journey cannot finish
anywhere, in Compass or in Gateway.

App Store review (guideline 5.1.1(v)) requires an app that creates accounts to let people start deletion
inside the app; a link out is accepted only when it reaches a working deletion flow.

Not exercised on a disposable account, because there is nothing to call.

## Decision needed

1. The deletion contract (Section 14.7 account lifecycle): what is erased at once, what is kept for legal
   holds and pay records, the recovery window, and the confirmation the person receives.
2. The server side: an Edge Function with the service role (deleting `auth.users` needs it) behind step-up
   authentication, refusing while the person holds an open engagement or unsubmitted time.
3. Then Compass calls it from Settings with a confirmation sheet, and Gateway gets the same control.

## Resolution (A42, RRR-86)

Fixed. The deletion contract is in the A42 completion report as proposed ADR text.

- Migration 1112: `app.account_deletion_preview()` (blockers, and what is kept and why) and
  `app.delete_my_account(p_confirm_email)`. In one transaction it erases the person's profile data and
  name, soft-deletes the person, ends their memberships and external account memberships, cancels their
  Onboarding, Blocked or Active engagements, records a Completed Erasure DSAR in every related org and
  deletes the auth user. It keeps time and pay records, tax forms, engagement records, the org's own
  records (without the name), the audit trail and the erasure request. It refuses with XR001 and deletes
  nothing under an active legal hold over the records it changes, while the person is clocked in, or
  when the person is the only Owner or Account Owner. It is idempotent per session through
  `private.account_deletions`. pgTAP `1112_account_deletion`.
- `@xos/data/account-deletion` is the shared client (Compass now, Gateway's settings next). It removes
  the person's profile media before calling the RPC.
- Compass: Settings, Delete Account opens Settings, Account. It shows the blockers or what is kept, asks
  for the sign-in email and a device check, then hold to delete, then the confirmation. A sign-in with no
  membership or engagement gets Delete Account and Sign Out on its own screen.
- On the iOS simulator against stack xos4-a42: a throwaway account created on Gateway's sign-up page was
  engaged as crew on the demo project. Compass showed the legal-hold refusal while a hold stood. After
  the hold was released it deleted the account and showed "Your account is deleted. Organizations told:
  1." The auth user is gone, the person has no name, the engagement is Canceled and the org holds a
  Completed Erasure request.
- Maestro `account-delete` covers a throwaway account from sign-up to confirmation.
