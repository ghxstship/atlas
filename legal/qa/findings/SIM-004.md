# SIM-004: No account deletion behind Delete Account

## Finding

Journey 38: "Delete a crew account from inside Compass and receive confirmation." In Compass, Settings,
Delete Account opens `<gateway>/settings/account` in the browser (on the simulator, Safari). That Gateway
page renders the generic `settings.account` section and has no deletion control. No RPC, Edge Function or
job deletes a person's account: the database knows account lifecycle only as a retention purge method
(`app.purge_method` 'Account Lifecycle', 0500) and a privacy statement (0501). So the journey cannot finish
anywhere, in Compass or in Gateway.

App Store review (guideline 5.1.1(v)) requires an app that creates accounts to let people start deletion
inside the app; a link out is accepted only when it reaches a working deletion flow.

Not exercised on a disposable account, because there is nothing to call.

## Decision needed

1. The deletion contract (Section 14.7 account lifecycle): what is erased at once, what is kept for legal
   holds and pay records, the recovery window, and the confirmation the person receives.
2. The server side: an Edge Function with the service role (deleting `auth.users` needs it) behind step-up
   authentication, refusing while the person holds an open engagement or unsubmitted time.
3. Then Compass calls it from Settings with a confirmation sheet, and Gateway gets the same control.
