# A38-001: Additional insureds have no schema

## Finding

Journey 43 asks that the vendor onboard with an insurance certificate "whose additional insureds are
verified". `app.insurance_certificates` (migration 0330, corrected API body in the Vendors catalog)
holds insurer, policy number, coverage type, limit, currency and validity only. There is no list of
additional insureds, no expected insureds per org or project, and no verification that compares the
two. The schemas package still describes the resource as "naming the required additional insureds",
and xpms carries an `additional_insured` column in the vendor directory sheet, but nothing in `app`
stores or checks it.

## What the journey covers today

vendor-lifecycle.spec.ts covers the rest of journey 43 end to end: the W-9 filed in Gateway and
verified in Atlas before Active (refused until then), the certificate on file shown as current on
the vendor's Compliance tab, the dock check-in, the invoice, the signed lien waiver and the Atlas
scorecard.

## Proposal

A03 or A16, in their migration range: `app.insurance_certificate_insureds` (certificate, insured name,
endorsement kind) and `app.required_additional_insureds` per org or project, with a derived
`insureds_verified` on the certificate (every required insured named) that the Compliance tab and
the vendor's onboarding step read. The Aurora extraction target gains a repeating `insureds` line
so journey 36's reader proposes them too. Size: one migration, one pgTAP file, two UI touches.
