# A59-003: Five project tabs are invisible on a laptop screen

## Finding

The project tab bar has 20 tabs in two groups (Plan, Build). The bar is an `overflow-x: auto` nav
1000 px wide at a 1280 px viewport with 1419 px of tabs, and it shows no scroll affordance (no fade,
arrow or More menu). Measured with `scripts/probe.mjs project-tabs`:

| Viewport | Tabs fully hidden                                   | Cut in half |
| -------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ----------- |
| 1280     | Procurement, Documents, Reports, Activity, Settings | Budget      |
| 1440     | Reports, Activity, Settings                         | Documents   |
| 1920     | none                                                | none        |

Opening one of the hidden tabs by URL does not scroll the bar, so on Procurement, Documents,
Reports, Activity or Settings no tab is shown as current. At 375 px only the first six Plan tabs
show and the Build group label sits off screen. Section 11.10 asks that any record is reachable
within 3 clicks; project Settings and Reports have no visible way in at 1280.

## Steps

1. Sign in as owner@example.com at 1280 x 800.
2. Open /demo-productions/p/NWS/overview: the bar ends at "Budg".
3. Open /demo-productions/p/NWS/procurement: no tab is highlighted.

## Suggested fix

Collapse overflow into a "More" menu (as Linear does), or at least show edge fades and scroll the
current tab into view on load.
