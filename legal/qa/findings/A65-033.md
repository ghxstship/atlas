# A65-033: Domain provisioning is manual

## Finding

Section 12: custom Atlas and Gateway domains through the Vercel Domains API with DNS verification and automatic
TLS; custom sending domains with SPF, DKIM and DMARC through Resend; white-label settings revert to default branding
at the next billing cycle after a downgrade, with notice.

`app.org_domains` records domains with plan gating and TXT verification (`packages/integrations/src/identity/
verifiers.ts`), but no client calls the Vercel Domains API or the Resend domains API. Brand resolution checks the
`white_label` feature at read time, so a downgrade reverts branding at once, with no notice.

## Why it matters

Every custom domain needs manual work by the platform team, and a downgrade changes a customer's brand without
warning.

## Suggested fix

Provisioning adapters for Vercel Domains and Resend domains behind ports (the production domain and accounts are
owner decisions, ADR 0033 decisions 4 to 6); keep the brand until the period ends and notify the Owner.
