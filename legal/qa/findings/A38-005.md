# A38-005: Sponsor asset approvals stop at the org

## Finding

Journey 47 has the sponsor's assets approved in Gateway. `app.sponsor_assets` and
`app.transition_sponsor_asset` (0492) exist, but the review is org-only (`vendors.entitlement.write`)
and there is no `api_external` view of sponsor assets and no RPC for the sponsor to submit or answer
one. Gateway's Asset approvals tab reuses the client approvals list, and `app.request_client_approval`
refuses any engagement that is not a Client one (`client_approval_client_only`), so for a sponsor the
tab can only ever be empty. The tab's comment still says no asset table exists.

## What the journey covers today

sponsor.spec.ts: the sponsor accepts the package in Gateway, sees the entitlement open and then
fulfilled, receives the proof-of-performance report, and that report is attached as gate 8
evidence for G8.SPONSOR_OBLIGATIONS. The asset review runs through `app.transition_sponsor_asset`
with separation of duties (the creator's own approval is refused); the sponsor's tab shows its
empty state.

## Proposal

An `api_external.sponsor_assets` view for the engaged sponsor (assets of its entitlements), an RPC
for the sponsor to submit a revision and to approve or return an asset the org proposes, and the
Asset approvals tab rebuilt on them.
