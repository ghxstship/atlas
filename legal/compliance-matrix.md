# Compliance Matrix

> **Draft for review by counsel before launch.** This matrix is a draft. It takes effect when the Owner approves it, and its effective date is that date of approval.

This matrix maps every row of Section 14 (Compliance) of the XOS 4.0 build specification to the artifact that implements it, the owning agent, the CI gate or test from Section 18 that will prove it, and its current state.

**States.** _Drafted_ means the governing artifact is complete as a draft in `legal/` and the row needs no product implementation beyond publishing at /legal. _Planned (Wave n)_ means the row depends on product or pipeline work that lands in wave n; any legal artifact it relies on is already drafted and named in the artifact column. Gate numbers refer to the numbered checks in Section 18. "Journey n" refers to Section 20.2. "Acceptance n" refers to Section 20.1.

A21 updates this matrix at every wave exit. A row moves to _Verified_ only when its gate or test passes in CI and in staging.
