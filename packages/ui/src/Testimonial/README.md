# Testimonial

Testimonial shows a customer's words with a required disclosure line.

`disclosure` is required and states the speaker's connection to the publisher: a customer, a partner, or someone who received anything of value. A testimonial runs only when a real customer said it and consented in writing; nothing here is invented, bought or written by staff. Until such a statement exists, the page ships without this component.

The preview uses the fictional tenant Northwind Live, as every XOS preview does, and its disclosure line says so.
