Atlas is where your organization plans, runs and closes its productions. Everything you can open is in the sidebar, grouped by what it is for. Groups that match your role start expanded.

## Move around

- Press **Mod+K** or **/** to open the command menu, then type the name of any page.
- Use **J** and **K** to move up and down a list, and **Enter** to open the focused record in the side peek.
- Press **Esc** to close the peek. The list keeps your place, your filters and your scroll position.
- Press **Mod+.** for focus mode, which hides the sidebar while you work.

## Pin what you use

Pin a page to **Favorites** from the sidebar. A record that is open in the peek is pinned with the page, so it opens straight back to that record.

## Get help where you are

Press **?** or choose **Help** in the header on any page. The help panel lists the articles for the page you are on, the keyboard shortcuts, what is new, and a way to contact support. Guided tours are offered once and can be replayed from Help at any time.
