## Classes, units and lots

An asset class describes a kind of equipment. A unit is one item with its own tag; a lot is a counted quantity of identical items.

## Check out and return

Scan a unit or lot in Compass to check it out to a person or project, and scan it again to return it. Each movement is written to the custody ledger, which can never be edited.

## Maintenance and damage

Log maintenance to keep units fit for use, and report damage with photos. A unit under maintenance or damaged cannot be checked out.
