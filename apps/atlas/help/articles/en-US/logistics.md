## Shipments

A shipment records goods moving to or from a site: carrier, contents, expected arrival and state. Check it in when it arrives and release it when it leaves.

## Dock schedule

The dock schedule assigns time slots at each loading dock. A slot can hold one delivery at a time; a vendor checks in to their slot from Compass.

## Marshalling yard log

The yard log records every vehicle that enters and leaves the marshalling yard, with times, so you can see what is waiting and for how long.
