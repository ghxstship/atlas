- **Assertion rank:** how strongly a canon value is backed, from a ratified standard down to an unverified claim.
- **Baseline:** a frozen copy of a schedule, used to measure slips.
- **Canon:** the shared XOS reference of phases, gates, record kinds, roles and other standard values.
- **Engagement:** the working relationship between your organization and an outside person or company.
- **Gate:** the check between two phases of a project; blocking criteria must be met to pass.
- **Peek:** the side panel that opens a record without leaving the list.
- **Phase:** a stage in a project's life, from concept to close.
- **Provenance:** where a value came from and how far it can be trusted.
- **Record:** any tracked item in a project, such as a task, decision or deliverable, with a kind and a state.
- **Record kind:** the type of a record, which decides its fields and states.
- **Refusal:** when Atlas declines an action because a rule forbids it. The message names the rule and how to proceed.
- **Unpriced:** a cost with no price yet. It is never treated as zero, and totals show how many are unpriced.
- **URID:** the unique reference of a canon element, such as a catalog item.
