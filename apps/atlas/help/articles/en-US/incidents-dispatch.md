## Incidents

Anyone on site can report an incident from Compass, even offline, with photos and their location. It is filed once when the device reconnects. A critical incident cannot be closed without a sign-off.

XOS has no near-miss type. Report something that could have hurt someone or caused damage but did not as an incident at Level 5 - Advisory, with What happened starting with the words Near miss. Filter Incidents by that severity to find them later.

## Dispatch board

The dispatch board shows open incidents by state and on the map. Assign responders, move incidents through their states and follow them to close. The table view lists every incident for the keyboard and screen readers.

## Crisis alerts

Announcing a crisis alert on the Emergency codes page tells the people clocked in on or assigned to the project and its dispatchers, through their Inbox, push, email and, for those who turned it on, text, and the alert shows how many were told. The Inbox has it at once; push and text reach phones within minutes, and email can take longer for a large alert. It also sends the all-clear to them. XOS does not contact emergency services: in an emergency, call your local emergency number.

## Emergency codes and radio channels

Emergency codes are the plain-language calls your organization uses in an emergency. Your organization keeps its own copy of the Standard Library's codes: open a code to change its label, the emergency it announces or its place in the list. Radio channels list who talks on which channel. Compass keeps the radio channels and the Standard Library's codes offline; your organization's changes to a code show in Atlas.
