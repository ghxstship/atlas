The partner console is for organizations that resell XOS to their clients.

- **Client Orgs**: create a client org, set its plan and invite its Owner. You cannot see a client's data until its Owner grants you a support session.
- **Default Brand**: set the brand your client orgs inherit.
- **Usage**: client usage rolls up here.
- **Wholesale Billing**: your clients are billed to you at wholesale once the platform records your partner agreement.
