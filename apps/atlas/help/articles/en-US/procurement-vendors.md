## Buy

1. **RFQs**: invite vendors to bid. Bids arrive sealed in Gateway and unseal at the deadline; award the one you choose.
2. **Purchase Orders**: raise a PO to commit spend. A PO above your approval limit routes to the next approver, and you cannot approve your own PO. The vendor acknowledges it in Gateway.
3. **Receipts**: receive goods against a PO, including by scan in Compass. A duplicate receipt is refused.
4. **Invoice Matching**: vendor invoices match against the PO and its receipts before approval.

The **Catalog** binds canon items to your vendors and their prices.

## Vendors

- **Directory** and **Classes**: the vendors you work with and what they supply.
- **Prequalification**: vet a vendor before you engage them. Someone other than the requester decides.
- **Insurance Certificates**: collect a certificate from each vendor, with the additional insureds you require. Aurora can read a certificate and show its confidence in each field before you confirm.
- **Scorecards**: score vendors after each engagement.
- **Sponsor Entitlements**: list what each sponsor is owed and track its fulfillment.
