## Advance packets

An advance packet gathers what a talent team or vendor must tell you before the show. Send it by secure link; the recipient completes it without an account.

## Submissions and riders

Completed packets arrive as submissions. Riders list the technical and hospitality requirements of an act.

## Reconciliation

Reconciliation compares each rider requirement with the venue's capability documents. A requirement the venue cannot meet, or one marked UNKNOWN on a critical item, shows as a gap for the next gate.

## Crew roster

Each party puts its people on its engagements: a vendor's technicians, a band and its touring crew, a sponsor's activation staff. Crew Roster lists them by party with their role, arrival, review state and whether they accepted. Accept or return each submitted entry, one by one or in bulk with one reason; you never review an entry you added or submitted. Open a person to see what they share, read and logged when you open it. Allergies show as on file unless you may read them.

## Allocations

Allocations say what each party and person is given for a production: badges, parking passes, radios and carts, catering, rooms, trips and per diems. A coordinator requests one for a party's pool or for one person; a reviewer who did not request it approves or declines it. Approving equipment holds it from class stock, a lot or the order it arrives on, and the dialog shows how many are available for those dates and how much of the allowance remains. Split a party's pool to its people, then issue each item: issuing confirms the person has it. The status reads held, ordered, received, issued and returned from the rows that record them. Holds that no longer fit what can supply them show in a banner. Radio allocations take channels, and Print radio hand-out gives the sheet people sign.

## Guest passes and guest emails

Each guest can have a pass type (an Access category of the project, such as GUEST or VIP), a table, a host from the project's people, and an email address. When a guest is approved they get a pass code; Print pass and Download pass give their A6 pass with the code the door scans. Guests have passes, not credentials.

Email guests sends the guests you choose an invitation, their pass as a PDF, or a message with a subject and text, in English or Spanish. Guests without an email, and, for a pass, guests not yet approved, are skipped, and addresses that asked your organization to stop are not emailed. Every guest email comes from your organization, with an unsubscribe link and the postal address. Each send lists what happened to every guest.
