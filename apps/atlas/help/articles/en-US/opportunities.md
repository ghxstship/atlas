## Postings and the pipeline

Publish a requisition as a posting in Gateway. Applicants move through the pipeline on a board: shortlist, interview, offer. A posting in a pay-transparency jurisdiction cannot publish without a pay range.

## Pools

Pools are groups of people you have worked with and want to call on again.

## Onboarding and engagements

An accepted offer becomes an engagement. Onboarding collects documents and checks; an engagement stays blocked until every required check passes.

## Ratings

Both sides rate each other at close. Neither sees the other's rating until both have rated or 14 days pass.

Ratings are always on. **Prompt members to rate parties** in Settings, Gateway and Marketplace only decides whether members see the engagements waiting for their rating. Turning it off never hides or removes a rating, and you can still rate from the engagement. Ratings are moderated only for abuse or personal data, never for their score.

## Registration pages

A registration page is your public page for crew and volunteers. It fills one of your Talent pools and never names it. Write the title and introduction, choose the pool and the minimum age, add questions or apply a question template, then publish. Ask about the work, never about a protected attribute. Copy the page's link to share it, or invite the people already in the pool.

People register from their own Gateway account, and you approve or decline them in the queue. Approving makes them a member of the pool. An org that needs adults sets the minimum age to 18.

## Inviting people into a pool

On a Talent pool, invite someone by email, or use Import people to send a whole list. Nothing creates a person: they sign in to Gateway with that address and accept on Home. A standing rate is informational and needs the payroll capability. Only pool managers see invitations, since they hold the contact details of people who never joined.

## Agreement templates and batches

On Onboarding, write your own agreement with merge fields such as the person's name, the shifts and the rate. XOS supplies no wording. On a project's Engagements tab, choose Generate agreements, pick the template and the people, and read the preview. A person with a missing field is left out until it is filled. Create, then Send.

People read the full text in Gateway and sign with their typed name, or decline with a reason. Countersign the signed ones from the batch; an Automatic Electronic Signature item then passes. Once a template has been used its wording is fixed, so duplicate it to change it. Without the payroll capability, a text with a rate shows no body and no hash.
