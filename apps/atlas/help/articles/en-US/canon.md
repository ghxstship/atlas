The canon is the shared vocabulary of every project: departments, disciplines, categories and elements; the nine phases and their gates; the 26 record kinds; roles; jurisdictions with their permit rules; and metrics. It loads with XOS and is read-only.

## Browse

Each canon page lists its entries with their definitions, grouped by their parent (disciplines by department, elements by category). Hover over or focus a phase or gate chip anywhere in Atlas to see its definition.

## Extend

**Org Extensions** add values your organization needs beyond the canon. Someone other than the requester approves each extension.
