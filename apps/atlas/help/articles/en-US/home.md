Home answers one question: what needs you now.

## Today

Today lists the records assigned to you or waiting on your decision, ordered by when they are due. Use **J** and **K** to move and **Enter** to open a record in the peek.

## Gate readiness

Gate readiness shows each active project's next gate and the blocking criteria still open. Open a project to see its gates in full.

## Alerts

Alerts collects the warnings that need attention across your projects, such as overdue permits, expiring certifications and incidents that are still open.

## Recent

Recent lists the records you opened most recently, so you can return to them in one step.
