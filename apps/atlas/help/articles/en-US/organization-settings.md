Settings hold the choices that apply to your whole organization: its profile and brand, members and roles, modules, localization, security, billing and integrations. You see only the settings your role may change.

## Locked values

Some settings can be locked at organization level, such as the time zone. A locked value cannot be overridden by a workspace, project or person, and the setting shows where its value comes from.

## Members, teams and roles

Invite members and give each a role. Teams group members for sharing, such as sharing a saved view with the Production team. Roles come with a set of capabilities; custom roles are available on plans that include them.

## Modules

An admin can turn a module off. It then disappears from navigation, search and the command menu for everyone in the organization.
