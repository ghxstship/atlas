## Reference

- **SOPs**: write and publish the procedures your team follows. People acknowledge a published SOP. If someone saved a newer version while you were editing, your save is refused so nothing is overwritten.
- **Documents**: upload the files your team needs. A project's documents are also on its Documents tab.
- **Verbiage Library**: save clauses and wording you reuse.

## Workforce

- **Feed**: post updates by section. A post can require reading; people acknowledge it, and you see who has not.
- **Handbook**: publish handbook articles.
- **Forms**: build forms with conditional logic, required photos and signatures. Crew can complete them in Compass, offline, and each submission becomes a record of the right kind.
- **Training**: build short courses for your crew.

## Public form links

A Published form without a photo, signature or GPS stamp can have a **Public link**. People outside the organization open it with an access code and answer without an account. A response becomes a record only when someone approves it. Responses show in Submissions with a Public link label.
