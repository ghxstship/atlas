## Fulfillments

A fulfillment is one hospitality need met: a room, a meal, a transfer. Assign it, then confirm it when the supplier confirms.

## Banquet event orders

A banquet event order (BEO) tells the caterer exactly what to serve, when and where. Generate it from the catering fulfillments of an event and send it for confirmation.

## Accommodation blocks

An accommodation block holds rooms at a hotel for a date range. Assign people to rooms from the block and see how many remain.
