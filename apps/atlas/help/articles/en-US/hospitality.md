## Fulfillments

A fulfillment is one hospitality need met: a room, a meal, a transfer. Assign it, then confirm it when the supplier confirms.

## Banquet event orders

A banquet event order (BEO) tells the caterer exactly what to serve, when and where. Generate it from the catering fulfillments of an event and send it for confirmation.

## Accommodation blocks

An accommodation block holds rooms at a hotel for a date range. Assign people to rooms from the block and see how many remain.

## Flights

Hospitality > Travel lists every flight booked under a Travel fulfillment, across the org or in the project's Travel section. Legs a traveler flies within 24 hours of the last landing are one journey, so each row shows Leg 1 of 3 and the journey's final arrival. Each time shows in the zone it was entered in. The built-in views show Upcoming flights, those In flight, or All; save one as your own view, and export the rows on screen as CSV, Excel or PDF. The fare never appears.

## Transfers

A transfer is one vehicle run: a pickup and a drop-off, a vehicle, a driver and the passengers riding. Type the drop-off time you expect; drive times are not computed and no map is drawn. Manage the project's vehicles from Vehicles. XOS refuses a driver, vehicle or passenger booked on two transfers at once, more passengers than seats, and the driver riding as a passenger. Each transfer shows Unassigned, Assigned, Underway or Done, and warns of a Tight turnaround (the driver's previous drop-off plus the turnaround in Settings > Scheduling) or a flight that lands after the pickup. The resource schedule lays transfers out by driver or by vehicle for the week. The driver is told when they are assigned.

## Green rooms and dressing rooms

Book a space at the project's venue for an act or a team, or use Book from set time: in is filled in 90 minutes before the set and out 60 minutes after it ends, and you can change both. An exclusive booking holds the room alone; shared bookings may overlap, and the overlaps to check list them together with the most people in the room at once against its capacity. Set moved shows when the set's planned time leaves the booking.

## Rooms by night and acts without rooms

The Matrix view of accommodation blocks shows each block's rooms assigned of held on every night. A night with free rooms is highlighted when the block releases within 14 days, and the block's creator and last editor get a reminder at 14, 7 and 1 days. The header counts the artists with lodging offered whose people hold no room yet. Link a block to a budget line to see its lodging held and assigned on the budget; nothing is written to the budget.

## Rooming list

The project's Rooming section lists the people of the travel party who need a room: the stays their passes or travel dates suggest, the rooms they hold, the nights still uncovered and what they cost. Filter to Missing, Partial or No dates. Assign room opens the allocation to issue when the person has one approved, and otherwise the room form filled in with the suggested stay; nothing is saved until you confirm. Each read of the list is recorded in each person's access log, so it loads only when you ask, and it cannot be exported.
