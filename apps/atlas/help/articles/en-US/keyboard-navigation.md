Atlas can be used end to end with the keyboard. Focus is always visible, and every icon button names its shortcut in its tooltip.

## Everywhere

- **Mod+K** or **/** opens the command menu.
- **?** opens help for the page you are on.
- **Mod+.** turns focus mode on and off.
- **Esc** closes the open menu, dialog or side peek.

## In lists and tables

- **J** and **K**, or the arrow keys, move between rows.
- **Enter** opens the focused record in the side peek.
- **X** selects the focused row. The bulk action bar appears with the actions every selected row allows.
- **Home** and **End** jump to the first and last row.

## On boards

Focus a card and press **Space** to pick it up, the arrow keys to carry it to another column, **Space** to drop it, and **Esc** to put it back.

You can change shortcuts that are not reserved by the browser in **Me, Keyboard shortcuts**.
