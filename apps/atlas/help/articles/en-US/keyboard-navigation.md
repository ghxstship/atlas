Atlas can be used end to end with the keyboard. Focus is always visible, and every icon button names its shortcut in its tooltip. Help lists every shortcut with the keys it has for you. The lists below show the default keys; Mod is Cmd on a Mac and Ctrl elsewhere.

## Everywhere

<!-- shortcuts atlas general -->

- **Mod+K**: Open the command menu
- **/**: Search pages and settings
- **C**: Create something new
- **?**: Open help for this page
- **Mod+.**: Turn focus mode on or off
- **Mod+J**: Ask Aurora
- **Esc**: Close a menu, dialog or peek
- **Mod+Z**: Undo the last change
- **Mod+Shift+Z**: Redo the change you undid

<!-- /shortcuts -->

The command menu shows the keys for each section next to its name, and the Create menu puts what you create on the page you are on first, so Enter starts it. Shortcuts never act while you type in a field, except the ones with **Mod**.

## Go to a section

Each is two keys, one after the other. After the first, a hint lists the keys that can follow; **Esc** cancels.

<!-- shortcuts atlas go -->

- **G H**: Go to Home
- **G I**: Go to Inbox
- **G M**: Go to My Work
- **G P**: Go to Projects
- **G S**: Go to Schedule
- **G W**: Go to Work
- **G O**: Go to Show
- **G A**: Go to Advancing
- **G L**: Go to Logistics
- **G E**: Go to People
- **G C**: Go to Crew
- **G F**: Go to Finance
- **G B**: Go to Procurement
- **G V**: Go to Vendors
- **G K**: Go to Knowledge
- **G R**: Go to Reports
- **G Y**: Go to Activity
- **G T**: Go to Settings

<!-- /shortcuts -->

## In lists and tables

<!-- shortcuts atlas lists -->

- **J**: Next row
- **K**: Previous row
- **Enter**: Open the focused record
- **X**: Select the focused row
- **Shift+X**: Select the rows from the last selected one to the focused one
- **Mod+A**: Select every loaded row
- **Shift+F10**: Open the focused row's actions

<!-- /shortcuts -->

The arrow keys also move between rows, and **Home** and **End** jump to the first and last row. Selecting rows shows the bulk action bar with the actions every selected row allows.

## On a record

These work on a record's full page and in the side peek, while focus is in the record.

<!-- shortcuts atlas record -->

- **S**: Change the state
- **A**: Assign an owner
- **M**: Add a comment
- **E**: Edit the title
- **O**: Open the full page
- **L**: Copy the link
- **Mod+Backspace**: Move to Trash

<!-- /shortcuts -->

A key does nothing on a record that does not offer its action to you.

## In a view

These work on a collection while focus is in it: its view switcher, its filters, your saved views and export.

<!-- shortcuts atlas view -->

- **V**: Switch the view
- **F**: Open the filters
- **Shift+S**: Save the view
- **Shift+E**: Export the view

<!-- /shortcuts -->

## In the Inbox

<!-- shortcuts atlas inbox -->

- **A**: Approve the focused item
- **R**: Reject the focused item with a reason
- **E**: Mark the focused item done
- **H**: Snooze the focused item until tomorrow

<!-- /shortcuts -->

## On boards

Focus a card and press **Space** to pick it up, the arrow keys to carry it to another column, **Space** to drop it, and **Esc** to put it back.

## In the showcaller console

While you call a show live, **J** and **K** move the standby, **S** puts the selected cue on standby, **T** jumps to a cue, **[** and **]** nudge the remaining plan by a minute and **D** changes the department. **Space** on the cue list calls the cue. A Stream Deck sends **F13** to **F20** (Go, Standby, Previous, Next, Nudge later, Nudge earlier, Jump and Department); set each key to a **Hotkey** action.

## Make the keys your own

In **Me, Keyboard shortcuts**, choose **Change** next to a shortcut and press the new keys; for a sequence, press a letter and then a second key. Atlas refuses keys another shortcut already uses and keys that belong to your browser, your computer or a screen reader. **Reset** brings back the default. Turn off **Keyboard shortcuts** there to stop every character-key shortcut without **Mod**, **Ctrl** or **Alt**; function keys **F13** to **F20** keep working, since they type no character.

In **Me, Preferences**, the **Sidebar** section hides the items you never use and puts the groups and items in your own order. Move one by dragging it, or focus its handle and press **Space**, the arrow keys, then **Space** again. **Reset to role default** brings back the starting layout.
