## Inbox

Items sort into **Needs you** and **For your information**. Approve, reject or reply right from an item, without opening the record. With the keyboard, **E** marks an item done, **H** snoozes it and **A** approves.

## My Work

Records you own or are accountable for, grouped by **Overdue**, **Today**, **Upcoming** and **No due date**, in the view you choose.

## Approvals

- **Waiting on me**: the steps you decide.
- **My requests**: what you submitted.
- **All**: everything you may see.

You cannot approve your own request.
