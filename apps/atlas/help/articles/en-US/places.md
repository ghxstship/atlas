## Venues

A venue records where a production happens: address, jurisdiction, time zone and capacity. The jurisdiction drives the permits a project needs.

A venue's levels name its floors in order, with basements below zero; spaces and site plans name the level they are on. Its technical specs record house power, dock and access, dimensions, rigging and network facts. A blank spec is not recorded and never passes a check. Never enter a network password: share credentials in a Restricted capability document.

## Spaces and zones

Spaces are the rooms, stages and areas of a venue. Zones group spaces for access control and occupancy. Credentials grant access by zone.

## Capability documents

Capability documents record what a venue can support, such as power, rigging points and load-in limits. Advancing reconciles riders against them.

## Site plans

A site plan is a project's drawing of a venue with pins for spaces, assets and points of interest. Upload a PNG, JPEG or PDF as its drawing; from a PDF, the page you choose becomes the drawing. On a Draft plan, draw routes, barriers, areas and labels, set the scale from one known distance, and measure lengths and areas. Every tool also takes its points as Across and Down percentages, and the arrow keys nudge the selected point.

A plan moves from Draft to Locked when its layout is fixed, and to Live when crews work from it; one plan per venue and level is live. Comments, notes at a point, mentions and Resolve work in every state. Export PDF prints the drawing with the layers and legend you choose.

## Signage

The signage register lists each project's signs with their text, size, material, place and artwork. Mark a sign ordered, printed and placed, or link it to a purchase order line so the order says when it is ordered and received. The build sheet prints the signs to build and place, grouped by plan and pin.

## Venue specs in advancing and permits

On a project's scope, Add venue specs as capabilities copies the venue's known power, rigging and connectivity specs into capabilities for reconciliation, documented when a current capability document backs them. Permits show the venue facts that matter to each rule; they never change which permits apply.
