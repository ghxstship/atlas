A gate is the check between two phases of a project. Each gate has criteria. Blocking criteria must be met before the gate passes; the others are recorded but do not stop it.

## Pass a gate

1. Open the project and choose **Gates**.
2. Review each criterion of the next gate. Attach evidence where a criterion asks for it.
3. When every blocking criterion is met, choose **Pass gate** and give a reason.

## When a gate is refused

If a blocking criterion is still open, Atlas refuses the gate and names the criterion. Attach the missing evidence or resolve the criterion, then try again. Every pass is logged with who passed it, when and why.

## Definitions

Hover over or focus any gate or phase chip to see its definition from the XOS canon. The glossary in Help lists every term.
