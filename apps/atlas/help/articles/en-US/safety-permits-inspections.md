## Permits

Atlas generates permit requirements from the project's jurisdiction and scope, each with its typical lead time. Track every permit from application to approval, and see in gate readiness which permits block the next gate.

## Inspections

An inspection runs a template of checks at a location. Run it in Atlas or in Compass, even offline. A failed item creates a punch item with the inspection, location and photo attached.

## Inspection templates

Templates define the checks of an inspection. Change a template and new inspections use the new version; past inspections keep the version they ran.
