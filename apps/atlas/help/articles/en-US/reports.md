## Standard reports

Gate Readiness, Coordinate Matrix, Budget versus Actual, Labor, Purchase Order Exposure, Incidents and the Final Cost Report each read live data you can see. The final cost report is produced when a project closes and compares it with its gate 3 baseline.

## Build a report

1. Open **Report Builder** and choose a collection.
2. Pick the fields, filters and grouping, then preview.
3. Save it. It appears in **Saved Reports**.

Totals are aware of missing values: an unknown amount stays unknown instead of counting as zero.

## Dashboards and delivery

Create a dashboard, then add saved reports to it as tiles. A saved report can be emailed on a schedule as PDF, CSV or Excel; each recipient receives only what they may see.
