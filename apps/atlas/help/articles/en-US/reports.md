## Standard reports

Gate Readiness, Coordinate Matrix, Budget versus Actual, Labor, Purchase Order Exposure, Incidents and the Final Cost Report each read live data you can see. The final cost report is produced when a project closes and compares it with its gate 3 baseline.

## Wrap-up

A project's **Reports** tab starts with its wrap-up: the debrief and a management summary, printed with budget against actuals, attendance, sustainability and carbon, and incidents as the wrap-up report PDF.

- Add debrief points by area: what went well, what to improve, and actions, each action with an owner.
- Write the management summary, or choose **Summarize** to have Aurora draft one from the project's figures and debrief points. **Use as management summary** puts the draft in the box; nothing is saved until you choose **Save summary**. A saved draft prints as "Drafted with Aurora and reviewed by" you, with the date.
- **Complete debrief** locks the points and the summary. **Reopen debrief** unlocks them. While the debrief is completed, a deleted point cannot be restored from Trash either; reopen the debrief first.

Each section of the report shows only what you may read; the others say "Not included". A figure that was not measured reads Unmeasured, never 0.

## Objectives and measurement

**Objectives and Measurement** compares each project's gate 1 objectives with what gate 8 measured, in your units. Without a project filter it shows how every project's objectives stand and each figure across the projects that measured it. An objective reads Met, Not Met, Incomplete (some figures are unmeasured and could still change it) or Unmeasured.

## Build a report

1. Open **Report Builder** and choose a collection.
2. Pick the fields, filters and grouping, then preview.
3. Save it. It appears in **Saved Reports**.

Totals are aware of missing values: an unknown amount stays unknown instead of counting as zero.

## Dashboards and delivery

Create a dashboard, then add saved reports to it as tiles. A saved report can be emailed on a schedule as PDF, CSV or Excel; each recipient receives only what they may see.
