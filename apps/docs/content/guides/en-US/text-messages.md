XOS can send work notifications by text message (SMS). The program is called **XOS notifications**. Texts are updates about your work from the organizations you belong to in XOS, such as shift changes, approvals and safety alerts. They are never marketing.

Texts are off until you turn them on yourself. An organization cannot turn them on for you.

## Who can get texts

Anyone who is a member of an organization in XOS and has a verified phone number on their XOS account. Texts go only to that verified number, and only for an organization you agreed to get texts from.

Agreeing is optional. It is not a condition of working with any organization or of using XOS.

## How to opt in

1. Sign in to Atlas and open **Personal settings**, then **Notifications**.
2. Make sure your account has a verified phone number. Until it does, the page says "Add and verify a phone number on your account to receive texts." and shows no consent box.
3. Under **What reaches you**, tick the box that reads: "Text me notifications from this organization at {phone}. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop or HELP for help. See our Text Message Terms and Privacy Policy." In place of {phone}, the box shows the last four digits of your verified number. "Text Message Terms" and "Privacy Policy" are links to those documents. They open in a new tab and do not tick the box.
4. Choose which kinds of notifications reach you by **Text (SMS)** in the same list.
5. Select **Save notification settings**.

The box is unchecked until you tick it. It shows checked only when you already agreed for that organization and number.

## Consent is per organization

XOS records your agreement for one organization and one phone number, with the time and the source. If you belong to more than one organization, you agree to each one separately on that organization's Notifications page. Agreeing in one organization does not turn texts on in another.

## What to expect

- Message frequency varies.
- Message and data rates may apply.
- Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
- Texts wait during quiet hours, 21:00 to 08:00 in your time zone unless you set your own. Texts that wait arrive together as one message when quiet hours end. Safety messages and a shift that starts within 12 hours, among a few others, arrive at once.
- Each text names the organization, links to the update in XOS, and ends with "Reply STOP to opt out."

## Keywords

Reply with the word alone as the whole message.

| Reply                                                              | What happens                                                                                                                                              | The reply you get                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| STOP (or QUIT, END, REVOKE, OPT OUT, CANCEL, UNSUBSCRIBE, STOPALL) | Texts stop for every organization that had your consent for that number.                                                                                  | "You will get no more texts from XOS notifications. Reply START to subscribe again."                                                                                                               |
| START (or YES, UNSTOP, SUBSCRIBE)                                  | Texts start again for each organization whose texts you stopped by text. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out."                                                  |
| HELP (or INFO)                                                     | Nothing changes.                                                                                                                                          | "XOS notifications: updates about your work from the organizations you belong to. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Help: support@ghxstship.pro. Reply STOP to opt out." |

## Turn texts off in settings

Open **Personal settings**, then **Notifications**, clear the box, and select **Save notification settings**. XOS records the withdrawal for that organization. You can also reply STOP to any text at any time.
