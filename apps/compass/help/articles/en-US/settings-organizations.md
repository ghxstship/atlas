order: 1
---

- **Organizations You Belong To** lists your roles in each. **Open** shows that organization in Compass now. **Make Default** makes it the one Atlas and the API act in, and the one Compass opens on a phone that hasn't opened another; it saves offline too. **Leave** ends your access at once and needs a connection. The only Owner of an organization makes someone else Owner first.
- **Organizations You Work With** are those that engaged you. **Open** shows their work; an engagement ends with its contract.
- **Company accounts** are the vendor, agency or contractor companies you belong to in Gateway. **Leave** ends your access to one. Create a company account in Gateway.
