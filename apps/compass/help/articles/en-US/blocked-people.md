Blocked People lists everyone you blocked. You block someone from the three dots on their post, message or shout-out.

## What a block hides

Their posts, comments on posts, shout-outs and messages, in every conversation, and the notifications about them. A post that asks you to acknowledge it still shows. They are not told, and their work with you carries on as before.

## Unblock

Choose **Unblock** next to the name. Their posts and messages show again. Unblocking needs a connection.
