More lists the rest of Compass for your role, in a fixed order. Members see screens such as Incidents, Inspections, Logistics, Show and Reference; people engaged by an organization see the entries for their role type.

Some entries open in Gateway or Atlas because Compass does not show them yet; see [Pages in Gateway and Atlas](help:elsewhere).

Members also find:

- **Workforce**: feed, chat, directory, forms, training and more
- **Aurora**, the assistant, when your organization has it on
- **Kiosk Mode**, if you run a shared clock-in device

The Offline queue, Profile, Settings and Help are at the end.
