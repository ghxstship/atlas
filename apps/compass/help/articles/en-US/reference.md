Reference keeps what you need on site, saved on your phone so it works without signal:

- **Emergency codes**: your organization's codes, with the names, meanings and order your safety team set in Atlas, each with its code word and color. If your organization keeps no codes of its own, or you work for it without being a member, you see the standard codes, and the screen says so. Connect once to download them.
- **Radio channels**: the radio plan, channel by channel.
- **Venue maps**: maps of the venue, when the project has them.
- **SOPs**: the published standard operating procedures.

Pull down to refresh when you have a connection.
