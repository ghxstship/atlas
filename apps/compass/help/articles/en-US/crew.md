Crew is the tab for field supervisors.

## On site now

Everyone clocked in on the project, with when they clocked in and how long they have been on.

## Late

People whose accepted shift has started and who have not clocked in.

## Dispatch Board

Opens [Incidents](help:incidents), where you dispatch and contain reported incidents.

Approve timesheets from the alert when notifications are on; see [Inbox](help:inbox).
