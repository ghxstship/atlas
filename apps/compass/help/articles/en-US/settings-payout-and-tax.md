- **Payout destinations** shows where each organization pays you: the payment provider and the last four digits only. Payout details are entered through each organization's payment provider, usually during onboarding, never in Compass.
- **Tax forms** lists the W-9, W-8BEN or W-8BEN-E you gave each organization, the last four characters of the identifier, when you signed it, and whether foreign performer withholding applies.
- A W-9 needs your name and mailing address for the 1099-NEC each organization files at year end. **Add Address** or **Edit Address** saves it; offline it saves on this phone and uploads when you're back online.
- **Give a Tax Form** opens Gateway: a tax form goes to the organization that asks for it, from that engagement's onboarding.

Both lists open offline once you've opened them online on this phone.
