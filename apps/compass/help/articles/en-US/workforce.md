Workforce gathers the tools for members of an organization.

## Team

Feed, Surveys, Chat, Directory, Events, Forms, Training, Handbook, Recognition and Help Desk.

## Site Operations

Occupancy, Weather, Guest Check-In and Lost and Found. They appear when your role on the current project allows them.

Choose a project first for site operations.
