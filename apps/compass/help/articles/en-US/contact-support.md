Open Help and choose **Contact Support**.

## Members of an organization

Ask your organization's teams, such as operations, IT, HR or facilities, in the [Help Desk](help:workforce-help-desk). They answer there.

## Everyone engaged by an organization

Questions about an engagement, a payment, a schedule or a document go to the organization that engaged you. **Open Messages in Gateway** takes you there.

## Your own account

Platform support helps only with your own account: signing in, your email and deleting your account. Choose a category, write a subject and what happened, then **Send Request**. You get a request number, and platform support answers by email. Sending needs a connection.

Turn on **Attach Diagnostics** to send the screen you were on, the app version and your device. Nothing else is sent.
