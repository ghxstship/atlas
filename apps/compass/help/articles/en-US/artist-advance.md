If an organization engaged you as an artist, or you represent one, **More** lists three screens for the current project. They read from your phone, so they work offline; pull down to refresh when you are online.

- **Advance summary:** each advance packet, how many sections are answered, when it is due, and how each answer was reviewed.
- **Rider:** each rider and its asks, with what the organization has fulfilled.
- **Settlement:** the settlement the organization submitted, its net amount and each line, deductions shown as minus.

To answer the advance or accept or dispute a settlement, tap **Open Gateway**; it opens that tab of the engagement.
