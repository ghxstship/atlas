Inbox gathers what needs you on the project:

- shifts offered to you, which open Schedule
- reported incidents, if you dispatch them
- changes that did not upload and need your decision, which open the [Offline queue](help:offline-queue)

When nothing needs you, Inbox says you are all caught up.

## Approve from notifications

Inbox offers to turn on notifications. With them on, you can approve or reject timesheets and other requests right from the alert, with a reason when you reject.
