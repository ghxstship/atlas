Inbox gathers what needs you on the project:

- shifts offered to you, which open Schedule
- reported incidents, if you dispatch them
- changes that did not upload and need your decision, which open the [Offline queue](help:offline-queue)

Your notifications join them: approvals, mentions and assignments under **Needs You**, everything else under **For Your Information**. A notification about a record opens the record. One about something Compass has no screen for, such as a new match for a saved search, opens the page in Gateway.

When nothing needs you, Inbox says you are all caught up.

## Approve from notifications

Inbox offers to turn on notifications. With them on, you can approve or reject timesheets and other requests right from the alert, with a reason when you reject. Tapping any other alert opens what it is about, even when Compass was closed.
