Show is the live running order of today's show, with how many cues are called.

## Follow and call

The next cue counts down, with a tap you can feel as it gets close. If you call cues, **Call Cue** calls the next one. When the stage manager puts a cue on standby, it shows as **Standby**. A cue whose time is still to be confirmed shows **TBC**: it is not counted down, buzzed or spoken.

## Running orders

When the project has more than one running order, such as a main stage and a side stage, the **Running order** button names the one on screen; tap it to pick another. The screen stays on the one you picked while you call cues; it never moves to another stage by itself. In show mode the running order's name stays on screen. A cue more than 12 hours away shows the day and time it is planned for instead of a countdown.

## Your department

Pick **Your department** once (Audio, Lighting, Video, Special Effects or Stage, or All). Compass remembers it on this device. The cards under the running order show your department's call for the current and next cue, or say there is none.

## Show mode

**Show Mode** turns the screen dark and keeps it awake for the show. **Exit Show Mode** when you are done.

Clients follow the same order, read only, in [Run of show](help:run-of-show).
