Each counter shows the count against the permitted load. Add or remove people at your door as they come and go.

- At 80 percent, slow entry.
- At 90 percent, hold entry and call your supervisor.
- At the permitted load, stop entry now.

Counters are set up in Atlas. Counts work offline.
