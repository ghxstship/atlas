---
title: Occupancy
summary: Counting people in and out at each door against the counter's load.
kind: workflow
nodes: [workforce.occupancy]
order: 1
---

Each counter shows the count against its load, and says where that load comes from. It is a permitted load only when a permit set it; a load from the space's area and a load factor is a calculated load. Add or remove people at your door as they come and go.

- At 80 percent, slow entry.
- At 90 percent, hold entry and call your supervisor.
- At the load, stop entry now.

Counters are set up in Atlas. Counts work offline.
