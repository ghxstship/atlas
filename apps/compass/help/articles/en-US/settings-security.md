Security needs a connection.

- **Password**: type a new one twice, at least 12 characters with letters and digits. If you signed in more than a day ago, Compass emails you a code to enter first.
- **Two-step verification**: tap **Set up authenticator app**, open the app from the button or add the setup key, then enter its 6-digit code. Rename or remove a factor here; add a security key in Gateway.
- **Recovery codes**: single-use codes for when you can't use your app. **Share Codes** saves them to your password manager or notes. You see them only once.
- **Passkeys**: rename or remove them here; add one in Gateway.
- **Sessions and devices**: sign out of any session you don't recognize, or all others.

Removing a factor, making recovery codes and, once you have a second factor, changing your password or removing a passkey ask for a code from your authenticator app or a recovery code first.
