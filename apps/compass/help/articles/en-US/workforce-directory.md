Search the people you may see. Show everyone, or only the people on site now.

Each person can be called or messaged in one tap, and shows their recognitions.

To stop seeing someone, block them from their card. A block hides their posts, comments, shout-outs and messages, and they are not told. See [Blocked people](help:blocked-people).
