Compass is the field app for members of an organization and for everyone it engages. What you see depends on your role.

## Tabs

- **Today**: your clock, your next shifts and the day's information.
- **Schedule**: your shifts and open shifts to claim.
- **Scan**: the raised button in the middle, for badges, gear and deliveries.
- **Inbox**: what needs you.
- **More**: everything else for your role, Settings and Help.

Field supervisors also have a **Crew** tab.

## Project and sync

Choose the project at the top of a screen. A banner says when you are offline or when changes wait to upload. Compass keeps working offline and uploads when you reconnect; see [Offline queue](help:offline-queue).

## Help

Choose **Help** in More or Settings for the articles of the screen you were on. Help is saved on your phone, so it works offline too.
