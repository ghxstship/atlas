Settings, Profile edits what organizations see when you apply or share your profile in Gateway.

- **About You**: your handle (your Gateway address, which changes once every 30 days), preferred name, pronouns, headline, bio, city, country and published rate. Tap **Save profile**.
- **Skills**, **Certifications**, **Languages** and **Links**: add one with its form, or tap **Remove**. A certification an organization verified shows **Verified by an organization**.

Once you have opened Profile online, it opens without a connection too. Changes save on this phone and upload when you're back online; the Offline Queue lists them. Choosing a skill needs a connection.

Who sees each part is under [Profile privacy](help:settings-privacy).
