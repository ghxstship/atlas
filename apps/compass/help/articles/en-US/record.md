When you open a record link from Atlas, such as Continue on phone, Compass shows the record's key, kind, state and title, with its dates and owner when it has them.

If Compass has a screen for that kind, such as Schedule, Inspections or Training, open it in one tap. Every record can also open in Atlas.

If you cannot see the record, Compass shows only its key and the way to Atlas, where you can check your access.
