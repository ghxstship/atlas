- **Who sees your profile**: Private (only you, and organizations you share it with), Network (people and organizations you have worked with) or Public. Then whether the marketplace lists you, and whether search engines may index a Public profile. Tap **Save privacy**.
- **Each section**: a section can be narrower than the profile, never wider. A choice saves at once.
- **Profile shares**: each share sent the fields you chose to one organization. **Revoke** stops future updates.
- **Blocked People and Organizations**: you don't see posts, comments, shout-outs or messages from anyone you block. **Unblock** shows them again.

Visibility and sections save on this phone offline. Shares and blocks need a connection.
