- **Theme**: Device, Dark, Light or **Sunlight**, the highest contrast for bright sun.
- **Haptics**: a tap you can feel when an action lands.
- **Battery Saver**: checks location less often while on shift.
- **Low-Data Mode**: photos upload on Wi-Fi only.
- **Biometric Unlock**: Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode when Compass opens.
- **Kiosk Mode**, if you run a shared clock-in device.
- **Personal settings**: opens your settings in Gateway.
- **Help**: articles and Contact Support.
- **Delete Account**: see [Delete your account](help:delete-account).

**Sign Out** asks you to confirm when changes have not uploaded, because signing out deletes them from this phone.
