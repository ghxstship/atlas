Settings, Work Details holds what organizations need to equip, feed, book and look after you.

- **Work phone**: saves on this phone offline, like your other settings.
- **Uniform sizes**, **Dietary needs**, **Accessibility needs** and **Travel profile**: change what applies and tap **Save** on that card.
- **Emergency contacts**: up to five, in the order to call them. Add one with its form; remove one with its button.

Work details other than the work phone need a connection and never stay on this phone. An organization sees them only when you share them with an engagement.

Your passport, loyalty programs, what you share with each engagement and your access log open in Gateway. Your [profile](help:profile) shows your contacts and sizes.
