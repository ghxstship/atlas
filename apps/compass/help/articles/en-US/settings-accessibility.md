Settings, Accessibility holds the accessibility settings Atlas, Gateway and Compass share.

- **Text size**: Default, Large or Larger. Compass changes its text at once.
- **High contrast** and **Motion**: Compass applies both at once.
- **Haptics**: a tap you can feel when an action lands.
- **Sounds**: the camera's shutter sound and other sounds.
- **Captions** is saved, but not in effect yet.

Once you have opened Accessibility online, it opens without a connection too. Changes save on this phone and upload when you're back online; the Offline Queue lists them. **Use the inherited value** drops your own choice.
