Profile shows your name and your role in Compass, such as Field, Field Supervisor, Crew or Vendor.

If organizations engage you, your engagements are listed with the project, the organization and the state.

**Certifications** lists the certifications an organization recorded for you and the ones you added to your profile, each with its issuer, whether an organization verified it, and when it expires. **Documents** lists the agreements you signed, the insurance certificates organizations keep on file for your company, and when your passport expires. Your passport number never shows.

Both read from your phone, so they work offline; they refresh whenever you are online. When something expires within 30 days, a note at the top of the card says so. The note turns amber within 7 days and red when the item expires today or tomorrow: the same points, 30, 7 and 1 days before, at which expiry reminders reach you. Each date counts on the calendar of the organization that keeps the item, or on your own for your profile entries and passport.

Tap a document with a file to open it. That needs a connection.

To add or change a certification, tap **Edit in Gateway** (or **Edit in Atlas** if you are a member of an organization). To sign or renew documents, tap **Manage in Gateway** or **Manage in Atlas**.
