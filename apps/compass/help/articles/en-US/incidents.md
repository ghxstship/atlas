Incidents lists the project's incidents with their number and state: Reported, Dispatched, Contained or Closed.

## Report an incident

1. Choose **Report Incident**, then the type. Critical types are marked.
2. Check the location Compass found, describe what happened in one line, and add photos or voice notes. Hold to record a voice note.
3. Hold to send.

In an emergency, call your local emergency number first. XOS does not alert emergency services.

Offline, the report is saved but not sent until you reconnect. If it is an emergency, call your local emergency number now.

For a near miss, something that could have hurt someone or caused damage but did not, choose Level 5 - Advisory and start What happened with the words Near miss, so it can be found later.

## Dispatch

If you dispatch incidents, a Reported incident has **Dispatch**, and a Dispatched one has **Contained**. New reports also appear in Inbox.
