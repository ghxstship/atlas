Incidents lists the project's incidents with their number and state: Reported, Dispatched, Contained or Closed.

## Report an incident

1. Choose **Report Incident**, then the type. Critical types are marked.
2. Check the location Compass found, describe what happened in one line, and add photos or voice notes. Hold to record a voice note.
3. Hold to send.

Offline, the report is saved and sends when you reconnect.

## Dispatch

If you dispatch incidents, a Reported incident has **Dispatch**, and a Dispatched one has **Contained**. New reports also appear in Inbox.
