---
title: Guest Check-In
summary: Checking guests in at the door with their plus-ones.
kind: workflow
nodes: [workforce.door]
order: 1
---

Search the guest list, choose an approved guest and **Check In**. Set how many plus-ones come in with them, then **Confirm Check-In**.

Checked-in guests show when and at which door they came in. Check-ins work offline.

Scan a guest's pass on the Scan tab, in Credential mode, or type the code printed on it here, to open that guest with their pass type, table and host. A pass that is not on tonight's list says so, and a pass already used says who came in with it and when. When a guest checks in, their host hears that they arrived.
