**Clock in team** punches a whole crew in or out from your phone. Field supervisors open it from the **Crew** tab. If you lead a staffing agency's crew on a project, open it from **Today**.

## Punch the team

1. Check the people to punch, or turn on **Select all**. The list shows today's shifts of the people you supervise.
2. Tap **Clock in**. When anyone you checked is already in, the main action is **Clock out** instead.

Each person is punched at your time and your location, so stand on site when the project needs a location to clock in.

## Offline

The punch waits on your phone as one change and uploads when you are back online. A punch sent twice counts once.

## When someone is not punched

The reason shows beside their name. For example, a shift may not have started yet: clock-in opens two hours before a shift starts and closes when it ends. You cannot punch yourself in here; use your own clock.

You cannot approve a timesheet that holds time you punched for someone. Another approver approves it.
