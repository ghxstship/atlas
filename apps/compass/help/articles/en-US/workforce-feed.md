The feed shows announcements, updates, polls and recognition for you. Pinned posts come first.

- A post marked Please read and acknowledge needs **Acknowledge**.
- Vote in a poll.
- React to a post and read its comments, where your organization allows them.

Acknowledgments and votes work offline and upload when you reconnect.

## Report or block

Tap the three dots on someone else's post to report it to your organization's moderators, who review it within 24 hours, or to block its author. A report they leave undecided after 24 hours goes to the GHXSTSHIP Trust and Safety team. Nobody is told who reported. A post with a slur is refused before anyone sees it. See [Blocked people](help:blocked-people).
