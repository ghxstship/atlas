nodes: [logistics]

---

- **Dock Slots**: today's slots with their window. **Check In** when the delivery arrives.
- **Gate Queue**: vehicles waiting at the vehicle gate. **Record Arrival** with the plate or trailer number, and **Release** a vehicle.
- **Yard Log**: write what happened in the yard and **Add Entry**.
- **Fulfillments**: lodging, catering, travel and amenities, each with its state. Move one to Active, Blocked or Complete, or cancel it.

Every change works offline and sends when you reconnect. Choose a project first.
