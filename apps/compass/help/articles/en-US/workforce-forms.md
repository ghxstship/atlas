Forms lists the forms your organization published, ready offline. Open one and fill it in:

- Required answers are marked, and Compass counts the ones left.
- Some forms take photos, a signature or a location stamp.
- Scored forms show your score and the pass mark.

**Submit Form** saves it; it submits now, or when you are back online.
