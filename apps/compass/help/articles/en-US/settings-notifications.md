Settings, Notifications holds the same choices as Atlas and Gateway.

**In your organizations** appears when you are a member. Pick the organization when you belong to more than one.

- Each kind of notification lists only the channels it can reach you on: In app, Email, Text (SMS) and Push. Until you choose, your organization's defaults apply. A channel marked **Required by your organization** stays on.
- **Quiet hours and digest**: during quiet hours, push notifications wait until the end and texts arrive together. Enter 24-hour times, such as 22:00, and choose the time zone they are read in. The **Email digest** gathers email that is not urgent into one message. Tap **Save**.
- **Texts**: agree to texts from the organization at your verified phone. This needs a connection.

**From other organizations** covers organizations you work with without being a member, and marketplace alerts: Email and Push for each kind, and **Your own quiet hours** for their pushes.

Safety alerts, changes to your sign-in security and a new or changed shift starting within 12 hours always arrive at once.

Once you have opened Notifications online, it opens without a connection too. Changes save on this phone and upload when you're back online; the Offline Queue lists them.
