Connected apps are tools an organization registered that you allowed to act for you. Each can do only what you can, in the areas you approved.

- Each app shows the organization that registered it, the areas it may use, when you allowed it and when it last used its access.
- **Disconnect** ends its access at once: its sessions end and it can no longer act for you. Connect it again from the app to give it access.

The list shows offline; disconnecting needs a connection.
