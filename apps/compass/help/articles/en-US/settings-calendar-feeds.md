A calendar feed puts your published shifts and the meetings, milestones and deadlines shared with you, from every organization you work with, into your own calendar app. Calendars refresh it about once an hour.

- Give a feed a name, which your calendar shows, and choose what it shows: shifts, meetings, milestones, deadlines and cues, such as your set times. **Edit** changes them without changing the link.
- **Add a feed** makes a new link. It's shown only once: tap **Copy or Share Link** to copy it or send it to yourself, or **Open in my calendar app** to subscribe on this phone.
- Anyone with the link can read the feed. If it gets out, **Get a new link**: the old one stops at once and your calendars stop updating until you subscribe with the new one.
- **Revoke** ends a feed for good.

The list shows offline; adding, a new link and revoking need a connection.
