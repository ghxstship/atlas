---

- **Your Name**: your full name as you write it, and optional given and family names for formal address and sorting. Tap **Save Name**. It saves on this phone offline and uploads when you're back online.
- **How You Sign In**: the email you sign in with and the ways linked to it, such as Google or Apple. This one account works in Gateway, Atlas and Compass for every organization.
- **Delete Account** opens [Delete your account](help:delete-account), which needs a connection.

To change your password or your second factor, use [Security](help:settings-security).
