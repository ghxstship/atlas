**More** > **Project details** lists the projects you are on a roster for. Open one to see what it asks of you: your travel request (the airport codes and dates you fly in and home, and whether you need a pickup or a room), your uniform sizes, or the vehicle you park on site. A project asks only for what it offers.

Project details save only when you are online, and they are never kept on your phone. Offline, the save buttons are off until you connect again.

These details stay on that roster entry and are never written to your profile. Each time someone else reads them, your access log in Gateway shows it, and they are deleted after the engagement closes. Gateway's **Project details** tab on the engagement holds the same details, with a search for airports by city.
