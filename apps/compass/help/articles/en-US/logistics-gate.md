---
title: Gate
summary: Find a delivery booking at the gate and move it through arrival, release and check-in.
kind: workflow
nodes: [logistics.gate]
order: 2
---

- **Pick a gate** to see today's delivery windows, in the project's time zone, with how many vehicles are booked.
- **Find a booking** by its reference code or plate. The search runs on this phone, so it works with no signal.
- **Arrived**: the vehicle is at the gate. Enter the plate when the booking has none.
- **Release** lets a waiting vehicle into the site, and **Check In** records it at the dock.

Every change works offline and sends once when you reconnect. Choose a project first.
