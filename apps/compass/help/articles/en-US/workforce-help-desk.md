The Help Desk is how members ask their organization's teams, such as operations, IT, HR or facilities, for help.

## Ask for help

Choose **Ask for Help**, then the team, a category, a subject and what you need, and **Send Request**. A request needs a connection to get its number.

## Your requests

Each request shows its number, state and who it is assigned to. A request marked Response overdue has passed its response time. The conversation continues in Atlas.
