Deleting your account signs you out and erases your sign-in, name, profile, devices and preferences. Every organization you work with is told. It needs a connection.

## What can block it

A legal hold an organization keeps on your records, being clocked in, or being the only owner of an organization or company account. Compass names each one and what to do.

## What is kept

Time and pay records, tax forms and engagement records for 7 years, as the law requires; records you created for an organization, without your name; the security audit trail; and a record of your request.

## Confirm

Type your sign-in email, confirm it is you, then hold **Delete My Account** for 2 seconds.
