Settings, Preferences holds the preferences Atlas, Gateway and Compass share. A change here applies in all three.

- **Language**, **Time zone**, **Date format** and **Units**: units also set how distances and measurements read.
- **Show schedule times in**: the event's time zone, or yours.
- **Translate messages** is saved, but not in effect yet.
- **Week starts on**, **Start page**, **Density** and **Persona** shape Atlas on a computer.

Under each setting, Compass says where its value comes from: your own choice, your organization, or the platform default. A value your organization locked cannot be changed. **Use the inherited value** drops your own choice.

Until you choose a time zone, Compass uses this phone's. Until you or your organization choose a language, Compass follows this phone's.

Once you have opened Preferences online, it opens without a connection too. Changes save on this phone and upload when you're back online; the Offline Queue lists them. The theme stays on [Settings](help:settings), beside Sunlight.
