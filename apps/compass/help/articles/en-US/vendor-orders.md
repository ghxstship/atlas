If an organization engaged your company as a vendor, **More** lists three screens for the current project. They read from your phone, so they work offline; pull down to refresh when you are online.

- **Purchase orders:** each order sent to you, with its total, its lines and whether you acknowledged it.
- **Deliveries:** shipments on the project, with ship and arrival times on the project's clock.
- **Submittals:** the records the organization shared with you for review, with their due time.

To acknowledge an order, send a document or answer a submittal, tap **Open Gateway**; it opens that tab of your engagement. Your dock slots are on the Scan tab.
