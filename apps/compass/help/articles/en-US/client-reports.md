If an organization engaged your company as its client, **More** lists **Reports**: each report it shared with you on the current project, when it was shared, how many rows it holds and when the share expires. The list reads from your phone, so it works offline; pull down to refresh when you are online.

To download a report, tap **Open Gateway**; it opens the Reports tab of your engagement. The running order is under **Run of Show**.
