If an organization engaged your brand as a sponsor, **More** lists **Proof of Performance** for the current project:

- **Entitlements:** what the organization owes you, how many and by when. Delivered ones are marked; anything past due on the project's calendar is marked overdue.
- **Proof documents:** the proof of performance the organization shared, such as photo reports.

The screen reads from your phone, so it works offline; pull down to refresh when you are online. To open a document or reply to the organization, tap **Open Gateway**.
