Aurora is an AI assistant. Ask in a sentence, such as When is my call time tomorrow?

It answers only from records you can see and never changes anything without your confirmation. When it suggests a change, confirm or reject it.

Aurora comes with the Core, Pro, Team and Enterprise plans, an Admin turns it on for the organization, and each organization has a monthly allowance of requests. **New conversation** starts over.

The first time you open Aurora, Compass asks whether to allow AI features. They send your questions, with the records that answer them, to a third-party AI provider, and nothing goes until you choose **Allow**. **Don't Allow** turns AI features off for you everywhere in XOS. You can change this in **Settings**, **AI features**. It does not stop other people in your organization using AI features on records they can see.
