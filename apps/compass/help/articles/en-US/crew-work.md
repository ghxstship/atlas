If an organization engaged you as crew or staff, **More** lists four screens for your engagement on the current project. They read from your phone, so they work offline; pull down to refresh when you are online.

- **Timesheets:** each period with its hours and state, and when it was submitted or decided.
- **Per diem:** each day's amount and the total. Today's is marked.
- **Travel and lodging:** your flights and ground transport, each time in the zone of its own airport or station, and your hotel dates.
- **Onboarding Items:** your checklist, what is left first, with a progress bar. It opens before your engagement is active.

Dates follow the project's calendar, so a day never moves because your phone is in another time zone.

To submit a timesheet or upload an onboarding document, tap **Open Gateway**; it opens that tab of your engagement.
