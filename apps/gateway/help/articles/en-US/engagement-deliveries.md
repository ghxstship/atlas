Deliveries is for vendors.

## Dock slots

The times booked for you at the dock: the zone, the start and end, any tracking number, and whether it is inbound or outbound.

## Shipments

The project's shipments with their tracking number, when they ship and when they arrive.

This tab opens when the engagement becomes Active, after onboarding, and closes when it completes or is canceled. Dock times appear once the organization books them; ask in Messages if you need one.
