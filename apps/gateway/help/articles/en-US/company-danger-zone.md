Transferring ownership or closing a company account is done with platform support, so the records the law requires are kept. To ask, open Help, choose **Contact support** and send a request about your account.

The last Account Owner cannot leave the account. Make someone else an owner first: choose **Open team** to go to [Team and account roles](help:company-team-and-account-roles).

Only Account Owners see this page.
