Your clocked shifts collect into timesheets, one per period. Each shows the period, the hours worked, when you submitted it and its state: Proposed, Submitted, Approved or Rejected.

## Submit

A Proposed or Rejected timesheet has **Submit**. Check the hours against your time entries, then submit it for approval.

## Time entries

Each clock-in with its clock-out, breaks and where it came from.

Timesheets open with the engagement's day-to-day work or its money. With self-billing on, the organization raises your invoices from approved timesheets; see [Invoices](help:engagement-invoices).
