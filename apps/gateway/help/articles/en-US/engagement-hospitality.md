Hospitality is for artists. It lists what the production arranged for you: lodging with its dates and guest name, and travel, catering and other items with their time and supplier.

This tab opens when the engagement becomes Active, after onboarding, and closes when it completes or is canceled. Ask for what you need in the [Advance](help:engagement-advance).
