Hospitality is for artists. It starts with your stay, day by day: your flights, your hotel's check-in and check-out, the pickups and runs that carry you or the people you answer for (with the driver, vehicle and times), the rooms booked for you and your set times. Anything the production changed since you last looked carries a tag that names what changed, such as a new set time, and anything it removed is listed under Removed since you last looked. A visit lasts 30 minutes, so refreshing the page keeps the tags; Mark all as seen clears them. Pickups and booked rooms are placed on their day but never tagged.

Below your stay come catering and the other items the production arranged, with their time and supplier.

This tab opens when the engagement becomes Active, after onboarding, and closes when it completes or is canceled. Ask for what you need in the [Advance](help:engagement-advance).
