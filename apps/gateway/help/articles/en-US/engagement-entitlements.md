Entitlements is for sponsors. It lists what your package includes: each item with its quantity, the date it is due and when it was fulfilled.

Each item is **Open**, **Overdue** or **Fulfilled**.

The brand assets for these entitlements are reviewed in [Asset Approvals](help:engagement-asset-approvals), and evidence that they ran arrives in [Proof of Performance](help:engagement-proof-of-performance). Ask the organization in Messages about an item that is overdue.
