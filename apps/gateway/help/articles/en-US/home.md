Home starts with a search box: type a role, a skill or an organization to search Explore.

## Up next

The card shows the next dated item in your current engagements, such as a shift, an event, a delivery or a deadline, with a countdown, the organization, the project and when it is. When nothing is dated yet, it shows the next engagement to start. Open the engagement from the card, or add the item to your calendar.

## Needs you

Everything waiting on you, each with its action in the row:

- documents to sign, with **Sign**
- offers to answer, with **Review offer**
- onboarding steps still open, and how many of them block your start
- a company account asking to represent you, with **Accept**
- interviews coming up
- engagements to rate, for 14 days after they complete

## Updates

When an organization receives, approves, schedules or pays one of your invoices, the latest steps show here. Unread ones are marked New.

## Recommended for you

Open opportunities you can apply for. **See all** opens Explore. When nothing matches, set an alert and Gateway tells you when something does.

The view switcher shows Home as a feed, or as an inbox with only Needs you and Updates.
