Upcoming gathers what is coming up across every organization you work with: your shifts, the items the organization shares on its schedule, dock slots, hotel check-ins, interviews and the first day of an engagement.

## List or calendar

The list shows each item with its date, time, organization and project. The calendar shows a month at a time; move between months with the arrows.

## Add it to your calendar

**Add all to calendar** downloads every listed item as one calendar file, which any calendar app opens. To add a single item, use the Up next card on [Home](help:home).

Items come from your current engagements and your interviews. When an engagement completes or is canceled, its items leave Upcoming. The organization adds them, so ask in Messages if something you expect is missing.
