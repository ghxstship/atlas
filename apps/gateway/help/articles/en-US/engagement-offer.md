Offer gathers the documents that make the engagement: the offer, the agreement, any statement of work and any non-disclosure agreement. Each shows its kind and state.

## Sign

A document waiting for your signature has **Sign**. Read it, then sign. Some documents complete only when the organization countersigns. Download a signed copy from [Documents](help:engagement-documents).

To change the terms of an offer before you accept it, counter it in [Applications](help:work-applications). An artist's representative with the Offers scope sees this tab for the artist.
