When the organization needs your approval, the request appears here with what it is about, when it was requested and any response. A request still open is marked Waiting for you.

## Decide

1. Under the request, choose **Approve** or **Reject**.
2. Approving, you can add a note to the organization. Rejecting, say why, so the organization can fix it.
3. Send. The request shows your decision, Approved or Rejected.

Requests are about a change order or a record. A change order waiting for you can also be decided on the [Change Orders](help:engagement-change-orders) tab.

You see what the organization shares with clients, never its internal notes, margins or other parties' rates.
