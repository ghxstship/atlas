The page shows the organization, the pay, the dates and the place, what the work is, its requirements and its calls. The side card adds the rate type, the number of positions, the deadline, who can see it and any minimum age.

## Client confidential

Some organizations keep the client and the event private until you are booked. A **Client confidential** listing still shows the organization, the city, the dates and the pay, but not the venue, and distance from a place is measured to a point near the venue rather than the venue itself. The venue appears once your application is accepted.

The organization's title and description cannot name the project, its client or the venue. Some details are the organization's own words and are not checked: position labels, application questions, requirement descriptions, interview and audition locations, and messages in an offer. A project, venue or client renamed after the listing was posted is not checked again either. If one of these gives the client away, report the listing.

## Requirements

Each requirement is a certification, insurance or equipment. Where Gateway can check your profile, it marks what you hold and what is missing; add missing certifications to your profile. The organization verifies everything during onboarding.

## Apply

The application shares your name and email with the organization. You answer the organization's questions, can add a note and must confirm you are available for the dates. You can also share chosen sections of your profile with that organization. Where a fee is posted, leave it blank to accept it or propose your own; with Rate on Request, propose one or leave it blank to discuss it.

- **Artists** apply with the press kit from their profile.
- **Vendor work and bid listings** take a sealed bid from a company account: lines with an item, a quantity and a unit price, and how long the bid is valid. A line you cannot price stays unpriced, never zero. Bids stay sealed until the deadline, and no other bidder sees yours.
- **Staff work applied for as a company** takes a slate: the members of your account you put forward.

After you apply, follow every step in [Applications](help:work-applications).

## Save or report

**Save** keeps the opportunity in [Saved](help:explore-saved). **Report listing** sends the platform your reason; it reviews every report and gives the organization its reasons for any action.
