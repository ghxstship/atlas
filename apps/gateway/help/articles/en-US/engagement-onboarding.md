Onboarding lists what the organization needs before you start, with your progress: how many steps are verified. Steps marked Required before you start block the engagement until they pass.

## Each step

A step shows its kind, whether it is required, how it is verified (automatically or by a reviewer) and any expiry. Its standing is To do, In review, Verified or Expired. Complete a To do or Expired step right in its row:

- **Document upload**: a PDF or photo up to 25 MB. On a phone you can take the photo now.
- **Tax form**: the form, your taxpayer identifier and, for foreign performers, withholding. The identifier is stored encrypted.
- **Electronic signature**: sign under Documents; the step completes when the organization countersigns.
- **Acknowledgment**: read the briefing, then acknowledge it.
- **Background check**: the organization's screening provider emails you a secure link. Only the result is stored here.
- **Payout details**: add them in [Settings, Payout and tax](help:settings-payout-and-tax).

The organization verifies each step next. Onboarding steps also appear on Home under Needs you.
