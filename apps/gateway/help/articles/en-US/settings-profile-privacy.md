## Who sees your profile

- **Private**: only you.
- **Network**: people and organizations you have worked with.
- **Public**: everyone.

Two more choices: let organizations find you in the marketplace, and let search engines index your public profile.

## Each section

Photo, pronouns, headline, about, location, skills, certifications, portfolio, rates, availability, languages, references, links and organizations can each have their own visibility, or follow the profile with Same as profile.

## Profile shares

When you apply, you can share chosen sections with that organization. Each share is listed with when it was made. **Revoke** stops future updates; the organization keeps what the law requires it to keep.
