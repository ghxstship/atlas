Proof of Performance is for sponsors. The organization shares the documents that show your entitlements were delivered, such as photos and reports. Each shows its state, and a document waiting for your signature has **Sign**.

If you expected proof that is not here, ask the organization in Messages. What your package includes is in [Entitlements](help:engagement-entitlements).
