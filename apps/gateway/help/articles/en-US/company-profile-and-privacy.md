Company settings appear while you act as a company account. They open on the company profile.

## The company profile

The public profile is at the address shown at the top. Edit:

- **Handle**: the profile address
- **About** the company
- **Website** and **contact email**
- **Who sees it**: Private, Network or Public
- whether search engines may index it

Account Owners and Admins edit it; Account Finance and Members can read it. If the page says the company profile is managed by Atlas administrators, ask an Account Owner to set it up.

The public profile also shows the company's service regions, capabilities, verified domain and open opportunities.
