title: Security
summary: How you sign in to Gateway, and signing out on this device.
kind: page
nodes: [settings.security]
order: 1
---

Gateway signs you in with an email link or your connected provider first. A password is optional.

Each device keeps its own session. **Sign out on this device** ends the session here; other devices stay signed in until you sign out on them.

If you cannot sign in, or your sign-in email has to change, [contact support](help:contact-support) about your account.
