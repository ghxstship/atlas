Time is for contractors paid by the hour. Each entry shows when you clocked in, when you clocked out (or Still clocked in), any break and where it came from.

Clock in and out from Compass when you start and finish. Invoice your time from [Invoices](help:engagement-invoices).

This tab opens when the engagement becomes Active, after onboarding, and closes when it completes or is canceled.
