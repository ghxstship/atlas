Settings, Work Details holds what organizations need to feed, book and look after you: emergency contacts, dietary and accessibility needs, loyalty programs and your passport details.

Uniform sizes and your seat, meal and home airport are no longer kept here. Each production asks for them on the project you are rostered on and keeps them only for that project: open the engagement and choose **Project details**.

An organization sees a category only while you share it with that engagement. Your allergies are restricted: only the catering lead and on-site medical on the project, and the organization's Owner, can read them, and every time they do it shows in your access log.

Your skills, certifications, rates and availability are in [Profile settings](help:settings-profile).
