Reports is for clients.

## Shared reports

Each report the organization shared with you: when it was run, how many rows it has, how many rows are still unpriced, its file format and the date the share ends. When a share is revoked or ends, the report disappears.

## Report records

Report records the organization shared with the project, with their date and state.

If you expect a report that is not here, ask the organization in Messages.
