Activation Schedule is for sponsors. It shows the events, distributions and bookings where your sponsorship is activated, as a calendar and a list, each with its time and state.

This tab opens when the engagement becomes Active, after onboarding, and closes when it completes or is canceled. Dates appear once the organization schedules them.
