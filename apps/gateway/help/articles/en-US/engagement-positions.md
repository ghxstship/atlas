Positions is for staff. It groups your shifts by the position each one fills, with the start, the project, the end and the state of each shift.

Positions are assigned once training is done. To accept a shift that is waiting for you, open [Shifts](help:engagement-shifts).

This tab opens when the engagement becomes Active, after onboarding, and closes when it completes or is canceled.
