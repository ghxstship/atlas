Schedule is for clients. It shows the project dates the organization shares with you: events, meetings, deadlines and other scheduled items.

The calendar opens on the month of the first item, and the list shows each item with its kind, its time or due date and its state.

This tab opens when the engagement becomes Active, after onboarding, and closes when it completes or is canceled. If a date you expect is missing, ask the organization in Messages. The detailed show plan is in [Run of Show](help:engagement-run-of-show).
