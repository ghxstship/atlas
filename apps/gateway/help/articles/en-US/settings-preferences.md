Preferences holds your personal defaults:

- **Language** of menus, emails and documents.
- **Time zone**: times are shown and entered in this zone.
- **Date format**: follow the language, or year-month-day everywhere.
- **Units** for distances, weights and areas.
- **Theme**: system, light or dark.
- **Week starts on** Sunday or Monday, on your calendars. Timesheets and pay periods follow the org's week.
- **Translate messages** written in another language.

Some settings here apply in Atlas only, such as the start page, density and persona.

Each setting shows where its value comes from, such as your own setting or the platform default. Save keeps your own value, and Reset to the platform default removes it. These are the same personal settings Atlas and Compass use.
