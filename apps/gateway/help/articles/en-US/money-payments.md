When an organization pays one of your invoices, the payment is listed here.

## What each payment shows

The date it was paid, the organization, the amount in its currency, the payment reference and its state.

Use the reference to match a payment with your bank statement. To see which invoice a payment settled and what is still owed, open [Invoices](help:money-invoices).

Show payments as a table or a list. On an organization's own Gateway address, only that organization's payments are listed.
