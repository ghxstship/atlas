Organizations that hold a live credential for you print a badge for it. This page shows one card per organization, with the photo its badge prints. An organization can ask you for a photo; the request opens this page.

## What each card shows

- **On your badge**: your photo prints on the badge.
- **Being checked**: your photo is being virus-checked and prints in a few minutes.
- **Not accepted**: the check refused your photo. Upload another.
- **Your profile photo**: you gave no badge photo, so the badge prints your [profile](help:profile) photo, when your privacy settings let the organization see it.
- **Requested** or **None yet**: the badge prints without a photo.

## Giving a photo

Choose **Upload or take photo** to pick a photo or take one with your camera. Drag the photo, or use the arrow keys and the zoom slider, to choose what shows in the 3 by 4 frame, then choose **Save photo**. Nothing reads or detects your face; the crop is yours.

**Use my profile photo** copies your profile photo to the badge instead, when it is a JPEG or PNG of at most 2 MiB.

## Who sees it, and taking it back

The organization's credential staff see the photo, and so does anyone who sees your badge. Saving a photo records your consent for that organization. Choose **Remove** at any time: the photo is deleted, your consent is withdrawn, and the organization prints your badge without it.
