A change order changes the scope or the cost of the work. Each one shows its number and title, why it is needed, its total and its state: Proposed, Priced, Approved, Rejected or Canceled. Its lines show each change with its amount, or No cost.

## Approve or reject

When a change order waits for your approval, the decision form is under it. Choose **Approve** or **Reject**; rejecting, say why. The same request appears in [Approvals](help:engagement-approvals).

Download any change order as a PDF on US Letter or A4.

An amount not known yet reads Unpriced, never zero.
