An alert is a saved search. Each one sends you an email and a push notification when a new opportunity matches it.

## Create an alert

Search or filter in [Explore](help:explore), then choose **Save this search** and name it. It appears here.

## Manage your alerts

Each alert shows its name, the role type it looks for (or Any role type) and its dates. For each one you can:

- open the name to run the search again in Explore
- **Turn off** alerts to keep the search without notifications, and **Turn on** to start them again
- **Remove** the search

Push notifications reach you in Compass on your phone. Email goes to the address you sign in with.
