Invoices open once money is open on the engagement, when it becomes Active. Each invoice is followed like a package: **Submitted**, **Approved**, **Scheduled** and **Paid**, with its total, its balance and its lines. Download each as a PDF on US Letter or A4.

## Submit an invoice

Contractors, crew, staff, vendors, artists and sponsors can submit an invoice:

1. Check the invoice number, the issue date and the due date.
2. Add a line for each item or service, with its quantity and unit price.
3. **Submit invoice**, then follow it on the payment tracker.

## Self-billing

Crew, staff and contractors can choose **Agree to self-billing**. The organization then raises your invoices from approved timesheets, and you can stop it at any time.

Every invoice is also in [Money, Invoices](help:money-invoices).
