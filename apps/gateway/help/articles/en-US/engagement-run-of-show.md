Run of Show is for clients. Each show lists its cues in order: the position, the label, the planned time and the duration. Once a cue is called during the show, its called time appears.

**Download PDF** gives the running order on US Letter, and **PDF (A4)** on A4.

This tab opens when the engagement becomes Active, after onboarding, and closes when it completes or is canceled. Dates around the show are in [Schedule](help:engagement-schedule).
