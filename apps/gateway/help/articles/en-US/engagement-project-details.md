Project details shows when the organization offers travel, uniforms or parking on your engagement. It asks only for what the project offers: your travel request (the airports and dates you fly in and home, and whether you need a pickup or a room), your uniform sizes, and the vehicle you park on site.

Each step saves when you continue, so you can stop and come back. Use my last details fills a step from what you entered on an earlier project; check it, then continue to save it.

These details stay on this engagement and are never written to your profile. The organization reads only the kinds its people need for their work, and your company sees them while you are on its roster; each time someone else reads them, your access log in Settings shows it. They are deleted after the engagement is complete or canceled, 90 days later unless the organization sets another time.

If your company entered something for you, you are told, and you can correct it here. You can clear a kind at any time.
