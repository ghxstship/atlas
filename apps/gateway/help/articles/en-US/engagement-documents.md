Documents lists everything the organization shared on this engagement: offers, agreements, statements of work, riders, insurance certificates, settlements and more. Each shows its kind, any expiry date and its state: Proposed, Sent, Signed, Executed, Expired, Superseded or Canceled.

## Sign

A document that is Sent waits for your signature. Choose **Sign** in its row.

Documents stay open for 18 months after the engagement completes or is canceled. Every document from every organization is also in your [Documents vault](help:documents).
