Per Diem is for crew.

## Allowances

Each day's allowance with its amount, and the total in each currency.

## Amenities

What the organization provides, such as meals, with the dates and the quantity per day.

This tab opens when the engagement becomes Active, after onboarding, and closes when it completes or is canceled. Allowances appear once the organization sets them.
