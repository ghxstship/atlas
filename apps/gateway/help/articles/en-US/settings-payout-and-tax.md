## Payout destinations

Where organizations pay you, by provider, such as Stripe Connect or the organization's accounting system. You enter payout details through each organization's payment provider, and only the last four digits are ever shown here.

## Tax forms

The tax forms you gave organizations, with the form, the organization, the last four characters of your taxpayer identifier and when you signed it. Organizations ask for them during onboarding.

Your W-9 mailing address and year-end details are in [Tax documents](help:money-tax-documents).
