A conversation shows every message with who sent it and when. Your own messages sit on one side and the organization's on the other.

## Reply

Write in the box under the messages and send. The organization sees it right away.

## What else you see

- The organization and the subject at the top. A conversation on an engagement links to that engagement.
- **Read by**: who from the organization has read the conversation, and when.

Opening a conversation marks it read for you. On a phone, **Messages** at the top goes back to the list.
