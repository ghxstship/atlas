Deliverables is for contractors. Each deliverable from your scope shows its due date, when you submitted it, any fee and its state: Open, Submitted, Accepted or Returned. A returned one shows the reason.

## Submit a deliverable

1. Under an Open or Returned deliverable, choose the document.
2. Write a short message.
3. **Submit deliverable**. The organization accepts or returns it.

The document must be on the engagement first. Share the file in Messages and it becomes a document you can submit here.
