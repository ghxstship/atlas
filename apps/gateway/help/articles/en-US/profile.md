Your profile is what organizations see when you apply or share it. Choose **Edit profile** to change it in [Settings, Profile](help:settings-profile).

## Profile strength

A percentage and the next thing to add, such as a headline, skills, a certification, your rate or availability.

## What the page holds

- **Certifications**: yours, marked Verified or Self-reported, and certifications organizations recorded for you, which you can add to your profile.
- **Availability**: dates you are available or not. **Add dates** with a start, an end and a status; remove dates you no longer need.
- **Ratings**: ratings organizations gave you after completed engagements, with any reply. Only you and the organization that gave a rating can read it, unless you turn on **Show all my ratings on my profile** in Profile Privacy. **Dispute this rating** if it is about the wrong person, false, abusive or shows personal data.
- **Representation**: a company account that represents you or asks to. Accept, decline or revoke it.
- **Profile shares**: the organizations you shared your profile with. Revoke a share to stop future updates.

Your public profile shows only what your [privacy settings](help:settings-profile-privacy) allow. **See it as others do** opens it.

When you act as a company account, this page shows the company's profile and team instead.
