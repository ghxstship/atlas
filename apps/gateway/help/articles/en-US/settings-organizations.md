## Your company accounts

Each company account you belong to, with your roles and its web address. **Leave** removes you from it. The last Account Owner cannot leave.

## Create a company account

Vendors, agencies, contractor firms and sponsors bid and invoice as a company.

1. Enter the company name.
2. Choose its web address: 2 to 48 lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens.
3. **Create account**. You become its Account Owner.

Then act as the company from the account menu. Its settings are in [Company settings](help:company-profile-and-privacy).
