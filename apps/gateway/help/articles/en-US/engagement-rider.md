Rider is for artists. Each rider for the project lists its items with their quantity, and whether the production confirmed each one or it is still pending.

Send or change your rider through the [Advance](help:engagement-advance). Hotel, travel and catering arranged for you are in [Hospitality](help:engagement-hospitality).
