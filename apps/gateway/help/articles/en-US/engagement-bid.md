Bid shows the sealed bid your company sent this organization: when it was sent, its total and each line with its quantity and unit price. A line without a price reads Unpriced, never zero.

Only your own bid is shown. Bids stay sealed until the deadline, and no other bidder ever sees yours.

When your bid wins, the organization sends [purchase orders](help:engagement-purchase-orders). To bid on a new opportunity, act as your company account and apply from [Explore](help:opportunity).
