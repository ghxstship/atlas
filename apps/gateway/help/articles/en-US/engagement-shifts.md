Shifts lists every shift of the engagement with its role or position, start, end, place and state: Draft, Published, Accepted or Canceled.

## Accept a shift

A Published shift waits for you. Choose **Accept shift** to take it. Once accepted, it is on your schedule in Compass, and you clock in from Today.

This tab opens when the engagement becomes Active, after onboarding, and closes when it completes or is canceled. Clocked shifts collect into [timesheets](help:engagement-timesheets).
