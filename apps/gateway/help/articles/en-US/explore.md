Explore lists the opportunities open to you, soonest deadline first. Pay is always shown before you apply; a listing without a fixed fee says Rate on Request.

## Search and filter

Type a role, a skill or an organization, then choose **Search**. Open **Filters** to narrow the list by role type, and under More fields by start date, end date and lowest pay in US dollars. Rate on Request listings stay in the results. **Clear filters** shows everything again. Show the results as a gallery of cards or as a list.

## Get an alert

After you search or filter, **Save this search** gives it a name. You then get an email and a push notification when a new opportunity matches. Manage saved searches in [Alerts](help:explore-alerts).

## Organizations

The Organizations tab lists organizations with a public profile: what they do, whether they are verified and how many opportunities they have open.

## How the list is ordered

Explore orders opportunities by two public factors only: the application deadline, then the start date. No organization can pay for placement, and your filters narrow the list without reordering it. You see an opportunity only when the organization made it visible to you. Read more in [How ranking works](/explore/how-ranking-works).

On an organization's own Gateway address, Explore shows that organization's opportunities only.
