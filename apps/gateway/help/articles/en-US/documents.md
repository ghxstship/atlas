Documents is your vault of every document organizations shared with you: offers, agreements, onboarding forms and compliance documents.

## Expiring soon

A note at the top counts documents that expire within 30 days. Renew them before then to stay eligible for work.

## Your documents

Each shows its title, kind, organization, expiry date and state. A document waiting for your signature has **Sign**. Open one to go to its engagement's Documents tab.

## Signed by link

Agreements you signed from a link without signing in, with when and by whom they were signed.

## Tax forms

The tax forms you submitted, which open [Tax documents](help:money-tax-documents).

Show documents as a table or a list. Documents to sign also appear on Home under Needs you.
