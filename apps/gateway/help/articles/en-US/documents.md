Documents is your vault of every document organizations shared with you: offers, agreements, onboarding forms and compliance documents.

## Expiring soon

A note at the top counts documents that expire within 30 days. Renew them before then to stay eligible for work.

## Your documents

Each shows its title, kind, organization, expiry date and state. A document waiting for your signature has **Sign**. A generated agreement or a quote is read first: open it to read and sign on its engagement's Documents tab. Open one to go to its engagement's Documents tab.

## Signed by link

Agreements you signed from a link without signing in, with when and by whom they were signed.

## Tax forms

The tax forms you submitted, which open [Tax documents](help:money-tax-documents).

Show documents as a table or a list. Documents to sign also appear on Home under Needs you.

## Reading and signing an agreement

Some agreements are written for you from the organization's own wording. Open it on the engagement's Documents tab, read the full text and type your full name to sign. Your name signs exactly that text, and the document records its fingerprint. If you do not agree, choose Decline and say why. The organization then countersigns, and the PDF is yours to download.
