Organizations reach you by email and push for offers, documents to sign, payments and messages. Quiet hours and language follow your preferences.

Choose **Open preferences** to set them in [Preferences](help:settings-preferences). Choose email or push for each kind of notification in [Notifications](help:settings-notifications).
