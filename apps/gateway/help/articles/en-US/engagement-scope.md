Scope is for contractors. It holds what you agreed to deliver.

## Statement of work

The statement of work and agreements for the engagement, each with its state, such as Sent, Signed or Executed. Open one to sign or read it in Documents. If none is shared yet, ask the organization in Messages.

## Milestones

The milestones, deadlines and tasks the organization shared, each with its date and state. They appear once the engagement is Active.

Your [deliverables](help:engagement-deliverables) come from this scope.
