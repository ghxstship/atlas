The team lists each member of the company account with their roles and since when they are a member.

## Account roles

- **Account Owner** and **Account Admin**: everything for the account's engagements.
- **Account Finance**: Money, documents and the account's payout and tax.
- **Account Member**: the engagements assigned to them.

## Add or remove members

An Account Owner or Admin invites someone by email with a role; only an Owner offers Account Owner. We send one email to the address, and the person signs in to Gateway with it and accepts on Home. Pending invitations are listed here until they are accepted, and Owners and Admins can revoke them.

A member marked **Not confirmed** was added without an invitation and has not confirmed yet. Until they confirm on Home, they cannot be put on a crew roster or assigned to an engagement, and nothing about them is shared.

An Account Member reaches only the engagements an Owner or Admin assigns them, on the engagement's Overview. Owners and Admins can remove members here.
