## Registering

An organization shares a registration page to find crew and volunteers. Sign up or sign in, then answer its questions. Your work phone comes first and saves to your settings. You confirm the page's minimum age, and you can share chosen sections of your profile with that organization only. Your account stays yours.

## What happens next

The organization approves or declines, and you hear either way. Once approved you are on its crew list and can see the work it offers that list in Explore. You can withdraw from the page at any time.
