Choose how organizations reach you for each kind of notification. For each one, tick **Email**, **Push to Compass** or both, then **Save preferences**. New notifications follow your choices.

## Invoices and payments

Each step of your invoices: received, approved, payment scheduled and paid. These updates always show on Home, whatever you choose here.

Email goes to the address you sign in with. Push reaches you in Compass on your phone.
