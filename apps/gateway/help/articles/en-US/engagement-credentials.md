Credentials lists what the organization issued you for the project, such as an access badge: the category, the credential number, the holder and the dates it is valid. Each is **Valid** or **Revoked**.

Download a badge as a PDF on US Letter or A4. In Compass, Scan shows your badge to scan at access points.

Credentials are issued once onboarding passes.
