Settings opens on Profile, where you edit what organizations see when you apply or share your profile. Your public profile is at the address shown at the top.

## Profile facts

- **Handle**: your profile address. Old handles keep working for 90 days after a change.
- **Preferred name**, **pronouns**, **headline**, **city** and **country code**.
- **Your rate**: leave it blank to show no rate. A blank rate is never shown as zero.

## About you

A short bio.

## Skills, certifications, languages and links

Add each one with its own form and remove any you no longer want:

- **Skills**: choose from the list.
- **Certifications**: the certification, who issued it, the issue date and any expiry. Certifications an organization verified are marked Verified.
- **Languages**: a language code such as en, es or pt-BR, and your proficiency.
- **Links**: a label and a full web address starting with https://.

Who sees each part is set in [Profile privacy](help:settings-profile-privacy).
