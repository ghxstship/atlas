Save an opportunity from its page with **Save**, and it is listed here until you remove it.

## What you see

Each saved opportunity shows its card: the organization, pay, dates and deadline. Open it to read the details and apply. An opportunity that no longer takes applications is marked so, and stays until you remove it.

## Remove one

Choose **Remove from saved** under the card, or on the opportunity's own page.

Show the list as cards or as a compact list with the view switcher. Saving an opportunity does not apply for it and the organization does not see it. To be told about new opportunities instead, save a search as an [alert](help:explore-alerts).
