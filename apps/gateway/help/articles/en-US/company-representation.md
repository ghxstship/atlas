Representation lists the artists your agency represents. Each shows the scopes the artist granted (Offers, Advance, Settlement or Documents), when it ends, and its standing: Waiting for the artist, Active, Expired or Revoked.

**Revoke** ends an active or waiting representation.

## Represent an artist

Ask the artist to share their Gateway profile with your agency. You then propose the representation, and the artist accepts it in Gateway.

Work on the artists' engagements from the [roster](help:engagement-roster).
