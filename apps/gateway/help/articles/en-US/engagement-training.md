Training is for staff. It lists the training and acknowledgment steps of your onboarding, each Verified or To do. A step to do opens Onboarding, where you complete it.

## Training sessions

Once the engagement is Active, the training sessions the organization shares appear with their time and state.

Your [positions](help:engagement-positions) follow once training is done.
