Invoices lists every invoice across your engagements, newest first.

## Table or list

- **Table**: number, organization, project, issue date, due date, total, balance and state.
- **List**: each invoice with its payment tracker and its lines.

## The payment tracker

An invoice moves through four steps, each with its date: **Submitted**, **Approved**, **Scheduled** and **Paid**. Before it is scheduled, the tracker shows when it is due. The balance is what is still owed.

## Send an invoice

Submit invoices from an engagement's [Invoices tab](help:engagement-invoices). With self-billing, the organization raises them for you from approved timesheets.

Each step also shows under For your information on Home, and reaches you by email or push as you choose in [Settings, Notifications](help:settings-notifications).
