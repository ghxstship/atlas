Crew is the roster of people who work an engagement for your company: a vendor's technicians, a band and its touring crew, or a sponsor's activation staff. It shows to the people who manage the roster: the artist on their own engagement, the account's Owner and Admin, an Advancing Lead assigned to the engagement, and an artist's representative with the Advance scope.

Add someone from your team, or invite an email address for someone who is not on XOS yet; they get one email asking them to sign in to Gateway with that address. Give each person a role and, when you know it, their arrival window in the project's time zone. Submit the roster when it is ready; the organization accepts or returns each person, and a returned entry shows the reason so you can fix it and submit again.

Each person accepts or declines their own entry on Gateway Home. Nothing about them is shared until they accept: then your company can see their profile and project facts, and they choose what to share with the organization. View profile opens what they share with your company; each time you open it, they can see that you did. Allergies show only as On file.

The roster opens while the engagement is onboarding and closes when it completes or is canceled. To swap someone after they were accepted, withdraw their entry and add the replacement.

The organization can set a names lock: a date and time after which your company names no new crew for the engagement. Crew shows when names lock, and after that Add, Invite and Submit of new names are closed. A returned entry stays editable and can still be submitted again, and you can still withdraw entries. To add someone after the lock, ask the organization to move or clear it.

Each row shows whether the person's travel, uniform sizes and vehicle are on file for the project. Project details opens them; like View profile, each time you open it the person can see that you did. When the organization allows it, you can enter a kind the person has not entered yet; they are told and can correct it. What the person entered themselves only they change.
