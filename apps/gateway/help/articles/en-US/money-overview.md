Money opens on the overview of your pay across every organization.

## Earned and pending

- **Earned** is what organizations paid you.
- **Pending** is what open invoices still owe you.

Totals stay in each currency, so pay in two currencies shows two amounts. An amount that is not known yet is never shown as zero.

## Next payment

The open invoice due soonest, followed like a package: Submitted, Approved, Scheduled and Paid, each with its date.

## Paid by month

A chart of what you were paid in each of the last six months, in your main currency.

In a company account, Account Owners, Admins and Finance see Money; Account Members do not.
