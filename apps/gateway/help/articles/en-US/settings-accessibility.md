---

- **Text size**: default, large or larger.
- **High contrast**: stronger borders and text.
- **Motion**: follow your device, or always reduce animation.
- **Haptics**: vibration feedback in Compass.
- **Sounds**.
- **Captions** on video and voice notes.

Each setting shows where its value comes from, such as your own setting or the platform default. Save keeps your own value, and Reset to the platform default removes it. These are the same personal settings Atlas and Compass use.

Gateway works with the keyboard alone, and every icon button names its shortcut in a tooltip.
