## Payout destinations

Where organizations pay the company, by provider, with only the last four digits shown. Payout details are entered through each organization's payment provider.

## Tax forms

The tax forms the company gave organizations, with the form, the organization, the last four characters of the identifier and when it was signed.

Account Owners, Admins and Finance see this page. Your own payout and tax details are in [Payout and tax](help:settings-payout-and-tax).
