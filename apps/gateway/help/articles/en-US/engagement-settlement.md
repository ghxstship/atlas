Settlement is for artists. After the show the organization sends a settlement: its state, project, date and when it was sent to you.

## The lines

Each line has its kind: Guarantee, Bonus, Split, Expense, Deduction or Withholding. Deductions and withholdings show as Less. A line without an amount reads Amount to be confirmed, and the net to you shows once every line has one.

## Answer

While a settlement is Waiting on you, choose **Accept settlement** or **Dispute settlement**. Disputing, say what should change. The organization sees your answer at once.

Settlement documents are listed under the settlement. An artist's representative with the Settlement scope answers for the artist.
