Every application and bid you sent is here, with its state: Submitted, In Review, Shortlisted, Offered, Accepted, Declined, Withdrawn or Not Selected.

## What each card shows

The organization, when you applied, the fee you proposed or your sealed bid with its number of lines and total, and any interview with its time and place. **View opportunity** opens the listing.

## Answer an offer

When the organization makes an offer, its card shows the version, amount, rate type, dates, the date to answer by and who proposed it.

- **Accept offer** to take it. The engagement then starts with onboarding.
- **Decline** to turn it down.
- **Counter** to propose another amount or dates with a message. Your counter goes to the organization, and you get a notification when they answer.

## Withdraw

**Withdraw** takes back an application that is still open. Once an offer is made, answer the offer instead.

Show applications as a list, a table or a board by state.
