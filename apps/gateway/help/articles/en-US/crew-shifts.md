## Opening your shifts

Your crew lead can send you a link and a 6-digit code. Open the link, type the code, and you see your own shifts on that project for the next 14 days, with the times in each shift's own time zone, your job title and the venue. Your device remembers the code on this link for up to 12 hours. Choose Forget this code on a shared phone.

## Accepting a shift

Choose Accept shift on a Published shift. Your crew lead sees it at once. Declining and punching in stay in your account and in Compass, so choose Sign in to see all your work for those.

## When a link stops working

A link stops working when it expires, when your crew lead revokes it or issues a new one, when your work on the project ends, or after five wrong codes. Ask your crew lead for a new one.
