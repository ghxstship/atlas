Purchase orders arrive here when the organization sends them, usually after your bid wins. Each shows its number, total, when it was sent and acknowledged, and its state: Proposed, Approved, Sent, Closed or Canceled. Its lines show each item, quantity and amount.

## Acknowledge

A purchase order that is Sent and not yet acknowledged has **Acknowledge**. Acknowledging tells the organization you received it and will deliver.

Download each purchase order as a PDF on US Letter or A4. Book deliveries against it in [Deliveries](help:engagement-deliveries), and invoice it in [Invoices](help:engagement-invoices).
