Organizations ask for a tax form during onboarding. Each form you submitted is listed here.

## Your tax forms

Each shows the form, the organization, the last four characters of your taxpayer identifier, when you signed it and whether you are a foreign performer subject to withholding. Your full identifier is stored encrypted and never shown.

## Year end

US payees get 1099-NEC data from each organization at year end. Each organization that pays you $600 or more in a year files a 1099-NEC with the name and address on your W-9, so keep the W-9 name and mailing address current. Add or change the address under each W-9.

## Payout destinations

Where organizations pay you, by provider, with the last four digits. Add one under [Settings, Payout and tax](help:settings-payout-and-tax).
