Put your work in your own calendar from [Upcoming](help:work-upcoming): **Add all to calendar** downloads every shift, set time and deadline as one calendar file that any calendar app opens.

To add one item, use **Add to calendar** on the Up next card on Home.

Choose **Open Upcoming** to go there.
