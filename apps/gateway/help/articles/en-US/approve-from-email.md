Some organizations ask the people they engage to approve a pass, a dock booking or an equipment request before it takes effect. When yours lets you decide from email, the request email carries an **Approve** button and a **Reject** button.

You still need an XOS account, any Gateway account, so the email can reach you. You do not have to sign in to decide.

## Decide

1. Press **Approve** or **Reject** in the email. The page that opens says which organization asks and where the code goes. Nothing happens yet.
2. Press **Email me a code**. A 6-digit code arrives by email; it works for 10 minutes. You can ask for a new one after a minute, up to five an hour.
3. Enter the code and press **Show the request**. The request and the step it waits on appear.
4. Press **Approve**, or write why and press **Reject**. The page confirms it, and the organization is told.

The page decides only what its button says: to reject an Approve email, open its **Reject** button instead.

## When it does not work

- **Wrong code**: the page says how many tries are left. After five wrong codes across both buttons, the link locks; sign in to decide.
- **Already handled**: someone decided the step, or the organization withdrew the request.
- **Expired**: the link lasts 7 days. Reminders from the organization carry a fresh link.
- **Approval emails off**: no code can be sent while approval emails are off in your notification settings. Sign in to decide instead.

If you are a member of the organization, its links always ask you to sign in, so its security settings apply.
