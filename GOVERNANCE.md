# Governance

## Roles

- **Owner:** Julian Clarkson, GHXSTSHIP Industries LLC. Holds final say on canon ratification, licensing, trademarks and releases.
- **Maintainers:** review and merge pull requests within the paths they own (ADR 0004), and keep CI green on `main`.
- **Contributors:** anyone who opens an issue or pull request under `CONTRIBUTING.md`.

## Decisions

- Reversible technical decisions are recorded as Architecture Decision Records in `docs/adr/` and proceed without waiting.
- Canon changes are data changes. 4.0 grammar stays proposed until a named ratifier is recorded, and supersession is stored as data.
- License changes, trademark policy changes and package publication under a new organization name require the Owner's written approval.

## Conduct reports

Conduct reports go privately to the Owner by direct message to the repository owner account on GitHub. Reports are reviewed promptly and handled under `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`.
