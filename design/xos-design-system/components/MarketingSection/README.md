# MarketingSection

MarketingSection is one band of a public page: an eyebrow, a display-40 heading, a lead and the section content.

Sections alternate on `bg-canvas` and, with `tone="surface"`, on `bg-surface`, separated by a hairline. Vertical rhythm is space-64, and space-96 from bp-lg. The heading is labeled by `id`, so in-page links and the navigation land on it.

Write `lead` as a direct answer of 40 to 60 words that names the product and states one verifiable fact; search and answer engines quote this passage. The consumer provides `id`, `title`, `lead` and the content.
