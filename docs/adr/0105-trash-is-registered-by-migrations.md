# ADR 0105: Trash Is Registered by Migrations

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-28
- Decider: Orchestrator post-merge fix (RRR-193), under ADR 0022's post-merge fix series
- Migrations: `2390_trash_registered_by_migrations.sql`

## Context

Trash (Section 4.10: "Delete moves it to Trash, which can be restored for the retention period") is the view
`app.trash_items`, regenerated by `private.rebuild_trash_items_view()` from the list in `private.trash_tables`
(`1031_trash.sql`). `private.sync_trash_tables()` adds to that list every app table with a uuid `id`, `org_id`,
`updated_by`, a Soft Deleted retention policy that purges by Delete, a column that names a row, and no row in
`private.trash_excluded_tables`.

Migrations called the sync only in 1031, 1032 and 1033. After that it ran only inside
`private.sweep_default_retention()`, which pg_cron runs at 03:07 UTC (`0930_retention_sweep.sql`). Thirteen tables
made eligible by later migrations were therefore in Trash on production from the first night after 4.0, and never on
a fresh database:

- the catalog after the migrations was not the catalog after a night, so a pgTAP run gave different answers before
  and after 03:07 UTC;
- `app.osha_cases` put its description, Confidential since `1385_osha_recordkeeping.sql`, in `app.trash_items` as
  `title` with no row in `private.view_column_sources`, so an export of Trash classified it Internal (Section 8.6).
  pgTAP 2030's check that a view showing a classified column under another name names it failed whenever the sweep
  ran during the suite;
- some of the thirteen were never meant to be restored by anyone: revoked tokens, derived translations, access
  controls and OSHA recordkeeping.

## Decisions

### 1. Each of the thirteen tables is decided

A table belongs in Trash when deleting one of its rows is a mistake a person can undo, and the table's own update
policy can restore it (`app.restore_trash_item` restores as the caller). Otherwise it is listed in
`private.trash_excluded_tables` with why. Excluded tables keep their retention policy, so their soft-deleted rows are
purged exactly as before.

| Table                       | Decision | Why                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| --------------------------- | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `shipments`                 | Trash    | A project's logistics record (0351), titled by the description 1180 added; kept 7 years.                                                                                                                                                                     |
| `price_books`               | Trash    | An org's price book, deleted through the v1 contract's delete (1170); kept 7 years.                                                                                                                                                                          |
| `emergency_codes`           | Trash    | The org's editable copy of the Standard Library (1612). Adopting the library again never brings back a removed copy, so Trash is the only way to undo a removal.                                                                                             |
| `enumerations`              | Trash    | The same, for the org's copies of the standard enumerations (1612).                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| `earned_media_items`        | Trash    | A project's coverage figures (1551); kept 7 years.                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| `osha_cases`                | Excluded | OSHA recordkeeping with health information (1385): every column is Confidential, only `data.restricted.read.medical` reads it, and 29 CFR 1904.33 sets its retention. Like `medical_encounters`, a case is corrected by the recordkeeper in the audit trail. |
| `osha_establishments`       | Excluded | The rest of the OSHA record, for the same reason; restoring an establishment would also restore the cases deleted with it.                                                                                                                                   |
| `osha_annual_summaries`     | Excluded | A certified Form 300A. It has no title column today and is listed so that adding one never puts it in Trash.                                                                                                                                                 |
| `access_review_campaigns`   | Excluded | Deleting a campaign cancels it (1202), and its guard refuses any change to a deleted campaign, so a restore could never succeed. A new campaign is opened instead.                                                                                           |
| `warehouse_destinations`    | Excluded | Deleting one destroys its bucket credential in Vault (1401, `t91_destroy_warehouse_credential`). It is added again with a new credential, as integration connections are.                                                                                    |
| `automations`               | Excluded | Written only through `app.save_automation` and `app.delete_automation` (1480), which keep the owner and plan rules; no one may update the table directly.                                                                                                    |
| `org_ip_allowlist`          | Excluded | A removed network is revoked access, changed through the security settings (1201).                                                                                                                                                                           |
| `chat_message_translations` | Excluded | Derived, cached text (1363, 1392); an edit or delete of the message retires it and the next reader translates again.                                                                                                                                         |
| `personal_access_tokens`    | Excluded | A revoked token never comes back (1432), as `api_keys`.                                                                                                                                                                                                      |

### 2. A Trash title is never shown unclassified

`private.trash_title_column` never offers a Restricted column: Section 8.3 masks Restricted values in views, and a
table whose only naming columns are Restricted stays out of Trash. `private.rebuild_trash_items_view` names each
Confidential or Restricted title column in `private.view_column_sources` as a source of `app.trash_items.title`, so
`private.export_column_classification` (2030) classifies the title as its highest source. It also keeps SELECT on the
view away from `xos_backup` (1650).

### 3. The registry is final after the migrations

`private.trash_table_candidates()` names the eligible tables, and `private.sync_trash_tables()` makes
`private.trash_tables` exactly those: new tables are added, a changed title column is updated, and a table that is no
longer eligible leaves. 2390 runs the sync, so the state after the migrations is the state after the sweep, and on
production the eight excluded tables the sweep had registered leave the registry and the view.

### 4. The gate fails at once, not at 03:07 UTC

`supabase/tests/2390_trash_registered_by_migrations.test.sql` asserts that after all migrations the sync adds,
changes and removes nothing, that the sweep adds no retention policy, that the registry is exactly the eligible
tables, and that 2030's registry check holds after a sync. pgTAP 2030 runs the sweep before that check, so it tests
the state production reaches every night.

## Rule for migrations that add or change soft-deletable tables

A migration that adds a soft-deletable app table, or makes an existing one eligible for Trash (adds a title column
such as `name` or `description`, `updated_by`, `deleted_at`, or a Soft Deleted retention policy that purges by
Delete), decides in the same migration whether its rows belong in Trash. If they do not, it inserts the table into
`private.trash_excluded_tables` with a rationale of at most 300 characters. Either way it ends with
`select private.sync_trash_tables();`, after its retention policy and its column classifications. A table in Trash
must let the people who delete its rows restore them through its own update policy and grants. A Confidential title
column is named in `private.view_column_sources` by the sync; a Restricted column is never a title. A migration that
drops a table in Trash removes its retention policy or excludes it and runs the sync first, because `app.trash_items`
depends on it. pgTAP 2390 fails otherwise.

## Alternatives

- Run the sweep at the end of every migration batch. Rejected: Supabase runs the migration files and nothing after
  them, and a hidden step would still leave each decision unrecorded.
- Mask a Confidential title in the view. Rejected: Section 8.3 masks Restricted values only, and the readers who pass
  the table's row level security may read the description; the export classification is what was missing.
- Keep OSHA cases in Trash, titled by their case number. Rejected: the record is health information kept by law, and
  the recordkeeper corrects it in the audit trail, as `medical_encounters` (1031).

## Consequences

- Trash on production loses the eight excluded tables and keeps the five it gained; nothing is purged sooner or later
  than before.
- An export of Trash is classified Confidential whenever a table in it is titled by a Confidential column.
- Nine tables already in Trash cannot be restored by anyone through `app.restore_trash_item`, because members hold no
  update policy or grant on them (`support_tickets`, `rating_replies`, `party_threads`, `chat_messages`, `feed_posts`,
  `poll_options`, `surveys`, `occupancy_counters`, `shared_reports`). They are left as they are and recorded for a
  later decision.
