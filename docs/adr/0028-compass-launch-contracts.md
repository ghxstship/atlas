# ADR 0028: Compass Launch Contracts

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-17
- Decider: Orchestrator, with owner approval of the recommendations (2026-10-17)

## Context

Device QA on the iOS simulator (A39) left five launch gaps (SIM-003 to SIM-007). The owner approved closing all of them for 4.0, with the native Live Activity as a launch feature. A42 built them.

## Decision

1. **Account deletion (journey 38; App Store guideline 5.1.1(v)).** `app.delete_my_account(email)` deletes the sign-in immediately, in one transaction.
   - **Erased:** profile tables, the person's name (the person row stays, soft-deleted and nameless), devices, preferences and profile media.
   - **Ended:** memberships. Onboarding, Blocked and Active engagements are Canceled; Closing and Complete engagements stay for settlement.
   - **Recorded:** a Completed Erasure DSAR in every related org.
   - **Kept and listed in the result:** time and pay, tax forms, engagements (7 years), the org's records without the name, and the audit trail.
   - **Refused with XR001, nothing deleted:**
     - a legal hold on any table the deletion writes;
     - an open time entry;
     - being the sole Owner or Account Owner.
   - **Mode:** idempotent and online only. `@xos/data/account-deletion` is the one client, for Compass now and Gateway settings next.
2. **Clock Live Activity (journey 52).** An ActivityKit widget extension generated by `@bacons/apple-targets` from `targets/ClockActivity`.
   - **Intents:** `ClockPunchIntent` is a `LiveActivityIntent` that runs in the app process. It appends the punch to the App Group queue, and the app drains it into the outbox as `app.record_punch`, keyed by the intent's UUIDv7. Lock-screen punches are therefore durable when Compass is not running.
   - **Lifecycle:** the local `compass-live-activity` module starts the activity at clock in, updates it at a break and ends it at clock out.
   - **Out of scope:** no APNs push. The in-app card stays as the fallback. Clocking in from outside the app needs the Section 11.13 App Shortcut, which is separate work.
3. **Incident dispatch by project role.** The Field system role no longer holds incident dispatch. The manager band dispatches org-wide. Project roles can carry project-scoped capabilities through `private.project_role_capabilities`. Field Supervisor dispatches on its own project.
4. **Field asset reach.** Field members read the asset units in their custody or out for their project (`private.asset_unit_assigned_to_me`), so their scans resolve.
5. **Voice notes (journey 54).**
   - **Recording and storage:** recorded with `expo-audio` and stored in `field-media` like photos, as media kind Voice Note, up to 10 minutes.
   - **Transcription:** deferred past 4.0, because it needs a new subprocessor.

## Consequences

Gateway's settings deletion control and the Section 11.13 App Shortcut remain to build.
